The situation

Travis Krieg runs a 5-person painting crew in South Australia. Like most tradies, he was sending quotes and hoping clients would reply. Some did. Many didn't. The ones that went quiet just... stayed quiet.

Travis had quotes sitting in inboxes for months. Some were worth $20,000 or more. He'd done the hard work — the site visit, the estimate, the quote itself. But following up? That fell through the cracks. Between managing the crew, being on the tools, and running the business, there was never time to chase.

That changed when Travis started using Not Forgotten Systems. He CC'd notforgotten.com.au on his quotes, and the system handled everything after that. Three follow-ups, spaced over two weeks, automatically. No manual chasing. No forgotten threads.

The results

Over three months, Travis won 14 jobs through automated follow-up. These weren't new leads — they were quotes he'd already sent that would have died in silence without the system.
