Why Good Businesses Lose Work

They didn't say no. Life got in the way.

Here is what actually happens to most quotes that go quiet.

🏢

The committee hasn't met yet.

Body corporates, councils, and boards meet quarterly. Your quote is sitting in an agenda somewhere. Nobody has rejected it. Nobody has approved it. Nobody has even looked at it since you sent it.

📧

The email got buried.

Your contact opened your quote on a Tuesday. Their phone rang. A delivery arrived. Three other quotes landed the same day. Then it was Friday. Then it was next week. Your email is now on page four of their inbox.

📋

Insurance. Always insurance.

The homeowner wants the work done. They submitted your quote to their insurer. The insurer hasn't responded. Nobody knows how long it will take. The homeowner is waiting. You're waiting. Nobody is communicating.

None of these are a no. They're just life. And life doesn't close deals unless someone keeps the conversation alive.
