The Real Problem

They didn't say no. Life got in the way.

Most quotes don't get rejected. They get buried. Here is what actually happens.

📋

Committees take months.

Body corporates, councils, and boards meet quarterly. Your quote is in an agenda somewhere. Nobody has said no. Nobody has even looked at it since you sent it.

📧

Emails get buried.

Your contact opened your quote on a Tuesday. The phone rang. Another email landed. Then it was Friday. Your email is now on page four of their inbox.

⏳

Insurance drags everything out.

The customer wants the work done. They submitted your quote to their insurer. The insurer hasn't responded. Everyone is waiting. Nobody is communicating.

None of these are a no. They are just life. And life doesn't close deals unless someone keeps the conversation alive.

Why Most People Don't Follow Up

Professional follow-up is customer care. It is making sure people feel looked after, not chased. The difference is everything.
