Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026  |  Version 1.0

In plain English: We use a small number of cookies and tracking tools to understand how people use our website and whether our follow-up emails are being opened. We don't use tracking for advertising or to build profiles of individual visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser; the website will still work.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They help the website remember things about your visit — like which pages you looked at — and make the experience better over time.

Some cookies are essential for a website to work. Others are optional and help the website owner understand how the site is being used.

3. Cookies We Do Not Use

We do not use:

  • Advertising cookies or tracking pixels for ad targeting
  • Social media tracking cookies

  • Third-party cookies from ad networks

We don't show ads on our website, and we don't share visitor data with advertisers.

4. Email Tracking

Our follow-up emails include a tiny invisible image (a 1×1 pixel tracking pixel). When the recipient opens the email and their email program loads images, our server records:

  • That the email was opened
  • When it was opened
  • How many times it was opened

This is not a cookie — it does not place anything on the recipient's device. It is a standard technique used by most email platforms to measure engagement.

Why we do this: Our clients (trade businesses) want to know whether their follow-up messages are being seen. Open data helps us understand which messages are effective and which ones aren't landing.

Limitations: Email open tracking is not perfectly reliable. Some email systems (including Apple Mail's Mail Privacy Protection and some versions of Microsoft Outlook) pre-load or block images, which means opens may not be detected. We present open data as directional insight, not definitive measurement.

Opting out: If you receive a follow-up email from Not Forgotten on behalf of a trade business and prefer not to be tracked, you can disable automatic image loading in your email program. You can also reply "unsubscribe" to stop receiving emails entirely, or contact us at james@notforgotten.com.au.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:

  • See what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete some or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Set your browser to "Do Not Track"

Instructions for common browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

If you block or delete all cookies, our website will still work. You may lose some website preferences, but core functionality is unaffected.
