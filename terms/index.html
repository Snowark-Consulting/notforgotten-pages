2. What We Do

Not Forgotten is an AI-powered quote follow-up service. Our core service:

  • Receives quote information from you (typically by you CC'ing us or forwarding quote emails to your dedicated intake address)
  • Extracts customer names, contact details, quote values, and scope of work from those emails and any attached quote documents
  • Sends follow-up emails and, where configured, SMS messages to your customers who have not yet responded to a quote
  • Tracks engagement (email opens, link clicks, replies) and reports on quote pipeline activity
  • Provides monthly Quote Insights reports showing patterns in your quoting activity

We use artificial intelligence to draft follow-up communications, extract data from documents, and analyse patterns. Every message we send is reviewed before it goes out, and you can review or modify any draft before it is sent if you choose.

Important: We follow up on quotes you have already sent. We do not generate new quotes, negotiate prices, or close sales. The relationship with your customer remains yours. We are a follow-up assistant, not a sales team.
