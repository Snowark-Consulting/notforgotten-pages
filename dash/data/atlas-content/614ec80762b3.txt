# Client Probability Score — Scoring Model

**Created:** 2026-07-27  
**Replaces:** Previous "business quality" ranking model  
**Purpose:** Rank prospects by probability of becoming a paying Not Forgotten client

## The Shift

The old model ranked painters by business quality (size, professionalism, reputation). The new model answers a single question: **"How likely is this painter to become a paying Not Forgotten client?"**

## Seven Dimensions

| Dimension | Max | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner accessibility** | 15 | Can we reach the decision-maker directly? |
| **Team-size fit** | 15 | Is the business the right size for $199/mo? |
| **Quote-volume signals** | 20 | Evidence of high quoting activity and follow-up burden |
| **Residential focus** | 15 | Repaint/residential = recurring individual quotes |
| **Follow-up gap** | 15 | How likely they have a follow-up problem |
| **No sales infrastructure** | 10 | No dedicated sales team, no CRM, no filter |
| **Personalisation potential** | 10 | Can we write a compelling, specific Email 1? |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | |

## Scoring Rules

### Owner Accessibility (0-15)
- First name @ domain (e.g. tony@) = 15
- Personal email (gmail/yahoo/bigpond/outlook) = 12
- info@ on small site (<5 employees implied) = 10
- info@ on larger site (6+ employees) = 5
- quotes@ = 12 (high quoting maturity, less owner-direct)
- admin@/office@/sales@ = 3-5
- No email found = 0

### Team-Size Fit (0-15)
- 2-5 employees = 15 (sweet spot)
- 6-10 employees = 12
- 1 employee (solo) = 8
- 11-20 employees = 5
- Unknown: estimate from website signals
  - Personal email domain = 13
  - Family-run language = 13
  - Large professional site (200K+ chars) = 10

### Quote-Volume Signals (0-20)
- Free quotes explicitly offered = 15
- Quoting CTA ("Get a Quote") = 8
- Strata/body corporate specialist = +10
- Real estate/property management work = +5
- No quote signals on site = 5
- Max capped at 20

### Residential Focus (0-15)
- Explicit residential/repaint specialist = 15
- Residential + commercial mix = 10
- Commercial-heavy with some residential = 5
- Commercial/industrial only = 0

### Follow-Up Gap (0-15)
- Strata/body corporate specialist (weeks-long decisions) = 14
- Free quotes + multi-service (high volume) = 13
- Real estate work (recurring, time-sensitive) = 12
- Free quotes = 10
- General quoting language = 8
- No quoting signals = 5

### No Sales Infrastructure (0-10)
- No sales team mentioned + small team = 10
- Family-run = 10
- No sales team mentioned (any size) = 8
- Unknown = 7
- Franchise signal = -15 (disqualifying adjustment)
- Sales team/department mentioned = -10
- National/multi-location = -5

### Personalisation Potential (0-10)
- Owner name extractable + multiple signals = 10
- Strong ICP signals (strata, Dulux, heritage) = 8-9
- Some signals = 5-7
- Minimal data = 3

## Negative Adjustments

Applied within the "No Sales Infrastructure" and "Owner Accessibility" dimensions:
- Franchise group: -15
- "Sales team" on website: -10
- National/multi-location: -5
- Large commercial-only: -5

## Confidence Flags

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| *(none)* | High-confidence score. Website researched, signals verified. |
| ⚠️ Provisional | Dead or missing website. Score based on email signals only. Needs alternative research. |
| ❌ Negative | Disqualifying signal detected (franchise, unreachable, etc.) |
