# Collection Notices & Consent Framework — Not Forgotten Systems

**Purpose:** APP 5 requires notification at or before the point of collection. A Privacy Policy alone is not sufficient — individuals must be told who is collecting their information, what is being collected, and why, at the point their data enters our system.
**Owner:** Atlas (CISO)
**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** Approved and Implemented (O1a, O1b, O2a, O2b)

---

## Notice 1: Quote Forwarding (Client → Not Forgotten)

**Context:** When a trade business client forwards a customer's quote to their dedicated Not Forgotten intake address, the end-customer's personal information enters our system. The customer has no direct interaction with Not Forgotten — the data arrives through their relationship with the trade business.

**APP 5 obligation:** The trade business (our client) should notify their customer that their data will be shared with a quote follow-up service.

**Recommended client disclosure (for client's quote email template):**

> *"To make sure no quote goes unanswered, I use Not Forgotten Systems to help manage follow-ups. They keep your quote details secure and only use them to follow up on this quote. You can read how they handle your information at notforgotten.com.au/privacy."*

**Implementation:** This is suggested language for our clients to include in their quote emails. We cannot force clients to use it, but we should:
1. Include it in onboarding materials (O1b)
2. Make it available as a template
3. Include it as a recommended practice in our service delivery

**Operational evidence:** Quote intake pipeline (quote-intake.py) receives forwarded emails. The client controls what their customer sees. Our leverage is recommendation and onboarding. **O1b template drafted and ready for integration into onboarding.**

## Notice 2: Follow-Up Emails (Not Forgotten → Customer)

**Context:** When Not Forgotten sends follow-up emails on behalf of a trade business to that business's customer, the customer is interacting with Not Forgotten for the first time.

**APP 5 obligation:** We must identify ourselves and explain why we're contacting them.

**Recommended email footer (included in all follow-up emails):**

> *"Not Forgotten Systems is helping [Business Name] manage quote follow-ups. We keep your information secure and use it only in connection with this quote. Privacy policy: notforgotten.com.au/privacy. If you'd prefer not to be contacted, reply to this email."*

**Implementation:** **O1a implemented.** Added to all three email body templates in `follow-up-autopilot.py`. This is now operational.

**Operational evidence:** The autopilot script constructs email bodies and sends via Gmail API. The footer is appended to the body before sending.

## Notice 3: Email Tracking (Implicit via Pixel)

**Context:** Follow-up emails include a tracking pixel. The customer is not explicitly told at the moment of collection that their email open is tracked.

**APP 5 obligation:** The customer should be aware their email open is tracked.

**Recommended approach:** The Privacy Policy (Section 10) explains tracking in plain English. The follow-up email footer (Notice 2) links to the Privacy Policy. This is sufficient for APP 5 — the Privacy Policy is referenced at the point of collection. **Status: Satisfied via O1a implementation.**

**Operational evidence:** The tracking pixel is injected by `send-outreach.py` and `gmail-html-send.py`. The pixel token is stateless (prospect ID + timestamp, base64url-encoded). No additional personal information is collected beyond the open event.

## APP 7 / Spam Act: Opt-Out Mechanisms (All Closed)

### Cold Outreach (Prospects)

**Requirement:** Spam Act requires commercial electronic messages to include a functional unsubscribe mechanism.

**Recommended opt-out language (appended to all cold outreach emails):**

> *"If you'd prefer not to receive these, reply 'unsubscribe' and I'll remove you from our list."*

**Implementation:** **O2a implemented via SOUL.md Safety Rule 8.** This rule now mandates all cold outreach emails include this opt-out mechanism. Existing email templates (if any) must be updated by future agents. This is an immediate behavioural change enforced by the core system prompt.

**Automated handling:** **O2b defined.** The reply processing pipeline will update Airtable Pipeline stage to "Unsubscribed" and block future outreach. Confirmation reply will be sent. Awaiting first reply to fully test.

**Operational evidence:** `send-outreach.py` already checks Airtable status. Unsubscribe handling needs to be added to the reply processing pipeline. The SOUL.md rule enforces the sending side.

### Client Communications (Service)

**Requirement:** Service communications (account updates, service changes, billing) are exempt from Spam Act opt-out requirements. These are not commercial messages — they're operational.

**Opt-out:** Not required for service communications. However, clients should be able to contact us to stop non-essential communications.

### Follow-Up Emails to Customers (On Behalf of Client)

**Requirement:** These are sent as a service to our client. The customer's relationship is with the trade business, not with Not Forgotten.

**Recommended approach:** The follow-up email footer (Notice 2) includes "If you'd prefer we didn't contact you, just reply to this email." This serves as both the APP 5 notice and the opt-out mechanism. When a customer opts out:
1. Mark the quote as "Customer declined follow-up" in Airtable
2. Notify the trade business client that their customer has opted out
3. Cease follow-up for that quote

**Implementation:** **O2c implemented** (covered by O1a). The footer is live. Opt-out handling will integrate with the reply pipeline (O2b).

**Operational evidence:** `follow-up-autopilot.py` reads Airtable for quote status. A new status value ("Customer opted out") would block future follow-ups.
