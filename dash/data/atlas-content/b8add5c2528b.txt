## Acknowledgement

By signing below, I confirm the following:

### Email Consent

1. I have obtained all necessary permissions and consents from my customers to contact them by email in connection with quotes I have sent.
2. I confirm that my customers have a reasonable expectation of receiving follow-up communication from me, because I have sent them a quote.
3. I understand that Not Forgotten will send follow-up emails to my customers on my behalf, using email content drafted by Not Forgotten's AI systems and reviewed before sending.
4. I understand that all follow-up emails will include an unsubscribe mechanism allowing customers to opt out of further communication at any time.

### SMS Consent

5. **[If SMS is enabled for my account]** I have obtained express consent from each customer to contact them by SMS in connection with quotes I have sent.
6. I understand that SMS consent must be explicit — a phone number provided on a quote form is not automatically consent to receive text messages.
7. I understand that every SMS message sent by Not Forgotten on my behalf will include information identifying my business as the sender and will provide a clear opt-out mechanism (e.g., "Reply STOP to unsubscribe").

### General

8. I am responsible for maintaining accurate customer contact information (email addresses and phone numbers) and will promptly correct any errors.
9. I understand that Not Forgotten will process customer data in accordance with its Privacy Policy, available at notforgotten.com.au/privacy.
10. I authorise NOT FORGOTTEN SYSTEMS (ABN 93 689 926 707) to communicate with my customers on my behalf for the purpose of quote follow-up, as described in the Terms of Service.
