# QPaint Reply Processing — Learnings (2026-08-14)

## Learning 1: "James" is ambiguous across QPaint

QPaint has its own employee named James. A customer's reference to "James" must
NOT be assumed to mean James Hardstaff.

- Vivien Lennan replied "responded to James yesterday and explained our situation."
- This was initially treated as "customer already engaged with James Hardstaff."
- Correct interpretation: she spoke to James at QPaint (a QPaint employee).

Rule: identity must be established from context/evidence before attributing a
customer interaction to a specific person. When a QPaint customer references a
name, do not assume it maps to Not Forgotten staff.

## Learning 2: Commercial outcome categorisation (QPaint follow-up)

QPaint follow-up produces pipeline truth, not just recovered revenue. Track these
five outcomes separately. Do not conflate them.

| Category | Definition | Example (14 Aug) |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue Recovery | Job genuinely recovered through our follow-up | (none today) |
| Revenue Confirmation | Job already won/accepted that follow-up discovers/confirms | (none today) |
| Pipeline Cleanup | Quotes confirmed Lost | Darryl Rogers, Rachel Stephenson, Nicholas Hanna, Ben Towler ($14,773, competitor selected) |
| Deferred Pipeline | Real opportunity with future timing | Lee Wright (~18 months), Alf Quattrocchi / Irene Prosser ($8,712, next year) |
| Engaged Opportunities | Customer actively considering/progressing | Cathy Cochrane (itemised breakdown) |

Rule: do not call a "confirmation" a "recovery" unless evidence shows our
follow-up caused or materially contributed to the win. Market evidence outranks
assumptions. This distinction matters for reporting and case studies.
