- **[Fact]** The only non-direct session this week — a `facebook.com` referral, the entire "Organic Social" channel — geolocates to Gretna US at 30.8s, matching the documented data-centre location. **So no non-direct session this week was human either.**
- **[Fact]** `nfs_internal` exclusion is still absent from the homepage as served (re-verified tonight: 0 occurrences), so our own visits count as direct traffic.
- **[Fact]** GA4 tag `G-03Y95NSDRV` is present and healthy (2 occurrences). A quiet week is not a tracking failure.
- **[Fact]** No `email`-sourced session has ever appeared in 16 weeks. `utm_source` appears nowhere in the live send path (re-verified tonight: only `scripts/disabled/send-outreach.py`).
- Insufficient data for causal analysis. On a ~22/week base, 50% swings are noise, and that remains true while attribution is off.

## Why It Matters

The website is not a lead source and should not be reported as one: zero enquiries, zero conversions, and it has never had an inner page viewed. Its job is to survive the trust check when a prospect looks us up before replying.

The number that matters is not 12. It is that essentially all traffic is unattributable direct, so we cannot say whether a single outreach email has ever produced a visit.

This week sharpens that. Even the sessions we *can* attribute are machines. Haarlem and Gretna are crawlers, not people. The only human-plausible segment is 2 Adelaide sessions. So the gap is not merely "we cannot see email traffic" — this week, the entire non-direct signal was machine noise. Until outbound links carry tags, "is the website working?" has no honest answer.

## One Recommended Action

**Approve the UTM tag on the two outbound link paths.** Fourth consecutive week surfaced; EXP-001 has now been unbuilt ~10 weeks. It is the cheapest measurement win available.

1. `scripts/canonical-send.py` — the body link template (no Gmail dependency, the half that has never actually stalled)
2. The Gmail send-as signature, edited in Gmail and then pushed with `bin/sync-james-signature.py` — **do not edit `james_signature.json` directly; the weekly sync silently reverts it**

Tag: `?utm_source=email&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=<sequence>`

**Test:** one tagged session with source `email` in GA4 within 7 days of the next send.

**Decision rule (carried from 27 Sep):** if tags are live for 30 days and zero tagged sessions appear, this report moves to quarterly. At that point it will have been tested properly and the answer is no.
