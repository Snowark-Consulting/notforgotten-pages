## 1. About Not Forgotten Systems

Not Forgotten provides a quote follow-up service for Australian trade and service businesses. When a plumber, electrician, builder, or other tradesperson sends a quote to a customer and doesn't hear back, we help make sure that opportunity is followed up professionally.

In providing this service, we handle personal information relating to four categories of people. This policy applies to all four.

### Who this policy covers

| Category | Who they are | How their information reaches us |
|---|---|---|
| **Website visitors** | Anyone browsing notforgotten.com.au | Through website analytics |
| **Prospective clients** | Trade business owners we contact about our service | Through our outreach and sales process |
| **Our business clients** | Trade businesses that use our quote follow-up service | Through onboarding and service delivery |
| **Our clients' customers** | People who receive quotes from our business clients | Through quotes forwarded to us by our clients |

We distinguish between these categories because our obligations differ depending on how we collect the information and our relationship with the individual.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Website Visitors

When you visit notforgotten.com.au, we collect anonymised information about your visit through Google Analytics:

- Pages you view
- How long you spend on each page
- Whether you're on a phone, tablet, or computer
- How you arrived at our site (e.g., from a search engine or a direct link)

This information does not identify you personally. We use it to understand how people use our website so we can improve it.

### 2.2 Prospective Clients

When we contact a trade business about our service, or when a business contacts us, we may collect:

- Business name
- Contact person's name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Industry and location

We collect this information from public sources (business websites, directories), from direct enquiries, or from conversations with the business.

### 2.3 Our Business Clients

When a trade business becomes a Not Forgotten client, we collect:

- Business name and contact details
- Information about the business's quoting process
- Communication records (emails, notes, service discussions)

This information is collected directly from the client during onboarding and through ongoing service delivery.

### 2.4 Our Clients' Customers

When a business client forwards a customer's quote to us, we extract:

- Customer name
- Email address
- Phone number (where included in the quote)
- Suburb or service area
- Description of the work quoted
- Quote value
- Any other information the client included in the quote

This information reaches us through the business client, not directly from their customer. The business client is responsible for letting their customer know they use a quote follow-up service. We provide suggested wording to help them do this.

Quote PDFs may contain additional personal information such as full street addresses. We extract only the information needed for follow-up and do not retain the full PDF longer than necessary for processing.

### 2.5 Information We Create

In the course of providing our service, we generate:

- Follow-up email drafts on behalf of our business clients
- Internal notes about communication preferences and context
- Reports and analysis about quote activity

## 4. How We Use Information

### 4.1 Core Service Delivery

We use personal information to provide our quote follow-up service:

- Processing quote emails forwarded by our business clients
- Extracting relevant information from quotes
- Drafting and sending follow-up communications on behalf of our business clients
- Tracking follow-up engagement and reporting results to our clients

### 4.2 Service Improvement

We analyse patterns in quote follow-up to improve our service. This analysis uses aggregated data where possible.

### 4.3 Communication

We communicate with our business clients about their service, and with prospective clients about our offering.

### 4.4 Website Analytics

We use anonymised website data to understand visitor behaviour and improve our website.

### What We Do Not Do

We do not:
- Sell personal information to anyone
- Use personal information for purposes unrelated to our service
- Use our clients' customer data to market to those customers directly
- Make automated decisions that have legal or significant effects on individuals
- Use personal information to train AI models that are shared between clients

## 5. AI Processing

We use artificial intelligence to help draft follow-up emails, extract information from quotes, and analyse communication patterns. When our AI processes data, the information in that specific task is transmitted to our AI provider for processing and returned to our system.

**Our primary AI provider is Google (Gemini), based in the United States.** Processing occurs under Google's data processing terms, which include commitments to protect personal information in accordance with data protection standards.

We may use additional AI providers for internal operations that do not involve personal information. Any change to our AI processing that affects how personal information is handled will be reflected in an update to this policy.

AI assists our operations, but human oversight governs how our service is delivered. Automated outputs are reviewed before they reach our clients or their customers.

## 9. How We Protect Information

We apply reasonable safeguards to protect the personal information we hold.

| Safeguard | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Encryption in transit | Data transmitted between our systems and third-party services is protected using HTTPS/TLS encryption |
| Access controls | Access to personal information is limited to authorised personnel and systems. Our automated systems operate under defined permission boundaries |
| Credential management | API keys and access tokens are stored in secured credential stores, not in code or logs |
| Provider security | We select providers who maintain security practices appropriate to the data they process |
| Regular review | We conduct monthly security assessments and address identified issues |

No online system can guarantee complete security. We take the protection of personal information seriously and continuously work to improve our safeguards. If you have specific security questions, contact us.

## 10. Email Tracking

Follow-up emails sent on behalf of our business clients may include a tracking mechanism that tells us whether the email was opened and when.

This tracking helps our business clients understand whether their follow-up messages are being seen. It is part of the service we provide.

**Limitations:** Email tracking is not perfectly reliable. Some email systems pre-load or block images, which means an "open" may not always correspond to a human reading the message. We present tracking data as a directional indicator, not a definitive measure.

If you prefer not to have email opens tracked, most email programs allow you to disable automatic image loading. Our emails remain readable with images disabled.

## 12. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Active client records | Duration of engagement plus 90 days |
| Quote data (resolved) | 24 months, after which identifying details are removed |
| Prospective client records | 12 months from last contact, after which identifying details are removed |
| Quote attachments (PDFs) | 30 days after processing |
| Email communications | Duration of engagement plus 90 days |
| System logs | 30 days, rolling |
| Website analytics | 14 months (Google Analytics default) |

When personal information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify it. Our retention processes are automated where possible and supplemented by manual review.
