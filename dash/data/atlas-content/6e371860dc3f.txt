## The Story

Travis Krieg Painting is a painting business in Adelaide, five crew, mostly residential. He started following up quotes that had gone quiet — the ones where the client said they'd think about it and never came back. Over four months, 14 of those jobs came back. Same quotes, same prices, just systematic follow-up instead of hoping.

## Role in Outreach

The Travis story is the conversion engine of the painter outreach sequence:

- **2-for-2 at Email 3.** Both [[Tas Moulis Conversion]] (knowledge/research/tas-moulis-conversion.md) and [[Star Painters Conversion]] (knowledge/research/star-painters-adelaide-conversion.md) converted after Email 3 carried the Travis story.
