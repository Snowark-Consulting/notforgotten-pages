stands as the default (business name); narrow the August rule on file to the correction case only; and
correct the memory line so it no longer reads as general. Either way one of the two must change.

**FACT.** No Email 1 without the complete, reviewed, approved sequence — every touchpoint drafted with an

**FACT — it holds for campaign outreach.** FF1, Wave, RE-ENG and V2 sequences were drafted and reviewed

**INFERENCE (Medium) — a scope question, not a breach.** QPaint customer care does **not** run
sequences. It is per-customer Customer Context Gate work: individual disposition per customer, "no

The decision's text, however, says *"every outreach campaign, every industry, every prospect. No

**Question for review.** State on the decision record whether white-label customer care is inside or
outside the scope of the sequence gate. The QPaint operating model post-dates this decision entirely, so
the boundary has never been written down — which is how a rule gets misread later as being broken.

### `2026-08-05-operational-handover-standard` — heavily used, with one founder gate worth removing

**FACT.** "Who owns the next action?" replaces "Should I reply?" as the first question for every customer
reply. Atlas never takes operational ownership (scheduling, quoting, pricing, site visits, commencement,
technical advice, the work). Acknowledgement and operational ownership are separate. Three Operating
Models: Invisible Infrastructure, Customer Care Representative, Hybrid.

**FACT — this is one of the most heavily exercised standards in the system.**
- `2026-08-20-qpaint-role-boundary` — SURFACE ≠ MANAGE; never chase a client's decision.
- `2026-08-30-qpaint-operational-handover` — Atlas owns routine ops end-to-end.
- `2026-09-24-qpaint-relay-authority` — **RELAY ≠ DECIDE**: once a decision owner has answered, Atlas
  relays, records, reconciles and monitors without a founder gate.
- Worked examples, current: the Steve Liversidge treads relay (QPaint set the $2,145 + GST figure; Atlas
  relays) — and this morning the armed relay correctly **skipped** on a fail-closed duplicate guard, which
  is the standard's fail-safe half working; the Alys warranty and inclusions relays; the 43118
  reclassification to HOLD as a client-owned relationship.

**FACT — one live tension.** Ten courtesy acknowledgements to Lost customers are still founder-gated "by
convention" (QPaint Daily Attention, today): replies as simple as "thanks for letting us know", two of
them carrying an added question. A brief acknowledgement is exactly what the standard tells Atlas to
consider, yet the class cannot be sent without a ruling and therefore queues every time.

**INFERENCE (High).** The standard is doing its job and has been progressively *sharpened* in Atlas's
favour (Relay Authority). The residual gate is convention, not the standard — and convention that
contradicts the standard is drift.

**Question for review.** Rule the courtesy-acknowledgement class once — batch-approve the eight

## Cross-cutting findings (the pattern behind the cluster)

**1. We record success criteria more readily than we instrument them.**
Four independent instances in one cluster: the ICP fields (92–94% empty 85 days after creation); the
Campaign Pulse standard (no retained artefacts to score); "one insight/day" in the 29 Sep philosophy
cluster (already flagged as uninstrumented); and decision review status (two reviews past date, register
says none overdue). A criterion with no instrument is a preference.

**2. Status advance is manual, so superseded decisions still read as live.**
`founding-atlas-as-primary-sender` (superseded by the james@ single-identity shift) and
`2026-07-04-messages-prospect-linked-field` (superseded by the V2 migration that deleted its table) are
both still 🟢 Made. A founding decision's own text says to create a referencing decision when thinking
changes — the referencing decisions exist; nothing sets the predecessor's status.

**3. The declared "primary channel" is the least available surface in the business.**
HQ has been unreachable (404, Pages disabled, owner scope) while every deploy verifies green by commit
SHA. 15 enabled jobs deliver to Telegram. This is a monitoring gap more than a hosting gap: the system
declared HQ primary and never built an alarm for its availability.

**4. One live rule contradiction directly affects customer-facing copy.**
Business-name fallback (founding decision) versus "Hi there" (August decision + memory). An operator
following memory would breach a founding decision.

## Recommendation — one systemic change

These four cross-cutting findings are one problem: **the decision register records what was decided, but
nothing forces a decision to stay instrumented, correctly-statused, or continuously available.** The
single highest-leverage action at this cluster review is a standing rule with two clauses:

> Every decision record must carry either a live instrument or an explicit "not instrumented" note.
> When a review date passes, the record must move to a pending-verdict state — so "Overdue Reviews: None"
> is a fact rather than a default.

That closes findings 1 and 2 permanently, makes finding 3 a normal monitoring item, and prevents the
same cluster recurring in November.
