**Confidence:** high
**Contested:** false

When an approved customer email's exact copy is unrecoverable, reconstruct from the FROZEN template + verified Airtable scope (never fabricate scope; never change a value/address), send via canonical-send, and transparently flag the reconstruction to James in the same report.
