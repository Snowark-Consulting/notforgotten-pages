# Decision: Inclusion Principle — Every Customer Deserves an Attempt

**Slug:** `2026-07-28-inclusion-principle`
**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** Made
**Review Date:** 2026-10-28
**Made By:** James Hardstaff

---

## The Decision

If a client provides us with a list of customers to follow up, our default position is: **every customer will be followed up.**

Scoring exists only to determine the order in which customers are contacted, never whether they are contacted.

The only valid reasons to exclude a customer are:
1. The client explicitly instructs us not to contact them.
2. The customer has requested no further contact or unsubscribed.
3. The opportunity has already been won or is confirmed closed.
4. The record is a verified duplicate.

Otherwise, every customer entrusted to us deserves an attempt.

## Evidence That Triggered This

QPaint pipeline import: 156 deals from Pipedrive. The standard Recovery Operations scoring model classified 3 deals as P3 (score <40) and excluded them from import. James identified this as a violation of the core promise: we don't forget anyone.

The 3 excluded deals:
- Callum Fox — $8,943, May 2026
- Ming Yuan — $4,840, Mar 2026
- Wayne Beckman — $1,100, Mar 2026

These were reinstated and imported as P3 (sequence last).

## Impact on Recovery Operations

The Priority Tier system is preserved — it governs sequencing, not inclusion:
- P1 (≥60): Contact first
- P2 (40-59): Contact after P1
- P3 (<40): Contact last

The Pipeline Import section of the Recovery Operations skill has been updated. P3 action changed from "Do not import" to "Import, sequence last."

The Inclusion Principle has been added to the Philosophy section as a permanent operating principle. It sits alongside the Core Principle and Execution Principle.

## Application to All Clients

This principle applies to every client Not Forgotten onboards, not just QPaint. When a client entrusts us with their pipeline, our commitment is: nobody gets forgotten. Not the big deals, not the small ones, not the old ones.

## Atlas's Challenge

None. This is the correct ethical and operational stance. The small, old deals might surprise us.

## Atlas's Acknowledgment

Internalised. Scoring = sequencing. Inclusion = universal. Every customer gets contacted.
