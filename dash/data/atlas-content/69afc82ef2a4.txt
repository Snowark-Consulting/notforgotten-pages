# Canonical-Send reply_to_message_id Must Reference Our Own Outbound — 2026-09-07

> Threading anchors are verified against our own prior message, never the customer's inbound reply.

Related: [[LEARNINGS]] (`learnings/LEARNINGS.md`), [[Learnings Catalogue]] (`learnings/catalogue.md`).

## 2026-09-07 — canonical-send `reply_to_message_id` must reference our own prior outbound, not the customer's inbound reply

**Confidence:** high
**Contested:** false
**Symptom:** Sending an in-thread reply via `canonical_send(...)` with `reply_to_message_id` set to the customer's inbound reply (the email they sent us) returned `status=BLOCKED` with `Threading: prior message recipient mismatch - BLOCK`. No email sent. Occurred on Scott O'Donnell #43149 same-day price-objection ack (2026-09-07).
**Cause:** `_resolve_threading()` verifies the thread anchor by checking the current recipient appears in the prior message's `To` header. The customer's inbound reply is `To: atlas@...` (not the customer), so the recipient check fails and it fail-closes correctly.
**Fix:** Pass `reply_to_message_id = <our prior sent message ID>` (the outbound Email 1 / last email we sent to that customer). For an in-thread reply use subject `Re: <original>`; Gmail threads by subject even without In-Reply-To. Omit `reply_to_message_id` entirely for a fresh email. Verified: re-send without the inbound anchor COMPLETE (Gmail 1a07a7d4323741f3, Sent-verified).
**Source:** 2026-09-07 Scott O'Donnell #43149 price-objection reply. Full detail in canonical-send card (`learnings/cards/canonical-send.md`).
