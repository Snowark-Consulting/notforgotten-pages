## 4. What a call must produce to count

The milestone is 10 genuine owner conversations. Voicemail, no answer and receptionist exchanges do not count. A connected owner conversation counts.

Six fields are the minimum for a call to be recordable (full 17-field standard in [[Founder Call Outcome Capture]]):

CONNECTED (y / voicemail / no answer / wrong number) · ROLE · VERBATIM (their exact words) · PAIN or NO PAIN · INTEREST (Hot / Warm / Cold / None, from evidence) · NEXT ACTION + OWNER.

Rough notes in any form are enough. Voice memo, Telegram message, photo of paper. **Atlas converts them.** James does not need to fill in a form.
