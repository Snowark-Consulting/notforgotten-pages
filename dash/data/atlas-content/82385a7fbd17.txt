### Key Finding (Already Evident)

**The Travis case study is the only touchpoint that has ever generated a reply or conversion.** Across approximately 175 emails sent over Email 1-2, zero prospects replied. At Email 3 (Travis), two replied and both converted. The sequence structure — curiosity → process → proof → breakup — produced conversions only at proof. Email 1-2 generated zero measurable engagement.

This finding will be tested against any late replies during the 30-day monitoring period, but the evidence is already strong enough to inform the next campaign's design.

### What the 30-day monitoring proved

1. **No late replies.** Zero July-cohort prospects replied after the monitoring start (Jul 29). The 2 meaningful replies (Tas, Star Painters) and 1 unsubscribe (Distinct Painters) were the complete engagement picture. The campaign's data was fully captured by Jul 29 — the 30-day window confirmed it, changed nothing.
2. **Email 4 produced nothing.** All ~60 breakup emails sent Jul 27-29 generated zero replies. The breakup template is confirmed non-generative (expected — it closes, it doesn't convert).
3. **The Travis trigger is the entire story.** 2 replies, 1 paying conversion, both at the single touchpoint carrying the [[Travis Case Study]] (`knowledge/case-studies/travis.md`). Every other touchpoint across 5 campaigns: zero meaningful engagement.

### Strategic answer to the four questions

1. **Why did businesses reply?** They saw a credible, specific number from a business like theirs ($180,115, 14 jobs, 4 months). Both replies came at that exact moment.
2. **Why didn't businesses reply?** Not knowable from July data alone (no open rates). V2 open tracking now fills this gap.
3. **Which signals predicted success?** Owner-accessible email patterns (firstname@ domain), prior interest signals. Both converters had them.
4. **Which assumptions were wrong?** "Curiosity-led questions lower reply friction" — disproved (0/125). "Email 1-2 warm prospects for Email 3" — challenged (no evidence of warming; conversions came at the proof touchpoint regardless of what preceded it).

### One action for James

**Approve the Travis-led Email 1 pilot.** It is the single highest-leverage next step: it tests the only proven conversion trigger at the earliest possible touchpoint, and the complete 4-email sequence is already drafted and waiting. All other recommendations are either done, active, or pending other reviews.
