## Context

Following Production Incident INC-2026-08-05-002 where 172 emails (156 QPaint + 16 FF1) were believed sent but never delivered, and the successful Stage 1-2 QPaint recovery and FF1 Wave 1 completion:

- The OAuth token for james@notforgotten.com.au has been re-authorised with `gmail.send` + `gmail.modify` scopes
- 16 Founder-to-Founder v1.0 emails were sent and verified from james@ (100% verification rate)
- Proven that james@ can be the production sender for NF outreach

## Decision

**All outbound outreach on behalf of Not Forgotten Systems must come from `james@notforgotten.com.au`.**

This includes:
- Founder-to-Founder outreach
- Cold outreach
- Follow-up with prospective Not Forgotten customers
- Discovery conversations
- Sales conversations
- Any communication where the relationship is between James and another business owner

**These communications must never come from an Atlas email address.**

Atlas continues to operate behind the scenes: preparing emails, managing sequences, monitoring replies, recording evidence, updating Airtable, updating Headquarters, drafting responses, and recommending next actions. But the relationship belongs to James.

## Exceptions

White-label customer communications remain unchanged. When representing a client (e.g. QPaint), communications continue from the established white-label identity (e.g. "Atlas — Customer Care, QPaint").

## Expected Outcomes

- Recipients always know they're speaking directly with James
- One relationship face for Not Forgotten from first contact through long-term client
- No identity confusion between NF outreach and white-label client operations

## Success Criteria

- 100% of NF outbound communications verified from james@notforgotten.com.au
- Zero NF communications from atlas@notforgotten.com.au
- No customer confusion about who they're speaking with

## James's Response

Approved. "From this point onwards, we optimise based on customer behaviour and evidence — not assumptions."
