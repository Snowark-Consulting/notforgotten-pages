## Incident

The CEO Brief generated at 2026-08-10 19:35 UTC (2026-08-11 05:05 ACST) displayed the business date as "Monday, 10 August 2026" when the correct Adelaide date was "Tuesday, 11 August 2026." The brief also presented 5 QPaint customer replies as requiring processing based solely on Gmail UNREAD state, when James had stated they were worked through yesterday.

## Root Cause

1. **Date error:** `date` command executed without `TZ=Australia/Adelaide`, producing UTC day (Monday) instead of Adelaide day (Tuesday).
2. **UNREAD≠UNPROCESSED gap:** No evidence hierarchy existed to distinguish Gmail label state from actual processing state. The brief assumed UNREAD = requires action, overriding James's statement.

## Enacted Corrections

1. **Business Date Standard:** All user-facing dates default to Australia/Adelaide. Always `TZ=Australia/Adelaide`.
2. **UNREAD ≠ UNPROCESSED:** Evidence hierarchy: Gmail + CRM + Outreach Notes + Execution History. CRM unavailable + James says handled = UNVERIFIED. Never override James on Gmail alone.
3. **CEO Brief Freshness Gate:** Every section certifies SOURCE/LAST VERIFIED/QUERY STATUS/FRESHNESS/CAVEAT. Source fail = UNKNOWN, no stale substitution.
