# Decision: QPaint Brand Spelling Canonicalisation

**Slug:** `2026-08-19-qpaint-brand-canonicalisation`
**Date:** 2026-08-19
**Status:** Made
**Decision by:** James Hardstaff

---

## Decision

**"QPaint"** (title case) is the canonical customer-facing brand spelling.

Use **QPaint**. Do not use **QPAINT** (all caps), except where it is legitimately part of
an existing proper reference — specifically the vanity phone number **"1300 QPAINT (772 468)"**.

---

## Primary evidence

| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| qpaint.com.au (QPaint's own site, body text) | "QPaint" (title case) — 52× |
| qpaint.com.au (title/meta tag) | "Qpaint" (lowercase p) — 18× (the wordmark style) |
| qpaint.com.au | "QPAINT" (all caps) — **0 occurrences** |
| QPaint PandaDoc notification emails (own signature) | "QPaint Team"; "1300 QPAINT (772 468)" (phone vanity only) |
| Airtable Clients table | "QPAINT" (all caps) — an admin data entry, not QPaint branding |

---

## What was updated (future customer-facing output)

- `knowledge/canonical-templates/qpaint/recovery-introduction-v1.md` — `{client_business}` example "QPAINT" → "QPaint"
- `knowledge/qpaint-white-label-send-protocol.md` — approved privacy footer "QPAINT" → "QPaint"
- `knowledge/decisions/2026-08-12-qpaint-privacy-governance.md` — approved footer "QPAINT" → "QPaint"
- `knowledge/customer-context-gate.md` — state label "QPAINT ACTION" → "QPaint action"
- Airtable Clients → Business name: "QPAINT" → "QPaint" (source of the `{client_business}` variable)

**Not changed:** historical sent emails (immutable), and unrelated internal docs/records that
are not customer-facing (e.g. "NF-QPAINT-42640" journey IDs, old dashboard specs).

---

## Permanent rule

> Customer-facing brand spelling is **"QPaint"**. The only legitimate use of "QPAINT"
> (all caps) is the vanity phone number "1300 QPAINT".
