# Quotes@ Inbox — Standard Operating Procedure

**Established:** 2026-07-23
**Status:** End-to-End Verified ✅ (tested 23 Jul 2026)
**Last test:** 23 Jul 2026 — 2 test quotes processed, all 7 steps passed

## When a Quote Lands in quotes@notforgotten.com.au

### 1. Extract
Extract all available data from the email and any PDF attachments:
- Customer name, email, mobile
- Quote value, scope summary, suburb
- Any other fields visible in the quote

### 2. Cross-Reference
Match the sender (the Not Forgotten client who CC'd quotes@) to their Airtable Client record by email address.

### 3. Create Quote Record
In Airtable Quotes table, create a record with:
- **Business Name** → linked to the Client record
- All extracted customer/quote fields
- **Status** → New
- **Urgency** → Warm
- **Atlas next action** → "Begin follow-up sequence"

### 4. Follow-Up Signature
When sending follow-up emails to the client's customer, use `atlas@notforgotten.com.au` as the sender but sign as:

```
Kind Regards
Atlas
Working with [Client Business Name]
```

The signature omits the standard Not Forgotten branding (no logo, no tagline, no website). Atlas acts as a white-label assistant embedded in the client's team. The client's customer should perceive Atlas as working for the painting business, not as a separate SaaS company.

### 5. Tracking
Everything gets tracked in Airtable:
- Quote records linked to Client
- Message records for every outbound/inbound touchpoint
- Customer responses tracked in the Messages table
