# Review Prep — Diagnose-Before-Change + CEO Briefing Scope (2026-09-13)

> Outcome data gathered ahead of the 2026-09-13 review of `2026-07-13-diagnose-before-change-ceo-briefing-scope` — two operational refinements: (1) diagnose before recommending major change, (2) routine outreach executed, not reviewed; CEO Briefing = strategic decision surface.

## Decision being reviewed

**What was decided (2026-07-13):**

1. **Diagnose before recommending major change.** When a campaign underperforms, work the evidence chain (deliverability → opens → clicks → replies), identify the most likely bottleneck, then recommend the smallest experiment most likely to improve it. No large strategic changes when a smaller, evidence-based experiment could answer the question.
2. **Operate within delegated authority — routine outreach is executed, not reviewed.** Workflow: draft → Voice Review → Technical Verification → send → reconcile → report in CEO Briefing. James sees an outreach draft before sending only if: (a) genuinely uncertain, (b) factual error or trust-damaging issue, (c) outside an approved sequence, (d) strategic decision required. CEO Briefing = strategic decision surface, not execution queue. "Chief of Staff test" filters what surfaces.

**Review date:** 2026-09-13 (60 days after decision).

## Outcome data — what has happened since

1. **Campaign Pulse Reporting Standard (2026-08-01).** Five-part structure (facts → analysis → hypothesis → forecast → decision), confidence ratings on every interpretation. Institutionalises the diagnose-before-change chain: facts first, interpretation labelled, hypothesis separate from conclusion. Decision `2026-08-01-campaign-pulse-reporting-standard`.

2. **Diagnose-before-change held under real pressure — V2 Email 3 HOLD (2026-09-04).** 32 Email 3 recipients, 0 replies at Day 6. The evidence-based response was HOLD (no Email 4), transition to MONITOR, and founder-phone candidates for 4 B-tier prospects — NOT more email volume, NOT a channel pivot mid-observation. Persistence ≠ repetition (2026-08-28) applied under evidence: silence ≠ lost, stop signals only halt, but continuation must clear the Customer Context Gate. This is the decision's framework working exactly as designed: diagnose the bottleneck (email channel exhausted for this cohort), recommend the smallest next step (founder calls for highest-signal subset), no strategic overreach.

3. **Full Business Review (2026-08-30) — strategic diagnosis, evidence-led.** 16-part review concluded: "working service that cannot yet be sold; 1 paying customer, 0.4% acquisition; next customer comes from founder calls, not email." This is a diagnosed pivot (channel strategy change) reached through evidence, not instinct. Drove the QPaint Operational Handover decision the same day. Demonstrates the boundary: strategic change presented to James with evidence → James decides → Atlas executes.

4. **Delegated authority executed at scale.** V2 Email 3 (32/32, Aug 28) sent under founder-direct authority record; de-confused Email 2 (9/9, Sep 4) sent james@ with Gmail verification + Airtable reconciliation; QPaint daily operations (12 follow-ups Sep 6 under Daily Attention Execute-Not-Report) executed without per-send James review. The delegation boundary held across ~50 sends in the review window. QPaint Operational Handover (Aug 30) + Daily Attention Execute-Not-Report (Sep 6) extended the same principle to client operations: Atlas decides + executes routine work, James sees exceptions only.

5. **One correction cycle — INC-2026-08-19 (6 generic QPaint Email 1s).** Delegation without a context gate failed: 6 emails sent through the canonical path without the Customer Context Gate. Response was NOT to reverse delegation — it was to code-enforce the CCG (Gate -2 in canonical-send). Architecture over memory: the fix strengthened the boundary rather than retreating from it. Also: QPaint Role Boundary (Aug 20) tightened Atlas's mandate (Surface ≠ Manage) after scope was misread. Both corrections refined the model without abandoning it.

6. **Chief of Staff test applied consistently.** Founder exceptions only: Kristen Watt buying signal (Sep 6) → internal handoff, no customer contact needed; Matthew Collard engaged (Sep 4) → white-label ack + handoff under routine authority; Ahmad/Star Painters stalled → surfaced as P1 customer-#2 decision for James. Routine QPaint follow-ups never surfaced as founder exceptions after the execute-not-report standard.
