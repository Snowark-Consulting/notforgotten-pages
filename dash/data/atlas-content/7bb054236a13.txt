# Review Prep — Diagnose-Before-Change + CEO Briefing Scope (2026-09-13)

> Outcome data gathered ahead of the 2026-09-13 review of `2026-07-13-diagnose-before-change-ceo-briefing-scope` — two operational refinements: (1) diagnose before recommending major change, (2) routine outreach executed, not reviewed; CEO Briefing = strategic decision surface.

## Decision being reviewed

**What was decided (2026-07-13):**

1. **Diagnose before recommending major change.** When a campaign underperforms, work the evidence chain (deliverability → opens → clicks → replies), identify the most likely bottleneck, then recommend the smallest experiment most likely to improve it. No large strategic changes when a smaller, evidence-based experiment could answer the question.
2. **Operate within delegated authority — routine outreach is executed, not reviewed.** Workflow: draft → Voice Review → Technical Verification → send → reconcile → report in CEO Briefing. James sees an outreach draft before sending only if: (a) genuinely uncertain, (b) factual error or trust-damaging issue, (c) outside an approved sequence, (d) strategic decision required. CEO Briefing = strategic decision surface, not execution queue. "Chief of Staff test" filters what surfaces.

**Review date:** 2026-09-13 (60 days after decision).
