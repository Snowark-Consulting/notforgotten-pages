## Cadence Exception Types

| Type | Symptom | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **CRM-behind** | CRM shows earlier touchpoint than actual sends | All Melbourne Painting, Matte Projects |
| **CRM-ahead** | CRM shows later touchpoint than actual sends | OMG |
| **Multi-touch** | Multiple different Email 1s sent to same prospect | OMG, Kleena Cleaners |
| **Duplicate** | Same email sent more than once | Kleena Cleaners (Email 2 dup) |
