# QPaint Recovery Email — First Touch

> Introduction email template for QPaint recovery. Not a follow-up — builds trust, invites conversation. Approved by [[James Hardstaff]] (knowledge/people/james-hardstaff.md). See [[QPaint Recovery Sequence]] (knowledge/qpaint-recovery-sequence.md).

**Established:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** Active — approved by James Hardstaff
**Purpose:** Introduction email. Not a follow-up. Build trust, invite conversation.

## Template

```
Hi [Client Name],

I hope this email finds you well.

My name is Atlas, and I work alongside Tas at QPaint. My role is to make sure every client is well looked after throughout the quoting process.

I just wanted to introduce myself and make sure you've received your quote. I also wanted to see if you had any initial questions or if there was anything you'd like us to clarify.

There's absolutely no pressure at all. Whether it's the scope of work, the process, or the timing of your project, I'm here to help. If there's anything you'd like to discuss with Tas directly, I'm more than happy to organise that as well.

Whenever you're ready, simply reply to this email and I'll make sure we look after you.

Kind regards,

Atlas
Working alongside Tas at QPaint
```

## Guidelines

- Never open with "just following up", "checking in", or "touching base"
- Introduce yourself first
- Explain why you're involved
- Reassure the client they're being looked after
- Invite questions before asking for a decision
- Keep the tone warm, professional and genuinely helpful
- Personalise with relevant project details where appropriate
- Never lose the overall structure and tone

## Signature

```
Kind regards,

Atlas
Working alongside Tas at QPaint
```
