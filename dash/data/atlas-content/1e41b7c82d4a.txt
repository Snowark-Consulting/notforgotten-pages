# Learning: Quote Intelligence Requires Commercial Context, Not Just Data

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Source:** QPaint new-quote intake audit (14 Sunday quotes) — Founder directive.
**Status:** Active learning. No schema/infra change authorised.

## The core insight

Not Forgotten cannot reduce a quote to: customer + email + value + address + basic scope.

The intelligence layer needs enough context to understand, for every quote:

1. **WHO** is being followed up (owner, builder, strata manager, body corporate committee, business)
2. **WHAT** was quoted (full meaningful scope, not just "interior/exterior")
3. **WHAT relationship already exists** (new customer vs repeat contact vs multi-quote relationship)
4. **WHAT type of commercial decision** is occurring (residential purchase, builder project, body-corporate tender, government procurement)
5. **WHO is likely involved** in that decision (individual, committee, strata manager, procurement officer)
6. **WHAT timing/dependencies exist** (committee meetings, builder schedule, expiry, access)
7. **WHAT makes this journey different** from a normal quote

## Consequences

- **Customer Segment is commercially meaningful context, not CRM metadata.** A "Residential" label on a body-corporate complex quote is not a cosmetic error — it changes the follow-up decision (who to contact, how decisions get made, cycle length).
- **Evidence over inference.** Segment must be established from primary source (the quote's own text: CTS designation, "body corporate complex", "NEW WORK", QBCC HWI, scaffolding-exclusion, complex scale), combined with organisation/property signals — NOT from email domain alone.

## What is being lost (intake audit finding)

| Field | Status | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Scope, value, GST, dates, expiry, name, email, address | Captured well | Extracted correctly from PandaDoc |
| Customer phone | Blank on ALL quotes | **SOURCE UNAVAILABLE** — QPaint's PandaDoc template has no customer-phone field (only QPaint's own numbers) |
| Customer Segment | Blank / defaulted "Residential" | Not classified at intake; body-corp/builder/business signals present in source but unused |
| Estimator | Inconsistent | "Prepared by" field blank on some PandaDoc docs, present on others (e.g. James Gooley) |

## Product decision (Founder, 2026-08-24)

1. **Do NOT redesign Airtable. Do NOT add fields. Do NOT change intake infrastructure.** Continue gathering evidence from real quote journeys first.
2. **Phone = SOURCE UNAVAILABLE.** Do NOT ask QPaint to change their PandaDoc template. Not Forgotten adapts to the client's existing workflow rather than requiring the client to redesign around us. Later: evaluate phone enrichment or alternative capture.
3. **Correct segment where evidence clearly establishes it** (body corporate / builder / business vs residential), but do not infer from email domain alone.
