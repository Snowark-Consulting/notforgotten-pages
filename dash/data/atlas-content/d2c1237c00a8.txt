# Engaged Dependency → Wait/Monitor → Detect Acceptance → Reconcile Won — 2026-09-12

> Good follow-up is not always more contact: an engaged customer blocked by something outside our control is a WAIT/MONITOR, not another nudge.

Operational learning from the Sian Leathem (#42970) win. No overclaim. Related: [[QPaint]] (`learnings/cards/qpaint.md`), [[Agent Architecture]] (`learnings/cards/agent-architecture.md`), [[LEARNINGS]] (`learnings/LEARNINGS.md`).

## The situation

Sian Leathem, 155 Dornoch Terrace Highgate Hill, $39,996.00. Customer replied
10 Aug that the quote looked reasonable and she was likely to proceed. The only
blocker was carpentry repairs that had to happen before painting — a dependency
managed by QPaint, not by Not Forgotten.

Sian signed the QPaint contract via PandaDoc on 12 Sep 2026.

## The correct NF behaviour (what actually happened)

```
ENGAGED
  → WAIT/MONITOR the external dependency (carpentry)
  → MAINTAIN CONTROL (brief acknowledgments, no pressure, no CTA)
  → DETECT ACCEPTANCE (PandaDoc "completed copy" notification)
  → RECONCILE WON
```

## The principle this reinforces

**Good follow-up is not always more contact.**

For an already-engaged customer whose genuine blocker is outside our control
(here: QPaint-managed carpentry), aggressive repeated follow-up would have been
wrong. The correct behaviour was to hold the relationship, keep the opportunity
visible so it could not be forgotten, and let the customer's own dependency
resolve — then reconcile the acceptance the moment primary evidence appeared.

This extends the existing "Next Touchpoint Date ≠ Contact" lesson
(2026-08-15): a review date for a customer with an external dependency is a
WAIT/MONITOR point, not a send trigger.

## Attribution discipline (reinforced, not changed)

Win Attribution = **Assisted**, not **Recovered**.

Evidence: the customer had already signalled "likely to proceed" before any
blocker-resolving intervention. There is no evidence the opportunity was lost,
abandoned or forgotten and subsequently recovered because of NF intervention.
Confirmation ≠ recovery. WON value = $39,996; Recovered Value left unpopulated.

If new primary evidence later shows NF intervention materially caused the win,
reassess then. Evidence before attribution.
