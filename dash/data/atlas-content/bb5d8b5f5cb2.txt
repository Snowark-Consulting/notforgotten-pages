# Government Requires Procurement-Aware Follow-Up — 2026-08-24

## The corrected principle

Do NOT use "Government = HOLD" as a standing rule. The correct learning is:

**GOVERNMENT REQUIRES PROCUREMENT-AWARE FOLLOW-UP.**

The system must understand the buying process, not merely see the word "government".

## The verified cases

- **QBuild** = a VERIFIED QPaint-managed procurement relationship → HOLD. (QBuild tender work arrives as "QBUILD PAINTING WORKS … As per QBuild specifications"; QPaint manages James ↔ Tas ↔ QBuild directly. NF does not do Email 1 follow-up.)
- **Other government** = requires its own evidence before any classification.

## Possible outcomes (per-case, evidence-driven)

| Evidence | Classification |
|---|---|
| QPaint-managed tender (e.g. QBuild) | HOLD |
| Formal procurement / RFQ | HOLD or procurement-specific treatment |
| Direct government customer with ordinary supplier communication | Follow-up may be appropriate |
| UNKNOWN (no procurement-model evidence) | HOLD until sufficient context exists |

## What must be established before a government record is contacted

- How QPaint obtained the opportunity
- Who the contact is (decision-maker / procurement / facilities / other)
- Whether it was a formal tender / RFQ
- Whether QPaint normally manages the relationship directly
- Whether the entity can independently engage QPaint
- Whether another government procurement mechanism is involved
- Whether normal supplier follow-up is commercially appropriate

## Evidence (2026-08-24)

- **Tracy Schadendorss** — reclassified from "Government — Procurement" to Commercial (private). "Eden Academy Bardon" = childcare centre, referral Google PPC, no government domain/procurement evidence. The government label was unsupported.
- **Leah Powell** — Yeronga State High School, "Government (other than QBuild)", procurement model UNKNOWN → HOLD pending individual review.
