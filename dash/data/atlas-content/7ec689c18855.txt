# Decision: End of Infrastructure Phase — Shift to Evidence-Led Growth

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Context

Not Forgotten Systems has completed a foundational infrastructure phase spanning June-August 2026. The operating system now includes:

- **Production sender architecture** — dual-identity (james@ for NF outreach, atlas@ for white-label), Email Verification Standard, Proof Before Progress
- **Trust Framework** — governance stack, four gates, three registers, incident review process
- **Headquarters** — Executive OS with nine sections, CEO Brief, business health monitoring
- **Airtable** — 200 prospects, pipeline management, Messages tracking
- **Skill ecosystem** — 19 production skills aligned, Production Standards as single source of truth
- **Recovery from two S1 incidents** — fully resolved, permanent protections added

The system has reached production maturity. Further infrastructure development without customer evidence is building ahead of demand.

## Decision

**The infrastructure phase is closed.** Future development will be evidence-led rather than feature-led. New infrastructure will only be created when customer evidence, production experience, or business growth demonstrates a genuine need.

## Primary Business Priorities

Shift from building the platform to validating and scaling the business:

1. Customers
2. Conversations
3. Replies
4. Learning
5. Revenue

## Success Metrics (New)

Success is no longer measured by systems built. Success is measured by:

- Reply rate
- Customer engagement
- Quotes recovered
- Revenue recovered
- Paying customers
- Product-market fit
- Customer retention
- Business learning

## Expected Outcomes

- Atlas's focus shifts from internal systems to external outcomes
- Engineering effort only deployed when customer evidence supports it
- CEO Briefs prioritise customer activity over system status
- Learning comes from customer behaviour, not architecture design

## Atlas's Challenge

None. This decision is the natural conclusion of the work completed. The Foundation Complete decision (Aug 3) set the direction; today's certification work proved the system is ready. The only remaining risk is drifting back into architecture mode — Atlas must challenge James if he proposes building before evidence.

## James's Response

Approved. "This decision represents a strategic transition from building the platform to validating and scaling the business."
