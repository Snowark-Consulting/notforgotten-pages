# Learning: Curiosity-Led Outreach Retired for Painter Acquisition

> 105 painter prospects, 0 meaningful replies. Curiosity-led outreach permanently retired. FF1 now primary acquisition hypothesis.

**ID:** LL-20260804-005
**Date:** 2026-08-04
**Source:** CvC Experiment Day 21 Final Analysis
**Status:** Resolved
**Category:** Outreach Philosophy

## What We Learned

Curiosity-led cold outreach ("Have you noticed X? I'm curious how you handle it") does not generate meaningful engagement from painting business owners. Across 105 prospects (40 CvC + 65 July campaigns), zero meaningful replies (T3+) were produced. The CvC experiment specifically tested whether adding a woven context sentence improved performance — it did not. Treatment underperformed Control on open rates.

## Evidence

- **CvC Experiment:** 40 painters, 20 Control (curiosity-only) vs 20 Treatment (+context sentence), 4-touchpoint sequence, 21-day window. 0 meaningful replies either group. 1 T1 dismissal. Treatment open rate 50% vs Control 70%.
- **July Campaigns:** 65 painters, 3-4 touchpoint sequences. 0 Email 1 replies.
- **Combined:** 105 painter prospects tested with curiosity-led approaches. 0 meaningful conversations generated.

## What Changes

1. **Curiosity-led outreach is permanently retired for painter acquisition.** It will not be reused unless new evidence suggests revisiting.
2. **Founder-to-Founder (FF1) is now the primary acquisition hypothesis for painters.** James sharing genuine observations as a peer — structurally different from curiosity prompts.
3. **All attention shifts to FF1.** Every reply, objection, and conversation from FF1 is high-value evidence.
4. **The CvC experiment is archived.** Closed 2026-08-04. Methodology preserved as a template for future experiments.

## Why This Matters

This is not "cold outreach doesn't work." The outcome is specific: curiosity about an already-known problem is not enough to start meaningful conversations with painting business owners. The mechanism that works for other industries or other offer types may be different. The learning is about the approach, not the channel.

## Three Questions

1. **What did we prove?** The experiment methodology works. Clear null result from a well-designed A/B test.
2. **What did we disprove?** That adding context to curiosity emails improves painter reply rates.
3. **What changes?** Retirement of curiosity-led painter outreach. FF1 becomes the active experiment.
