## Context

Atlas described the 48-hour observation window as "mirroring natural human timing — a business owner wouldn't follow up the moment a quote lands." This framing was incorrect.

## Decision

The 48-hour observation period is not designed to imitate human behaviour. It exists because it creates the best customer experience. That distinction matters.

**Wrong:** "We wait 48 hours to seem human."
**Right:** "We wait 48 hours because it produces the best outcome for the customer."

## Future Architecture: Adaptive Communication Timing

The long-term vision is context-driven timing, not fixed rules. Atlas should eventually determine the most appropriate communication moment based on:

- Industry norms and expectations
- Quote value and complexity
- Customer type (residential, commercial, strata)
- Decision-making process (individual, committee, corporate)
- Urgency signals (expiring quotes, seasonal windows)
- Previous interaction patterns
- Client preferences and past outcomes

Until that capability is proven, 48 hours remains the production default. No implementation required.
