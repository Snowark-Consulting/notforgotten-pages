# Founder Call Outcome Capture

Atlas converts James's rough call notes (verbal or text) into structured CRM truth. Preserve Voice of Customer verbatim, separate from Atlas interpretation. Do not embellish James's notes. UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.

## Capture Fields

- CALL COMPLETED: [y/n — did a real conversation happen?]
- PERSON: [name]
- ROLE: [owner / decision-maker / estimator / receptionist / other]
- DATE/TIME: [Australia/Adelaide]
- ACTUAL CUSTOMER LANGUAGE: [verbatim — the owner's exact words, untouched]
- QUOTE VOLUME: [if stated — e.g. "~20 quotes/month", else UNKNOWN]
- CURRENT FOLLOW-UP PROCESS: [what they actually do — verbatim where possible]
- PAIN: [if stated — the problem, in their words]
- NO PAIN: [if stated — e.g. "we already follow everything up"]
- OBJECTION: [verbatim objection if raised]
- MISUNDERSTANDING: [if they misunderstood the offer — e.g. "I thought you provided leads"]
- INTEREST LEVEL: [Hot / Warm / Cold / None — based on evidence]
- NEXT ACTION: [agreed action]
- NEXT ACTION DATE: [when]
- OWNER: [Atlas / James / other]
- QUALIFICATION STATE: [QUALIFIED / NOT A FIT / NO PAIN / PRICE OBJECTION / ALREADY SOLVED / ONLY WANTS LEADS / NO ANSWER / VOICEMAIL / CALL BACK / OTHER]

## Voice of Customer Preservation Rule

Voice of Customer wording is captured VERBATIM and stored SEPARATELY from Atlas interpretation. FACT first, INTERPRETATION second. Never translate the customer's words into Not Forgotten terminology before recording them.

Examples to preserve exactly:
- "I don't have time to chase them."
- "We already follow everything up."
- "People ghost us."
- "We don't want to annoy customers."
- "My estimator handles it."
- "Most of our work is repeat business."
- "$199 is cheap."
- "I wouldn't pay for that."
- "I thought you provided leads."

## Milestone Counting (10 Real Owner Conversations)

Count only genuine owner/decision-maker conversations. Do NOT count: emails, opens, voicemail, no-answer calls, generic receptionist conversations, weak social interactions.

Purpose: learn whether the market genuinely experiences the problem Not Forgotten is designed to solve.

## Pattern Analysis

After enough conversations, identify recurring patterns. Never create doctrine or conclusions from one conversation.
