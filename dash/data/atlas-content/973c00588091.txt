## The pattern this event establishes

**Three consecutive events have now re-injected content a prior run had already examined and rejected** (20th event's `tools/`-Python block on the 21st; the 15th-occurrence block and the multipart trap on both the 21st and the 22nd). "Never inherit last night's attestation verdict" is no longer a caution — it is the routine case. Re-attest every block against the filesystem, every run.

A second pattern: the writer now **regresses repairs**. The 19th event's repair deliberately kept the autostash trap **with its discriminator**; the 22nd event stripped the discriminator straight back out. A repair that changes a card's prose is not stable — the writer will revert it. This is an argument for keeping the discriminator in the series fact sheet (where it is corroborated by the 22nd event's own readable window) rather than only in the card.
