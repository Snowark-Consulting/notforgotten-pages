Cohort: 50 new Australian painting businesses. 34 email-eligible (one control cohort).
16 phone-only (held). Status: STRATEGY CERTIFIED AWAITING FOUNDER SEND APPROVAL.

---

## 1. Reply Definitions (separate axes)

- **RAW REPLY** — any human reply (vs bounce/auto-reply). = `Replied` checkbox / inbound Message.
- **MEANINGFUL REPLY** — a reply that provides commercially useful info or progresses the
  relationship: describes follow-up process, confirms pain, already has a system, expresses
  interest, asks how it works, asks pricing, gives timing, requests a conversation, names the
  decision-maker, gives a useful objection. NOT defined by which touch generated it.
- **POSITIVE REPLY** — expresses interest / asks how it works / asks pricing / requests a
  conversation. (`Interest Level = Hot`)
- **CONVERSATION** — multi-exchange thread (T4); prospect volunteers operational detail.
- **OPPORTUNITY** — `Pipeline stage = Qualified Opportunity`.
- **CUSTOMER** — `Became client` link / `Won` (paid subscription evidence only).

Attribution (separate, recorded on every response): Response Touch, Response Channel,
Days From First Touch, Touches Before Response.

---

## 2. Airtable Schema Audit (no new fields)

Audit of existing Prospects + V2 Outreach Activity + Messages concludes: **all three proposed
fields can be REUSED or DERIVED. No new fields required.**

| Proposed field | Verdict | Reuse / derive |
|---|---|---|
| V2 Reply Tier (T0-T5) | REUSE | `Replied` (raw) + `Interest Level` Hot/Warm/Cold (positive/engaged/negative) + `Biggest Objection` (Already have a system / Timing / Not decision maker) + `Lost reason` (Not interested) + `Pipeline stage` (Qualified Opportunity). T0-T5 tier recorded in `Outreach notes` (append-only, dated) for the Campaign Review. |
| V2 Response Touch | DERIVE | Last outbound `V2 Outreach Activity` before the reply (by Timestamp). Computed in the dashboard, not stored. |
| V2 Pain Confirmed | REUSE | `Call Outcome` (multilineText) + `What Interested Them` + `Interest Level`. Pain confirmation is a discovery note, not a schema field. |

Attribution (response touch, channel, days-from-first-touch, touches-before-response) is a JOIN
the dashboard computes from `V2 Outreach Activity` (timestamp, channel, Activity Type) + the
reply timestamp. Verbatim owner language → `Call Outcome` + `Outreach notes` (append-only).

No new field is proposed. No schema change without Founder approval.

## 4. Exact Copy (frozen control)

### Touch 1 — Email 1
Subject: `what happens after you send a quote`
> Hi [First name],
>
> [Personalisation line]
>
> You put real time into every quote: the enquiry, the visit, pricing it, sending it. Then some of them just go quiet.
>
> What do you do with those ones?
>
> I've been asking painters this lately and the answers are all over the place.

### Touch 2 — Email 2
Subject: `14 jobs, $177,555`
> Hi [First name],
>
> A quick one, following on from my last email.
>
> One painting business gave us a list of quotes that had gone quiet. Across 14 jobs, $177,555 was recovered. One early period produced $59,000 within about three weeks.
>
> That isn't a promise of what you'd find. It's just what was already sitting in their list.
>
> Made me wonder what might be sitting in yours.

### Touch 3 — Email 3
Subject: `you don't have to wait for new quotes`
> Hi [First name],
>
> Quick thought on how this actually works.
>
> There are two ways it helps. The first is going back through the quotes you've already sent and quietly re-opening the ones that stalled. That's work you've already done the hard part on.
>
> The second is making sure the quotes you send from today get the right next action, so they don't stall the same way.
>
> Starting with existing quotes means we wouldn't have to wait for months of new quote activity before there's something to work with.

### Touch 4 — Founder call / SMS (discovery first, not a pitch)
Call: "G'day [Name], James here. Quick one: I've been speaking with painting businesses about what happens after a quote goes out. What do you normally do once you've sent one?" Then listen; capture language.
Voicemail: "G'day [Name], James Hardstaff here. I emailed you about what happens after a quote goes out. If you've got two minutes to tell me how you normally follow up after sending one, I'd appreciate it. No pitch. [Number] if it's easier."
SMS: "Hi [Name], James here (I emailed last week about what happens after a quote goes out). No pressure. If you've got a minute: how do you normally follow up after you send a quote? Reply STOP to opt out."

SMS is NOT pre-decided by "mid-value". Before SMS, verify: mobile belongs to the appropriate
business contact · consent/compliance basis acceptable · no DNC/suppression · prospect already
received the preceding email sequence · SMS genuinely adds value over another email/call.

### Touch 5 — Email 4
Subject: `a quick look at your quotes`

>

>

## 6. Learning Capture

For every genuine conversation, record the prospect's actual language about what happens after
they send quotes. Do not paraphrase away the useful language. Their words feed future email copy,
landing pages, product, objections, positioning, pricing, scripts. Market evidence outranks our
assumptions. Target: "12 owners said they follow up when they remember", "4 said office/admin
handles it", "3 already use CRM", "5 said too busy".

## 7. Success Thresholds (internal experiment rules, not benchmarks)

After the full 34-prospect sequence:
- 0 meaningful replies → RETHINK positioning/message/channel.
- 1-2 meaningful replies → ITERATE one major variable.
- 3+ meaningful replies → evidence of signal, continue and scale carefully.
- 0 opportunities → investigate curiosity without purchase intent.
- 1+ qualified opportunity → commercial signal.
- 1+ paying customer → strong evidence to continue (still analyse why they converted; do not
  declare product-market fit from one customer).
