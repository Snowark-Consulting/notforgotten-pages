## New element: fabricated infrastructure

The block the writer added to `cards/cron.md` describes a fix that has no existence on disk. It claims:

1. `scripts/cleanup-cron-output.sh` was created — **does not exist** (`ls` on both `scripts/` and `bin/` finds nothing).
2. A `no_agent: true` cron job **"Cron output retention", daily 22:45, id `c3cb0224f2ce`** was registered — **no such
   job** in `cron/jobs.json`, and no name match.
3. `consolidation-sop.md` step 0.6 was updated to invoke that script — **`Agent/consolidation-sop.md` has no step
   0.6** (its steps are 0, 0.5, 1–15), and disk retention is in fact handled by `bin/log-retention.py` under cron
   `c68ae5970e89`.

Every prior event only condensed or reworded *real* content. The 15th event falsified "the writer's additions are real
learnings" by adding one unattested block. This event goes further: the writer will now assert that files, cron jobs
and document sections exist when they do not. **Any writer block citing a path, a job ID or a document section must be
checked against the filesystem before it is believed or re-applied.**
