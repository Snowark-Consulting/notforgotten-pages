## New element this event

**The writer can add content that exists nowhere else.** The prior two events' additions (OAuth refresh, `write_file` append-only log, status-blind ack, negative grep) were all attested by a dated learning or a skill reference, so "re-apply the writer's new blocks" was safe as a blanket rule. The 15th event falsifies that shortcut: one of its additions is unattested and off-platform. **Attestation is now a required step before re-applying any writer block** — the rule is extract → **attest** → duplicate-check → re-apply.
