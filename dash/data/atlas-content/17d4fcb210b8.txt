# Next Touchpoint Date ≠ Contact (Review vs Contact) — 2026-08-15

Founder clarification from the Sian Leathem case.

## The lesson

A **Next Touchpoint Date (NTP)** does not necessarily mean **CONTACT CUSTOMER**. It can
mean **REVIEW THE OPPORTUNITY**.

When a customer explicitly says they will come back to us ("I'll be in touch soon"), the
system should **review** the situation at the appropriate time before deciding whether
another contact is warranted — it must not auto-contact on the date simply because the
date arrived.

## How to handle "I'll be in touch"

- Preserve the commercial state (Engaged stays Engaged).
- Send a brief acknowledgment only — no CTA, no question, no pressure, no promise to
  follow up on a specific date.
- Set the next touchpoint as a REVIEW point, and make First Action explicitly
  "REVIEW ONLY (not contact)" so no automation treats the date as a send trigger.
