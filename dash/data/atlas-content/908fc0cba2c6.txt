# QPaint Customer Journey — Five Production Emails (v2)

**Customer:** Sarah — received exterior painting quote ($8,500, 3-bedroom home, Adelaide)
**Status:** Awaiting James approval
**Refinements:** Tas woven naturally throughout. Every close invites a real conversation.

---

## Email 1 — Reassurance

**Subject:** Your quote from QPaint

Hi Sarah,

I wanted to make sure you received the quote for the exterior of your home and check whether anything needs clarifying.

I know getting quotes can feel a bit overwhelming. Different painters. Different numbers. Different approaches. It's not always easy to compare them.

Tas has been doing this long enough to know that. He'd rather you took your time and felt confident in the decision than felt rushed into something.

So if anything in the quote doesn't make sense, or you'd like me to talk through how we arrived at a particular number, just ask. I can grab Tas if there's something I can't answer myself.

No pressure from this end.

Even if it's just to say you've received the quote and you're still thinking about it, I'd appreciate hearing from you. Then I'll leave you to it.

Atlas
Customer Care — QPaint

## Email 2 — Decision Support

**Subject:** A few things worth knowing

Hi Sarah,

I've been doing this alongside Tas for a while now, and there are a few things people often wonder about when they're thinking over a painting quote. Thought I'd share them in case they're helpful.

**Who will be in my home?**

The crew who'd be doing your exterior are the same painters Tas has worked with for years. They've done hundreds of homes around Adelaide together. They'll introduce themselves on the first day, and they take pride in how they leave a place.

**How long will it take?**

For the exterior of a home your size, typically around 5 to 7 working days, depending on weather. Tas plans jobs with a bit of buffer built in because Adelaide weather doesn't always cooperate. We'll confirm exact dates before we start.

**What if something's not right?**

One thing Tas always says: the job's not finished until the customer says it is. If anything needs attention afterwards, you tell us and we come back. No arguments. That's been his policy since he started.

**Do I need to be home?**

Up to you. Most people come and go. The crew Tas has built are the kind of people you feel comfortable leaving a key with.

If something's holding you back — even something small — I'm happy to talk it through. Sometimes it's the little things that make the difference.

Atlas
Customer Care — QPaint

## Email 3 — Confidence

**Subject:** A few words from people we've worked with

Hi Sarah,

I could tell you we do good work. But everyone says that.

What actually matters is what happens after the job starts. Do they turn up? Do they clean up? Do they fix things if something's not right?

Here's what we hear from people after they've worked with us.

Most were nervous beforehand. That's completely normal. Tas says it's actually a good sign — it means they care about their home.

Then the crew arrives. They're on time. They're polite. They get on with it.

By day two or three, most customers tell us they stopped worrying. The house starts to feel normal again. The crew become familiar faces. The job just... happens.

When it's finished, the transformation is the thing everyone mentions first. A house they've looked at every day suddenly looks different. Fresher. Cared for.

I checked in with Tas before writing this, and he said something that stuck with me. He said most of his customers become friends. Not in a sales way. In the way that happens when someone does good work on your home and you keep bringing them back.

I'm sharing all this because I know choosing a painter is a bigger decision than most people expect. These are the things that matter once the work starts.

If you've got questions — or if another quote has raised something you want to talk through — I'm here. And Tas is happy to jump on a call if that would help.

Atlas
Customer Care — QPaint

## Email 4 — Honest Conversation

**Subject:** Where are you at with the quote?

Hi Sarah,

I've sent a few emails now about the exterior quote. I hope they've been helpful rather than annoying.

I wanted to ask honestly — where are you at with it?

Sometimes the timing's not right. Sometimes another quote came in lower. Sometimes you're still deciding and you just need more time. All of that's completely fine.

I was talking with Tas about your quote this morning. He asked me to make sure you knew one thing: there's no pressure from this end. If the scope needs adjusting, we can rework it. If you've decided to wait until spring, we'll pick it up then. If you've gone another direction, no hard feelings at all — Tas just asked me to make sure you knew you were welcome back anytime.

I'm emailing because I'd rather know where things stand than keep guessing. Even a quick "still thinking" or "not right now" helps.

Whatever's happening, I'd genuinely like to hear it.

Atlas
Customer Care — QPaint

## Email 5 — Outcome Support

**Scenario:** Sarah hasn't replied to any of the four emails. It's been 14 days since Email 4.

**Subject:** Checking in

Hi Sarah,

It's been a little while since I last emailed about the exterior quote.

I'm not writing to chase you for a decision. Genuinely — that's not what this is.

I'm writing because I don't want to keep emailing you if the timing's wrong, or if you've gone another direction, or if painting the house just isn't a priority right now. All of those are perfectly reasonable.

But I also don't want to assume any of those things if you're still interested and life simply got in the way. That happens more often than you'd think.

So I thought I'd ask one more time, and then leave it with you.

If you're still considering the quote and you'd like me to stay in touch, just let me know. Even a quick "not yet" is enough. I'll check back when it makes sense.

If you've moved on, that's completely fine too. I'll stop emailing and leave your details here in case anything changes down the track. Tas has had customers come back two years later and he's treated them exactly the same as if they'd never left.

Either way, I hope the information I've shared has been useful. If you ever want to pick this up again, you know where to find me.

Atlas
Customer Care — QPaint
