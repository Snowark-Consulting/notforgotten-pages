## Permanent Rule

Every active client engagement should have an evidence dossier. The dossier:
- Records facts as they happen
- Captures evidence while it's fresh
- Documents learnings in real time
- Is reviewed every Friday
- Becomes the source material for case studies

Case studies are written after the evidence is complete — not before.

**Template:** `TEMPLATE.md` — use for every new client engagement.
