## 1. Who We Are

Not Forgotten Systems helps trade and service businesses follow up on quotes they've sent to their customers. When a plumber, electrician, painter, or other tradesperson sends a quote and doesn't hear back, we help make sure that opportunity isn't forgotten.

In providing this service, we handle two categories of personal information:

- **Our client's information** — the trade business owner's name, contact details, and business information.
- **Our client's customers' information** — the names, contact details, and quote information of the people our clients are quoting. This information comes to us through quote emails forwarded by our clients.

We are an Australian company. Our service is hosted on infrastructure in Australia, the United States, and — for certain AI processing — China. Where data leaves Australia, we take steps to ensure it remains protected.

| Information | Example | Why we collect it |

This is collected through Google Analytics and is anonymised. We do not track individual visitors across other websites.

### Information from email opens

When we send follow-up emails to our clients' customers, we include a tiny invisible image (a tracking pixel) that tells us whether the email was opened. We collect:

- Whether the email was opened
- When it was opened
- How many times it was opened

We do this so our clients know whether their follow-up messages are being seen. This helps us improve our service and reporting.

**Note:** Email open tracking is not perfectly accurate. Some email systems (like Apple Mail and Microsoft Outlook) pre-load images or block them entirely, which means we may not detect every open. We present open data as directional rather than definitive.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use personal information for the following purposes, and only for these purposes:

1. **Providing our core service** — following up on quotes sent by our clients to their customers. This includes sending follow-up emails, tracking responses, and reporting on engagement.

2. **Improving our service** — analysing patterns in quote follow-up to improve our approach. This analysis is done at an aggregate level where possible.

3. **Communicating with our clients** — contacting clients about their account, service updates, or new features.

4. **Generating Quote Insights reports** — providing our clients with monthly analysis of their quote activity (e.g., "15 quotes sent this month, 8 followed up, 3 converted").

5. **Website analytics** — understanding how visitors use our website so we can improve it.

We do **not**:

- Sell personal information to anyone
- Use personal information for marketing other products or services
- Use personal information to train AI models that are shared with other customers
- Make automated decisions that have legal or significant effects on individuals

## 4. AI Processing

We use artificial intelligence to help draft follow-up emails, extract information from quote PDFs, and analyse communication patterns.

When our AI processes data, the information in that specific task (which may include names, business details, quote values, and email content) is sent to our AI providers for processing.

Our primary AI provider is Google (Gemini) in the United States. We may also use other AI services for internal-only tasks that do not involve client-identifiable information.

**What this means for your data:** When our system processes a quote or drafts a follow-up email, the content of that task is sent to Google's Gemini AI service for processing. Google processes the request and returns a response. We do not send data to AI providers for training purposes, and we do not permit our providers to retain or use your data for their own purposes.

**Transparency note:** We are committed to transparency about how we handle your data. Our primary AI processing is conducted using Google's Gemini in the United States, which operates under robust data protection frameworks. We ensure that any AI processing involving client data adheres to our strict privacy principles and minimises data exposure.

## 6. How We Protect Information

We take the security of your information seriously. Here is what we do to protect it:

| Measure | What it means |
|---|---|
| Encryption in transit | All data sent between our systems and third-party services is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS |
| Cloud provider security | We use established cloud providers (Google, Airtable, Vercel) who maintain their own security certifications |
| Access control | Access to client data is limited to authorised personnel and our automated systems. Our AI assistant (Atlas) operates under defined access controls |
| Credential management | API keys and access tokens are stored securely, never in code or logs |
| File permissions | Sensitive files on our servers are restricted to authorised system processes only |
| Regular security reviews | We conduct monthly security health checks and quarterly comprehensive reviews |

**Honest assessment:** We are a small, growing business. We do not have the security infrastructure of a large enterprise. We are transparent about this with our clients. We invest in security proportionate to the sensitivity of the data we handle, and we improve our security posture every month. Our full security practices are documented internally and available to clients on request.

## 8. Your Rights

Under Australian privacy law, individuals have rights regarding their personal information. This applies to both our clients and our clients' customers.

### Access

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you. We will provide this within 30 days. Contact us at james@notforgotten.com.au with "Privacy — Access Request" in the subject line, describing what information you're looking for.

### Correction

If information we hold about you is incorrect, you can ask us to fix it. Contact us with the details and we will update our records.

### Deletion

You can ask us to delete your personal information. We will do so unless we have a legal or contractual obligation to keep it (for example, financial records we're required to retain).

For our clients' customers: if you receive a follow-up email from Not Forgotten on behalf of a trade business and want your information removed, contact us directly or reply to the email. We will process your request and notify the trade business that sent the original quote.

### Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your personal information properly, contact us first. We take privacy seriously and will investigate.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

### Our website

Our website at notforgotten.com.au uses Google Analytics, which places cookies in your browser to understand how visitors use the site. These cookies collect anonymised information about pages visited, time on site, and referral sources. They do not identify you personally.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. The website will still work.

### Email tracking

As described in Section 2, our follow-up emails include a tracking pixel that tells us whether an email was opened. This is a tiny (1×1 pixel) invisible image. When the email is opened and images are loaded, our server records the open event.

This tracking is part of the service we provide to our clients — it helps them know whether their follow-up messages are being seen. If you prefer not to be tracked, most email programs allow you to disable automatic image loading.
