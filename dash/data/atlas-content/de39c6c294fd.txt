## 4. ⚠️ STRUCTURAL FINDING — the daylight-saving transition, Sun 4 October

**This is the first DST transition in this system's life.** SA moves ACST (+09:30) to ACDT (+10:30) at
02:00 on Sunday 4 October. Nothing in the vault, the registers or the cron config had ever accounted for
it. Two separate consequences, one verified by direct test and one by arithmetic.

### 4a. Every daily cron fires an hour early on Sunday 4 Oct, and some fire twice

I called the scheduler's own run computation (`cron.jobs.compute_next_run`, the same function
`mark_job_run` uses to re-arm a job after it fires) with the real schedule expressions and an Adelaide
clock. Result, reproducible across the whole job set:

| Job (intent) | Computed next run on Sun 4 Oct | Wall clock delivered |
|---|---|---|
| QPaint Daily Attention `30 7 * * *` | `2026-10-04T06:30:00+10:30` | **06:30**, not 07:30 |
| Overnight Founder Readiness `0 4 * * *` | `2026-10-04T03:00:00+10:30` | **03:00**, not 04:00 |
| Nightly consolidation `0 21 * * *` | `2026-10-04T20:00:00+10:30` | **20:00**, not 21:00 |
| Nightly backup `30 21 * * *` | `2026-10-04T20:30:00+10:30` | 20:30 |
| Prepare-Ahead (this job) `0 22 * * *` | `2026-10-04T21:00:00+10:30` | 21:00 |
| Draft surfacer `5 22 * * *` | `2026-10-04T21:05:00+10:30` | 21:05 |
| Learnings integrity guard `20 21 * * *` | `2026-10-04T20:20:00+10:30` | 20:20 |

Every one is exactly one hour early. Wall-clock order is preserved, so no job runs out of sequence.

**And a second consequence I did not expect: the affected jobs fire twice.** Re-arming from the early
fire lands on the intended time the same day — `30 7 * * *` computes to `06:30`, then from 06:30 it
computes to `07:30`. So on Sunday the QPaint Daily Attention, the overnight readiness report, the
**nightly memory consolidation**, the nightly backup and this job all run twice. From Monday the
computation is correct again (`30 7 * * *` from Sun 23:00 → `2026-10-05T07:30:00+10:30`).

**Assessed impact: acceptable, no intervention.** No customer send is due on Sunday in either pipeline
(§2), every send path is idempotency-guarded (`CAMPAIGN_ID + RECIPIENT` = one send), and the duplicated
jobs are all idempotent or append-only. The cost is one extra LLM pass and a few duplicate Telegram
posts. **I did not hand-edit `next_run_at` in `jobs.json`** — intervening in the scheduler's stored state
during a cron run is a higher risk than the defect it would fix.

**The scheduler has this trade-off documented in code** (`cron/jobs.py:3254-3268`): it cannot
distinguish a config/host timezone migration from a legitimate DST offset change, and the comment
explicitly accepts that a DST boundary will recompute rather than fire. The recompute-then-double-fire
behaviour is the same defect seen from the other side. **Recommendation: hand this to Josh/Winston as a
Hermes-core item, not a vault fix** — it recurs every October and every April.

### 4b. QPaint's customer email moves an hour earlier in Brisbane from Monday

Adelaide shifts, Queensland does not. QPaint's customers are overwhelmingly in Brisbane and the
surrounding QLD coast.

| | Adelaide 07:30 | Brisbane local |
|---|---|---|
| Up to Sat 3 Oct | 07:30 ACST | **08:00 AEST** |
| From Mon 5 Oct | 07:30 ACDT | **07:00 AEST** |

So from Monday, every QPaint customer email arrives an hour earlier in the customer's own day than it
has for the entire life of this operation. The send-evidence records show the historical arrival at
08:08–08:13 Brisbane.

**Nothing here is a rule breach.** The send window is bound in Adelaide time, the weekend prohibition
covers Sat/Sun only, and 07:00 is still within the protocol's "natural business hours wherever
practical". But it is a silent, unowned change to the customer's experience caused by our state's clock,
and it will persist until April 2027.

**Recommendation — one line, either way:**
- **(a) Accept it** (default if you say nothing): 07:00 Brisbane is a reasonable time to reach a
  home-services customer, and changing it is churn without evidence.
- **(b) Preserve the customer's experience**: move QPaint Daily Attention to `30 8 * * *` for the DST
  period, so QPaint customers keep receiving at ~08:00 Brisbane. Everything the job does shifts an hour
  later with it, which is harmless.

My recommendation is **(b)** on the principle that a customer's experience should not change because our
state changed its clocks — but (a) is defensible and I will not move it without your word.
