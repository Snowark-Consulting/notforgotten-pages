# EXP-001: End-to-End Attribution

> Every external link traceable to source. UTM standard deployed across all channels.

**Status:** running  
**Released:** 2026-07-25  
**Review Date:** 2026-08-25  

## What Changed

Every external link now carries standardised UTM parameters. Cold outreach, email signatures, website CTAs, social profiles — every visitor can be traced back to how they found us.

Naming convention: `knowledge/utm-naming-convention.md`

## Hypothesis

UTM-tagged links will reveal which channels drive website traffic, which produce enquiries, and eventually which produce paying clients. Without attribution, all traffic looks like `(direct)` — we can't optimise what we can't measure.

## Expected Result

Within 30 days:
- 0% of traffic shows as "(direct) / (none)" from known outreach recipients
- At least one channel emerges as the dominant traffic driver
- The weekly intelligence report includes a "Traffic by Channel" section
- We can trace at least one enquiry back to its source channel

Within 90 days:
- Attribution data connects a channel to a qualified lead
- Attribution data connects a channel to a paying client
