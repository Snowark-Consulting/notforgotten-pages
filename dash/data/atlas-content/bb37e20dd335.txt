## 3. bin/ symlink path-resolution constraint (root cause, mitigated)

- The scheduler blocks any script whose resolved path falls outside `scripts/`
  (`resolves outside the scripts directory`). `bin/*.py` symlinks under
  `scripts/bin/` therefore fail.
- Mitigated 2026-08-14 by copying `log-retention.py`, `monthly-outreach-report.py`,
  `sync-james-signature.py`, `followup-queue.py` from `bin/` to `scripts/bin/` as
  real files. Log Retention now runs ok.
- Remaining constraint: any FUTURE `bin/` symlink will hit the same block. Documented,
  not repaired (would need scheduler config or a different layout).
