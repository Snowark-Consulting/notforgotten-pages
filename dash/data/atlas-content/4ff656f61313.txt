## MB-001: pandadoc-extract.py — scope_summary cleanup

**Created:** 2026-08-06
**Source:** pandadoc-extract.py v2.0.0 certification
**Severity:** Low
**Status:** Open

**Problem:** The `scope_summary` field currently includes full terms and conditions, warranties, privacy text, and contract boilerplate alongside the meaningful scope-of-work section. The extracted text is 30K+ chars, making it impractical for CRM display.

**Requirement:** Extract only the meaningful scope-of-work section (inclusions, exclusions, notes about the specific job) and exclude:
- Standard terms and conditions
- Warranties and disclaimers
- Privacy text
- QBCC insurance boilerplate
- Payment terms
- General specifications (paint types, application methods)
- Acceptance/contract boilerplate

**Guard:** Any change must:
1. Preserve all six certified primary fields (customer_name, customer_email, pandadoc_number, quote_value, address, quote_date)
2. Pass regression testing against the certified test set before production use

**Dependencies:** None. Can be tackled independently.
