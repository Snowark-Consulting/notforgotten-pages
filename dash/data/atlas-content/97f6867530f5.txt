## Context

This decision overturns `2026-07-29-dual-identity-communication-policy` which established separate sender identities for prospects (atlas@) and clients (james@). That decision was made earlier the same day. On reflection, James recognised that the dual-identity approach introduced unnecessary complexity and an artificial relationship boundary.

The technical infrastructure built for the dual-identity policy — the james@ OAuth token, the canonical signature retrieval, the weekly sync — remains in place and is fully leveraged by the new single-identity model.

## The Decision

### One face, one relationship

All external Not Forgotten communications come from `james@notforgotten.com.au`. This includes:

- Cold outreach
- Prospect follow-ups
- Discovery conversations
- Client onboarding
- Ongoing account management
- Monthly reports
- Renewal discussions
- General business communication

### Atlas's role — behind the scenes

Atlas is the intelligence and operations platform. Atlas:

- Drafts all communications
- Schedules all communications
- Monitors all replies
- Analyses customer behaviour
- Recommends next actions
- Maintains CRM records
- Generates reports
- Ensures no opportunity is forgotten

Atlas does NOT:
- Send as a separate identity
- Appear as the primary relationship owner
- Introduce itself as "Atlas, Customer Success Coordinator" in NF communications
- Have a visible role in the customer relationship

### Atlas as technology reference

When appropriate, Atlas may be referenced within communications as the technology that supports Not Forgotten. For example:

- "Atlas keeps track of every follow-up so nothing gets missed."
- "Our system monitors your quote activity and flags opportunities."

Atlas is the product, not the person. The customer's relationship is with James and Not Forgotten.

### White-label exception

Recovery operations for clients (e.g. QPaint) continue to use their approved white-label identity. This decision applies to Not Forgotten's own business communications, not to client recovery operations where Atlas appears as an embedded team member of the client's business.

## The Guiding Principle

**"James builds the relationships. Atlas elevates every interaction."**

## Expected Outcomes

1. Customers experience one continuous relationship from first contact through to long-term client
2. No artificial handoff between Atlas and James
3. Atlas is understood as the technology that makes Not Forgotten reliable, not a separate person
4. Every communication is consistent in voice, identity, and signature
5. The system is simpler to operate, maintain, and explain

## Success Criteria

- 100% of external NF communications sent from james@
- Zero emails sent from atlas@ as a primary NF identity
- Customer feedback indicates they feel they are working with James, not an AI
- Atlas is referenced only as technology, never as a person

## Implementation

1. Overturn `2026-07-29-dual-identity-communication-policy`
2. Rewrite SOUL.md directive #16 to the new principle
3. Rewrite `knowledge/sender-identity-policy.md` as single-identity James-led
4. Update `email-draft` skill — all sends from james@
5. Update `cold-email` skill — sender identity change
6. All outreach templates use James's voice and james@ sender
7. White-label recovery operations remain separate
