# Monthly Recovery Intelligence Report

> Synthesises evidence from all completed recovery sequences into actionable recommendations. First Monday of each month. First report: Sep 7 2026. See [[Atlas Learning Framework]] (knowledge/atlas-learning-framework.md).

**Cadence:** First Monday of each month
**First report:** Monday 7 September 2026 (covering August)
**Purpose:** Synthesise evidence from all completed sequences into actionable recommendations.

---

## Report Structure

### 1. Executive Summary
- Total sequences completed this month
- Total revenue recovered
- Recovery rate (% of completed sequences that resulted in Won)
- Top-line recommendation

### 2. Pipeline Activity
- Emails sent (by stage)
- Replies received
- Sequences completed
- Outcomes: Won / Lost / Deferred / No Response
- Revenue recovered vs pipeline value

### 3. Email Performance
- Reply rate by email number (Email 1 vs 2 vs 3 vs 4 vs 5)
- Conversion rate by email number (% of replies at each stage that led to Won)
- Average time to first reply
- Email 1 open rate (when tracking is available)

### 4. Customer Behaviour
- Most common buying stage at first reply
- Average days from quote sent to first engagement
- Most common objections (ranked by frequency)
- Most common buying signals

### 5. Segment Performance
- Residential: reply rate, conversion rate, avg deal value recovered
- Government: reply rate, conversion rate, avg deal value recovered
- Corporate/Commercial: reply rate, conversion rate, avg deal value recovered
- By referral source: QPaint Website vs Facebook vs Body Corp vs Previous Client

### 6. Recovery Score Calibration
- Did higher-scored deals convert at higher rates?
- Which scoring dimensions are most predictive of recovery?
- Recommended adjustments to the scoring model

### 7. Sequence Recommendations
- What's working (keep)
- What's not working (change or remove)
- Proposed changes (specific, evidence-backed)
- One experiment to run next month

### 8. Learnings
- What surprised us?
- What confirmed our assumptions?
- What did we get wrong?
- What do we know now that we didn't know last month?

---

## Rules

- **Recommend changes only when supported by evidence across multiple customers.** A single unusual outcome is noise, not signal.
- **Do not change the sequence automatically.** Changes require James's approval.
- **Good judgement always takes priority over the framework.** If customer context suggests a different approach, recommend it.
- **Every recommendation must cite specific evidence.** "3 out of 4 government contacts replied to Email 1" is evidence. "Government contacts seem more responsive" is not.
- **Continuous improvement through evidence, not continual change through opinion.**
