# Observation-Window Handoff — Operational Interruption, Not Pipeline Failure

**Date:** 2026-08-24
**Scope:** QPaint quote follow-up (John Wilson, Steve Liversidge, Sharon Fleay)
**Category:** Operating model / process ownership

## Symptom
Three fresh PandaDoc quotes (20 Aug) had their 48-hour observation windows expire (22 Aug) and received zero NF contact. All three records were correctly created and correctly dated — the only missing step was the human REVIEW → DECIDE handoff.

## Root cause
- Intake worked (quotes-pulse-phase2 running) — records created correctly.
- Observation window worked — NTD set to +48h correctly.
- **The handoff `NEW QUOTE → 48h OBSERVATION → REVIEW → DECISION` did not run.**
- `follow-up-autopilot` (the automation that surfaced/acted on expired windows) was disabled/retired, and the manual review step was suspended while QPaint outbound work was deliberately stopped during the operating-model correction.

## Classification
This was an **operational interruption**, NOT evidence that the intake pipeline is broken. No infrastructure change is warranted from these three records.

## The learning (standing rule)
1. An observation window expiring **must surface the customer for REVIEW → DECIDE**.
2. It must **never** mean automatic Email 1.
3. It must **also never disappear** simply because automated outbound is disabled — a disabled autopilot must fall through to a human review signal, not a silent gap.

## Action taken
- John Wilson, Steve Liversidge, Sharon Fleay each individually reviewed and (after founder approval) sent Email 1 through canonical_send. Reconciled in Airtable. Next date = REVIEW only (31 Aug).

## Status
Closed for the three records. Operating-model gap to be addressed separately once the customer book is controlled.
