# Core Product Learning — Not Forgotten Is Not An Email Sequence Tool

**Date:** 2026-08-19
**Type:** Major product learning (permanent)
**Source:** QPaint generic-mail-merge incident (6 real customers received generic Email 1 copy)
**Owner:** Atlas / James Hardstaff

---

## The Learning

Not Forgotten's value is **not** "send Email 1 after X days, Email 2 after Y days."

The value is: **understand what is happening with every quote and determine the right next action.**

Sometimes that action is follow-up.
Sometimes it is waiting.
Sometimes it is escalating to the client (QPaint).
Sometimes it is answering a question.
Sometimes it is founder judgment.
Sometimes it is doing absolutely nothing.

**Automation executes the decision. Customer context determines the decision.**

---

## What Broke

Atlas collapsed the decision model into:

> DUE + ELIGIBLE + TEMPLATE = SEND

This treated canonical templates as finished copy and reduced customer understanding to a
mail-merge of [First Name] + [interior/exterior]. Six fresh-intake customers (quotes received
2–3 days prior) received a generic "recovery-introduction" that did not reflect their actual
project, timing, or decision stage.

## The Correct Model

> CUSTOMER TRUTH → CURRENT COMMERCIAL STATE → CORRECT NEXT ACTION →
> COMMUNICATION OBJECTIVE → APPROPRIATE MESSAGE → COMPLIANCE GATES → SEND

Codified as `knowledge/customer-context-gate.md`.

---

## Permanent Rules Introduced

1. **Customer Context Gate (A–H)** — mandatory evidence-based brief before any outbound touch.
2. **Template = framework, not finished copy.** Verbatim only when the generic wording genuinely fits.
3. **Fresh vs recovery distinction** is mandatory.
4. **Customer Experience Test** — "would this feel like someone understood my situation?" NO/UNCERTAIN → HOLD.
5. **No over-personalisation** — relevance, not data-showing-off.
6. **The system must be able to decide NOT to communicate.**
