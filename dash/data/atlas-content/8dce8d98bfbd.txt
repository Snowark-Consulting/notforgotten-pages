## Option A — Close the cohort *(recommended)*

Mark `Pipeline stage: Campaign Complete`, `Sequence Status: Lost`, append a dated note recording why
(sequence abandoned after contamination; Email 2 never sent; no contact since Email 1). No email sent.

**Why this is the honest read:**

1. **Timing integrity.** Email 2 was designed as a Day +5 follow-up. Sent on Day +39 it is not the
   sequence, it is a different communication with a different meaning — and a worse customer experience
   than silence.
2. **The experiment is contaminated.** No clean learning can come from continuing it, so the only
   reason to send would be acquisition, not measurement.
3. **The channel is proven flat.** 105 curiosity-led painter prospects (0 meaningful replies), V2: 52
   prospects, 1 inbound all-time, 1.9% by prospect. The 30 Aug Full Business Review concluded the next
   customer comes from founder calls, not email. Adding 14 emails to a dead channel contradicts that
   diagnosis.
4. **Persistence ≠ repetition permits continuation, but the Customer Context Gate decides it.** The
   honest next action for a prospect who was emailed twice, 34 days ago, with no reply, is not another email.
5. **Nothing is lost.** Records stay searchable at `Campaign Complete`; if a founder call later produces
   interest, the history is intact.

## Option B — Resume with a fresh Email 2

Send Email 2 ("I didn't expect this") now, or re-cut it for a 34-day gap, then continue E3/E4.

**Costs:** breaks the frozen-sequence condition the experiment rests on; a 34-day-late Day+5 follow-up
reads as a mail-merge failure; 14 more sends into a channel with 0 replies from 105+ comparable prospects;
no measurement value (contaminated).

## Recommendation

**Option A.** This is CRM truth, not a strategic pivot: the sequence was abandoned in August, the cohort
was parked pending approval, and the approval question is now simply "close it or revive it". The
evidence supports closing. Confidence: High (90%) — delivery, absence of Email 2, and absence of replies
are all verified from james@ Sent, not inferred from CRM.

**Effort if approved:** one Airtable PATCH pass over 14 records, no sends, no customer contact. Reversible.
