### Discovery Standard
- No "are you still interested?" or closure-seeking language
- No manufactured urgency
- No invented project circumstances
- No price reminders ("$X is a meaningful investment")
- No analytical inference presented as fact to customer
- "clarify or help with" not "clarify or adjust"
- Each email: warm, natural, curious, commercially aware, easy to answer
