# Intelligence Escalation Framework

**Established:** 2026-08-05 (refined from BK-012)
**Source:** James Hardstaff
**Status:** Permanent Operating Standard

---

> "The objective of Not Forgotten is not to report every event. The objective is to help businesses make better decisions."

---

## The Principle

Escalate information based on its operational importance, not its existence. There are three levels.

---

## Level 1 — Immediate Operational Escalation

Immediately notify the client when the customer requires action.

| Trigger | Example |
|---|---|
| Quote accepted | Customer confirms they want to proceed |
| Scheduling request | "When can you start?" |
| Phone call request | "Can Tas call me?" |
| Technical question | "Can you explain the scope?" |
| Pricing clarification | "What's included in this?" |
| Complaint | Any dissatisfaction with service |
| Safety issue | Any safety concern |
| VIP/strategic customer | High-value or long-term customer |
| Legal/reputational risk | Anything that could damage the client |

**Action:** Forward to client immediately with full context. These require operational action.

---

## Level 2 — Significant Business Intelligence

Notify the client promptly (outside the monthly report) when a single event contains strategically important business intelligence.

| Trigger | Example |
|---|---|
| High-value quote lost | Six-figure job goes to competitor |
| Major commercial customer lost | Key account chooses elsewhere |
| Multiple losses to same competitor | Pattern emerging — second or third loss to the same company |
| New objection never seen before | "Your insurance doesn't cover this type of work" |
| Recurring pattern beginning | Same objection from three different customers |
| Anything James has asked to be escalated | Explicit request overrides standard routing |

**Action:** Not an operational emergency, but strategically important. Notify client within the same business day with context and significance. These go outside the monthly report.

---

## Level 3 — Monthly Business Intelligence

Everything else is aggregated into the Monthly Business Intelligence Report.

| Trigger | Example |
|---|---|
| Lost on price | Standard competitive pricing loss |
| Chose another contractor | Standard competitive loss |
| Timing didn't suit | Customer not ready now |
| Committee selected another | Institutional decision process |
| Standard competitive losses | Routine market dynamics |
| General customer feedback | Positive or neutral feedback |

**Action:** Record, categorise, and include in trend reporting. Do not interrupt the client individually. These become trends, insights, and recommendations at month-end.

---

## The Learning Question

Every lost opportunity should answer one question:

**"Did we learn something today that we didn't know yesterday?"**

| Answer | Action |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | Escalate the learning according to its importance (Level 1 or 2) |
| **No** | Record it, categorise it, and include in trend reporting (Level 3) |

---

## Decision Framework

When a customer reply arrives that constitutes a loss:

1. **Classify the loss reason** — price, competitor, timing, scope, other
2. **Ask the learning question** — did we learn something new?
3. **Assess the escalation level** — Level 1 (action needed), Level 2 (strategically important), Level 3 (routine)
4. **Respond to the customer** — graceful close per BK-006
5. **Route the intelligence** — escalate or record per the framework
6. **Verify, evidence, record** — standard production gates

---

## Monthly Business Intelligence Report

Aggregate all Level 3 losses and present:

- Number of lost quotes
- Total value lost
- Primary loss reasons
- Competitor mentions
- Pricing objections
- Timing objections
- Communication issues
- Customer feedback
- Recommended improvements
- Atlas observations
- Any new learnings from Level 2 escalations

Level 2 items that were escalated during the month are also included for completeness, noted as "previously escalated."

## Client Intelligence Preferences

The Intelligence Escalation Framework is configurable for each client. Every client has an Intelligence Preferences profile in Airtable.

### Default Profile

| Level | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | Immediate operational notification |
| Level 2 | Same-day strategic notification |
| Level 3 | Monthly Business Intelligence Report |

### Customisation

Each client may refine their preferences over time. Examples:

- "Notify me of every lost commercial quote."
- "Notify me only when a quote exceeds $20,000."
- "Notify me whenever a new objection appears."
- "Notify me if we lose three jobs to the same competitor."
- "Include everything else in the monthly report."

### The Hard Rule

**Level 1 operational events can never be disabled.** Quote accepted, scheduling requests, complaints, safety issues, VIP contacts, and legal/reputational risks are escalated immediately regardless of client preferences. This is non-negotiable.

### Permanent Principle

Not every business values the same information. The value of Atlas is not deciding what is important. The value of Atlas is understanding what is important to each individual client.

The Intelligence Escalation Framework remains the default operating model. Client preferences may refine — but never weaken — the framework.

Atlas should automatically apply each client's Intelligence Preferences when deciding whether to escalate. If no preferences are set, the default framework applies.
