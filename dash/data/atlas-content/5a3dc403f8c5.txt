# Decision: Quote Insights V1 — Retention Layer

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Status:** Made
**Author:** James Hardstaff
**Category:** Product Strategy

## Decision

Monthly Quote Insights V1 is an official retention tool within the Not Forgotten product strategy. Its purpose is to create ongoing value for clients beyond the core quote follow-up service by showing them what Not Forgotten is learning from their quote pipeline and customer conversations.

## Retention Chain

FOLLOW-UP creates customer conversations → CUSTOMER CONVERSATIONS create structured data → STRUCTURED DATA creates business intelligence → BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE creates ongoing value → ONGOING VALUE supports CLIENT RETENTION.

## Retention Goal

A client should eventually feel: "Even beyond the jobs Not Forgotten recovers, I don't want to lose the visibility and intelligence it gives me about my business."

## Report Structure (V1)

1. The Money — Won, Recovered, Lost, Open
2. Revenue Recovered — specific recovery narratives
3. Old Quotes Cleared Up — dormant backlog visibility
4. Why Work Was Lost — verified reasons only
5. Worth Watching — max 5 curated opportunities
6. What I'd Pay Attention To — max 3 evidence-based actions
7. One Thing We're Learning — single data-grounded insight

## Principles

- Evidence-based, not manufactured
- Simple, not analytics-dashboard
- Commercially useful, not technical
- Easy for a business owner to understand
- Generated from data NF already collects through core follow-up
