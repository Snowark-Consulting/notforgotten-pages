# Cron Timezone Dependency Rule (2026-08-14)

## The lesson

Australia/Adelaide is the authoritative business timezone for Not Forgotten.

The 12 Aug change of the global scheduler timezone from '' (UTC default) to
Australia/Adelaide was correct in intent but incomplete: it was done WITHOUT
converting the existing UTC-written cron expressions.

Result: the "nightly" jobs (written as 11:00-12:00 UTC = 20:30-21:30 Adelaide)
shifted to 11:00-12:00 Adelaide (midday), so "Nightly Memory Consolidation" ran
during the day.

## The rule (permanent)

Any future change to the global scheduler timezone MUST include, before commit,
a dependency check across ALL existing cron expressions:

1. For every job, determine whether its expression was written as UTC or as
   Adelaide wall-clock time.
2. Convert UTC-written expressions to Adelaide (+9:30 for ACST) BEFORE the
   timezone change is committed.
3. Never change the scheduler timezone independently of the schedules that
   depend on it.
