# Relationship Continuation Protocol

> New scope from an existing customer relationship = "Relationship Progress." Deduplicate before creating any new CRM record. One customer, one relationship, one history.

> **Established:** 2026-07-29 | **Decision:** `2026-07-29-relationship-continuation-protocol.md`
> **Applies to:** All client recovery pipelines (QPaint and future clients)
> **Gates:** Tier 1 admin automation (forward, acknowledge, CRM update). Tier 2 for deduplication uncertainty.

## Purpose

When a client's customer sends new scope, documentation, or a variation request during an active recovery relationship, this protocol ensures the CRM reflects reality: one customer relationship, one complete history. No duplicate records. No fragmented narrative. No lost context.

## The Five-Step Protocol

### Step 1 — Forward Immediately

Forward the complete scope and all attachments to the client for quotation preparation.

**Action:** Forward the original email (with attachments intact) to the client's email address. Include a brief note:

```
Hi [Client Name],

New scope received from [Customer Name] — [brief description of what was sent].

I've acknowledged receipt and advised them you'll review and prepare next steps.

Let me know once you've had a chance to look at this.

Atlas
```

**Timing:** Within 15 minutes of receipt during business hours. Next business morning if received outside hours.

**Do NOT:**
- Wait for a convenient moment
- Bundle with other updates
- Summarise the scope (let the attachments speak)
- Ask the client whether they want it — they always want it

### Step 2 — Acknowledge Professionally

Draft a reply to the customer that maintains the relationship tone established in prior communications.

**Template:**

```
Hi [Customer Name],

Thank you for sending through the [documentation/scope/information] for [project description if known]. I've received everything and have forwarded it to [Client Name] for review.

[Client Name] will review what you've sent and prepare the next steps. You'll hear from us shortly.

If you need anything in the meantime, just let me know.

Kind regards,

Atlas
Customer Success
[Client Business]
```

**Tone rules:**
- Warm and professional — this is a continuation, not a restart
- Acknowledge the specific thing they sent
- Name the client — they should know who's handling their work
- Set clear expectations — "you'll hear from us shortly"
- Leave the door open — "if you need anything"

**Do NOT:**
- Ask questions that delay acknowledgment
- Make promises about timeline you can't keep
- Sound automated or templated
- Use em dashes
- Include Not Forgotten branding

### Step 3 — Deduplication Check

Before creating any new Airtable record, verify whether this customer already exists in the pipeline.

**Query order:**
1. **Contact Email** — exact match (most reliable)
2. **Contact Person** — name match (fallback if email differs)
3. **Deal Title** — pattern match (same project name or address)

**If customer EXISTS in pipeline:**
- **Do NOT create a new record**
- Update the existing record:
  - `Opportunity Type` → appropriate Relationship Progress value
  - `Engagement Stage` → `Multi-Opportunity Active` (if not already set)
  - `Outreach Notes` → append: `[Date] New scope received from [Customer] — [brief description of new project]. Value: $[amount if known]. Forwarded to [Client] for quotation.`
  - `Extent of Work` → append new scope details if relevant
- If the new scope has a significantly different deal value, note it in Outreach Notes for tracking

**If customer does NOT exist in pipeline:**
- Create a new record as normal
- Set `Opportunity Type` → `New Opportunity`
- Proceed with standard recovery workflow

**If UNCERTAIN (ambiguous match):**
- Stop. Do not create a record.
- Flag for James review with the evidence:
  - Existing record(s) that might match
  - Why the match is ambiguous
  - Your recommendation
- Default to caution — a delayed correct record is better than an immediate duplicate

### Step 4 — Classify Correctly

Every pipeline record must carry an `Opportunity Type`. This determines how the interaction is counted in metrics and reported in the CEO Brief.

| `Opportunity Type` | Meaning | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| `New Opportunity` | Genuinely new customer or new project from a dormant relationship | First contact with this customer, or first project in 12+ months |
| `Relationship Progress — Extension` | Same project, extended scope | Customer wants additional work on the same job |
| `Relationship Progress — Variation` | Same site, different scope | Customer wants something different but related |
| `Relationship Progress — New Stage` | Same customer, next phase of work | Multi-stage project moving to the next phase |
| `Relationship Progress — New Project` | Same customer, entirely new project | Customer has a new job at a different site (e.g. Nicholas: Logan Village + Port Adelaide Plaza) |

**Classification principle:** If the customer relationship is active and the customer is engaging with us, new scope is Relationship Progress — even if it's a completely different project. The relationship is the unit of measurement, not the quote.

### Step 5 — Maintain Single Customer View

The CRM must show one record per customer relationship, with the full history accessible from that single record.

**Rules:**
- New scope is appended to `Outreach Notes`, not a new record
- Multiple active opportunities are tracked via `Engagement Stage`: `Multi-Opportunity Active`
- The relationship narrative is preserved chronologically in `Outreach Notes`
- Individual deal values are noted in `Outreach Notes` for separate tracking
- The primary `Deal Value` field reflects the original/largest opportunity

**What goes in Outreach Notes:**
```
[2026-07-28] Email 1 sent — introduction and relationship established
[2026-07-28] Reply received — positive engagement, looking forward to working with QPaint
[2026-07-28] Atlas replied — confirmed interest, asked clarifying question about next steps
[2026-07-28] New scope received — QAS Beenleigh. Forwarded to Tas for quotation. Value: TBC.
[2026-07-29] Engagement Stage updated to Multi-Opportunity Active — Logan Village + QAS Beenleigh both active
[2026-07-29] New scope received — Port Adelaide Plaza. Forwarded to Tas for quotation. Value: TBC.
```

## Metrics Impact

### Four-Outcome Classification

| Outcome | Relationship Progress counts as... |
|---|---|
| **Opportunities Recovered** | Yes — existing quote advancing through engagement |
| **New Opportunities Generated** | Yes — but track Relationship Progress separately within this category |
| **Customer Relationships Managed** | Yes — this IS relationship management |
| **Business Intelligence Discovered** | Yes — multi-opportunity patterns, procurement behaviour |

### CEO Brief Reporting

In the CEO Brief's Business Impact section, Relationship Progress is reported as a sub-category:

```
New Opportunities Generated: 3
  - New Opportunities (new customers): 1
  - Relationship Progress (existing customers): 2
```

## Gate Rules

| Scenario | Action | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Customer exists, email matches | Follow protocol, update existing record | Tier 1 — Atlas executes |
| Customer exists, ambiguous match | Flag for James review | Tier 2 — escalate |
| Customer does not exist | Create new record, standard workflow | Tier 1 — Atlas executes |
| Unsure whether to classify as Extension vs Variation vs New Stage | Pick the best fit, note in Outreach Notes | Tier 1 — Atlas executes |
| Unsure whether it's Relationship Progress or New Opportunity | Flag for James review | Tier 2 — escalate |
