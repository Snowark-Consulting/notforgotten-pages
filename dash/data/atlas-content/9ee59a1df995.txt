# Review Prep — Relationship-First CRM Philosophy

> Outcome data gathered ahead of the 2026-08-29 review of `2026-07-29-relationship-first-crm`.

## Decision being reviewed

**What was decided (2026-07-29):** The CRM's organising principle shifts from "opportunity management" to "relationship storytelling." One record per customer relationship, chronological narrative in Outreach Notes, structured-but-human entries, glanceable in 30 seconds, six-month test on every entry. Governing question: "Does this create a clearer picture of the customer's journey?" Test question: "Will this make it easier to understand this customer's history six months from now?"

**Review date:** 2026-08-29

## Verdict framing for review

The philosophy is embedded in operating procedure (CCG, ledger, structured notes) but penetration is partial: 26% of Prospects carry structured notes, and the Nicholas reference example has a duplication question. The review should decide: (1) how to handle the Nicholas duplicate, (2) whether structured notes are required for all Prospects or only active/recovery cohorts, (3) whether V2 Outreach Activity should become the canonical timeline for V2 prospects (leaving Outreach Notes for state only).
