## The Customer Journey

| Stage | Fear It Answers | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reassurance | "Did I choose the right company to quote?" | Your quote from QPaint |
| 2. Decision Support | "What will it actually be like if I choose QPaint?" | A few things worth knowing |
| 3. Confidence | "Can I trust these people in my home?" | A few words from people we've worked with |
| 4. Honest Conversation | "Is it okay if I'm not ready yet?" | Where are you at with the quote? |
| 5+ Outcome Support | "Will they respect whatever decision I make?" | Checking in |

Continue until an outcome is reached: Won. Lost. Deferred. Waiting. Not Proceeding.

---

## Learning Framework

Every customer interaction answers three questions:

1. **What happened?** — Reply, objection, delay, conversion, silence.
2. **Why do we think it happened?** — Pattern recognition across customers.
3. **What should we test next?** — Change Request supported by evidence, not opinion.

Success is measured by outcomes:
- Conversations started
- Quotes progressed
- Jobs won
- Revenue recovered
- Lessons learned

Not by emails sent. Not by open rates. Not by sequences completed.
