## Decision 1 — the weekend-send guard is NOT undocumented. The premise of the open ruling is wrong.

**What is currently on record:** today's daily note and `skills/notforgotten/quote-intake/SKILL.md`
Pitfall 64 both state the no-weekend-send guard "appears in **no** knowledge file, skill, or decision
record", and the open thread asks James to choose between *codify it* and *drop it*.

**What the files actually say (verified by direct read, 18 Sep):**

| File | Line | Text |
|---|---|---|
| `skills/cold-email/SKILL.md` | 618 | `### Weekend Send Prohibition (permanent — 2026-07-12)` — "**Never send outreach on a Saturday or Sunday.** If the standard cadence produces a weekend date, push to the following Monday." |
| `skills/snowark/growth-operations/references/qpaint-prepare-ahead.md` | 63 | "**The Weekend Send Prohibition applies to QPaint Pipeline too.** Before any send window, check the day of week" — Saturday → Mon, Sunday → Mon |
| `skills/snowark/growth-operations/SKILL.md` | 530–532 | Enforcement gap + **interim mitigation**: "If Sunday → push to Monday. Update Airtable's `Next Touchpoint Date` to reflect the push via Airtable API PATCH." |
| `skills/snowark/growth-operations/references/weekend-send-gap-2026-07-12.md` | 13 | "The Weekend Send Prohibition exists as policy in the cold-email skill but has zero automated enforcement" |

The guard is **already permanent documented policy, already extended in writing to the QPaint
Pipeline, with a documented PATCH procedure**. What does not exist is a *decision-library record* — and
what is genuinely missing is *enforcement inside the send path* (that gap is real, documented, and
unchanged since July).

**Why the earlier conclusion went the other way:** a grep of `knowledge/` and `skills/` for
`weekend` / `non-business-day` / `next-business-day` returned nothing. The policy text uses the phrase
**"Weekend Send Prohibition"**, which none of those patterns match. The absence of a hit was read as
absence of the rule. It is not.

**Recommendation — Decision 1:** do not re-decide whether to codify the guard. Instead confirm the
narrower, real gap and close it:
- Treat the Weekend Send Prohibition as settled and already applicable to QPaint (no new decision needed).
- Add an explicit one-line reference to it in `quote-intake/SKILL.md` next to the `Quote Date + 48h`
  rule, so intake stops diverging (this is the actual root cause: the guard is policy, but intake was
  never told it applies).
- Add a **day-of-week check to `canonical-send.py`** so a weekend date fails closed instead of relying
  on a human or a note. Policy without a gate has already failed twice (Jul 12 and Jul 13).
- Record Pitfall 64's "written down nowhere" claim as corrected in the skill itself.
- **Impact: medium.** Removes a recurring divergence class and closes a known enforcement hole.
  **Confidence: High (92%)** — the contradicting text is quoted above and was read directly.
