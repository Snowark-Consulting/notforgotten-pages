## Critical Rules

1. **48-HOUR NO-CONTACT RULE:** Do not send follow-up within 48 hours of verified quote receipt timestamp.
2. **SOURCE-BOUND INTAKE:** Every quote must have identifiable provenance. No evidence = UNKNOWN.
3. **DIRECT RECIPIENT GMAIL VERIFICATION:** Before concluding any customer was NOT contacted, use direct `to:email@domain.com` Gmail search. Bulk `in:sent` pagination can produce false negatives (150-result cap).
4. **CURRENT STATE OVERRIDES SCHEDULE:** A scheduled follow-up must not send if the customer has replied, the stage has advanced, or a stop condition is active.
5. **OUTREACH NOTES APPEND-ONLY:** Read → Preserve → Append timestamped → Write → Read-back.
6. **FAIL-VISIBLE INTAKE:** No silent drop of quotes. Unclassified = surfaced.
7. **DAILY ATTENTION:** 36-hour look-ahead. P1-P4 priority. QPaint action / ATLAS ACTION / JAMES ACTION / MONITOR.
8. **MANDATORY NEXT TOUCHPOINT:** Every active record must have a valid Next Touchpoint Date.
9. **AIRTABLE READ-BACK:** Verify every write with independent read-back.
