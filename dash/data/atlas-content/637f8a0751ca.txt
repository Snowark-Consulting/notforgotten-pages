# Reply Quality Taxonomy — Context vs Curiosity Experiment

> Applied to every reply from both Control and Treatment groups.
> Established: 2026-07-14

## Classification Tiers

| Tier | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **T0** | Bounce / Auto-reply | Hard bounce, OOO, mailbox full. Not a human response. |
| **T1** | Dismissal | Explicit no-interest. "Stop emailing." "Not interested." |
| **T2** | Polite deflection | Friendly but closed. "We're good thanks." No opening. |
| **T3** | Engaged reply | Answers the question substantively. Shows curiosity. May ask a question back. |
| **T4** | Discovery conversation | Multi-exchange thread. Prospect shares specifics. Pain points surface naturally. |
| **T5** | Qualified opportunity | Prospect explicitly wants a solution. Asks about pricing/features. Agrees to a call. |

## Meaningful Threshold

**T3+ = Meaningful conversation.** T0-T2 are noise.

## Primary Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Reply rate | (T1+T2+T3+T4+T5) / Delivered |
| Meaningful reply rate | (T3+T4+T5) / Delivered |
| Positive signal rate | (T3+T4+T5) / All replies |
| Conversation progression | T3 → T4 → T5 rates |

## Application Rule

Every reply receives one tier classification. If a reply is borderline, the lower tier applies (conservative classification). Classification is recorded in the experiment archive at the time of reply and never retroactively changed.
