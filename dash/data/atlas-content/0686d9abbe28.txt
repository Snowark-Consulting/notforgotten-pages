## Why It Might Have Changed

**Prediction check (owed today).** Last week's report predicted at least one `email-signature` session would appear by 27 September, with the decision rule that zero sessions means email never drives a visit and this report moves to quarterly. Zero `email-signature` sessions appeared. **But the fix the prediction depended on was never made.** The Gmail signature still reads bare `http://notforgotten.com.au` with no UTM parameters as at the 12:00 sync today (re-verified). Verdict: **untested, not falsified.** The decision rule cannot be honestly fired, because no tagged link ever went out. This is the third consecutive week this recommendation has been surfaced without action.

**New finding, and it widens the gap.** EXP-001 built both a naming convention (`knowledge/utm-naming-convention.md`, 25 Jul) and a working tagging engine (`apply_utm_tags()`, `send-outreach.py`). That script was moved to `scripts/disabled/` on 7 Aug 2026. The live send path `scripts/canonical-send.py` has zero UTM references, as do `canonical_template.py` and `gmail-send-guard.py`. Across every live script in the vault, `utm_source` appears nowhere. The engine was not left unimplemented: it was retired alongside the script it lived in, and the reports kept saying "Phase 2 pending" so nobody noticed.

- **[Fact]** Instagram traffic is recurring, not spiking. Meta-sourced sessions have appeared every week since about 10 Aug.
- **[Hypothesis, 70%]** The 3 US sessions are data-centre noise, consistent with the documented Ashburn / Boardman / Council Bluffs share. The single Singapore session is new but is one session and warrants no interpretation.
- **[Fact]** The `nfs_internal` exclusion is still absent from the homepage HTML as served (verified today, 0 matches). Our own visits count as direct visitors, so some of the 5 direct sessions are likely James or Josh.
- Insufficient data for causal analysis of week-to-week volume. That has been true for 16 weeks and will remain true while attribution is off.

## Why It Matters

The site is not a discovery channel and is not being asked to be. Its job is to survive the trust check when a prospect looks us up before replying. So the number that matters is not 8. It is that roughly **83% of all traffic across 16 weeks is unattributable direct**, and we still cannot say whether one outreach email has ever produced a visit.

The all-homepage reading matters more than it looks. Nine of nine page views on `/`, three weeks running, means no prospect has reached a service page, a case study, or a proof point. If outreach is sending people here, they are bouncing off the front door or never clicking. Either way the content below the homepage is currently unmeasurable and unjustifiable.
