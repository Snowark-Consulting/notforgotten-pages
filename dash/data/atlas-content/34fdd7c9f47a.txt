# Weekly Loss Consolidation — Process

**Established:** 2026-07-29
**Principle:** Do not notify Tas about individual lost opportunities. Consolidate all losses into a single weekly email at end of working week.

---

## Why

- Respect Tas's time — he's a painter, not an inbox manager
- Individual losses create noise without actionable intelligence
- Patterns emerge when losses are reviewed together
- Feedback is more valuable when aggregated and contextualised

---

## Process

### During the week

For each opportunity marked as Lost:
1. Reply to the customer asking for the reason — genuine, gracious, curious (never defensive)
2. Update Airtable: Stage → "Lost", Recovery Status → "Lost"
3. Log the response (if received) in Outreach Notes
4. Do NOT notify Tas individually

### End of week (Friday afternoon ACST)

Compile a single Weekly Loss Summary email to Tas containing:

```
Subject: Weekly summary — [N] quotes closed this week

Hi Tas,

Here's a quick summary of quotes that didn't proceed this week:

[Table: Name, Project, Value, Reason (if known)]

Key takeaways:
- [Pattern if any — e.g., "2 of 3 were budget-related"]
- [Recommendation if any — e.g., "Worth revisiting the Berwick Gardens quote in 3 months"]

No action needed from you — just keeping you informed.

Kind regards,
Atlas
```

### Example format

| Contact | Project | Value | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Andres Sillet | 8 Bay Drive, Russell Island | $33,008 | Lost — awaiting reason |
| [Name] | [Project] | [Value] | Lost — budget |

---

## Exceptions

Notify Tas immediately (outside weekly cycle) if:
- Customer provides specific feedback about Tas or QPaint's work quality
- Customer mentions a competitor by name with useful intelligence
- Loss is a previous repeat client
- Pattern suggests a systemic issue that needs immediate attention

---

## Relationship to Four Outcomes

This process feeds directly into **Business Intelligence Discovered**. Every loss contains information. The weekly consolidation turns individual data points into patterns.
