# Founder Call Execution Sheet (Final)

Objective: generate real conversations with painting business owners and learn whether the
quote-follow-up problem is painful enough to pay for. Optimise for conversations → pain →
opportunities → customers → MRR. Not opens, not volume.

## First 3 calls

1. **Cameron Davidson Painters & Decorators** — Cameron, 0418 832 500, SA (local)
2. **Upscale Painting & Decorating** — Esmayel Nazari, 0490 094 862, Sydney CBD
3. **Brisbane Professional Painters** — 0411 717 176, Brisbane (ask for owner)

## Core opener

"G'day [Name], James here. I emailed you recently but thought I'd give you a quick call.
I've been speaking with painting businesses about what happens after they send a quote.
What do you normally do once you've sent one?"

(No name / receptionist: "G'day, James here. Is the owner around? I wanted a quick word
about how you follow up your quotes.")

## Discovery flow (stay with the problem, don't interrogate)

Follow what they say. Work toward consequence, not just process:

- Who follows quotes up? (you / office / nobody)
- How consistently?
- How many quotes sent (approx)?
- How many times does each get followed up?
- "And the ones you don't get back to — do you actually know what happened to them?"
- "How many do you reckon end up like that?"
- "Do you ever go back through old quotes?"
- "Do you know why most of the ones you lose actually went elsewhere?"
- "Do you know roughly what your quote conversion rate is?"
- "Is that something you'd actually like better visibility on?"

Do NOT pitch on the first pain signal. Stay one more question to understand the consequence.

## Pitch bridge (permission only)

Only when genuine pain or curiosity is present:
"That's actually exactly why I started Not Forgotten."

## Simple pitch

"I built a system around that problem.

We can start with quotes you've already sent and identify opportunities that have gone
quiet, so we don't have to wait months to see whether there's value there.

Then, for the new quotes you send, we make sure each one gets the right next action so
they don't quietly disappear.

It also means you actually know what's happening across your quotes instead of guessing."

No AI language. No feature dump. No technical explanation unless asked.

## Proof (only when relevant, never forced)

"With one painting business, we recovered 14 jobs worth $180,115 from quotes that had
gone quiet."

Frame as an actual historical result, not a promise. Do NOT claim "most businesses make
the $199 back from one job."

## Pricing (answer directly if asked)

$199/month + $99 setup. Then bring it back to whether the problem is valuable enough to solve.

## Primary CTA

"Why don't we start by looking at some of the quotes you've already got sitting there and
see what we find?"

Move from hypothetical to real customer data as fast as possible.

Secondary (if not ready to share quotes): a short follow-up conversation.
Never end an interested call with "I'll send you some information." Get a defined next action.

## What to record after each call

Attempted / Connected / Decision-maker reached / Current follow-up process / Approx quote
volume / Who follows quotes up / Follow-up consistency / Old quotes revisited (y/n) / Quote
conversion known (y/n) / Loss reasons known (y/n) / Pain confirmed (y/n) / **Pain language
VERBATIM** / Existing solution / Interest / Objection / CTA offered / Next action agreed /
Opportunity / Customer / MRR.

Most important: capture the owner's exact words for the pain.

## Learning loop (after ~10 genuine owner conversations)

Running count: calls attempted / connected / decision-makers / pain confirmed / interested /
next steps / opportunities / customers.

Then analyse: most common follow-up process, most common pain, strongest language, common
objections, % with the problem, % already solving it, what triggered interest, what killed
interest, whether positioning matches market evidence.

Do not optimise copy/positioning/product/pricing until evidence accumulates.
