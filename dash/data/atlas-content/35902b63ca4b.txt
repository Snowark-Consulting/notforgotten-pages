# Decision: Airtable as Operational Source of Truth

---

## Hierarchy of Truth

Each system has one responsibility. No system should attempt to replace another. They continuously reconcile.

| System | Responsibility | Question It Answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Gmail** | Communication Source of Truth | What was actually sent and received? |
| **Send Evidence** | Production Source of Truth | Can we independently prove delivery? |
| **Airtable** | Operational Source of Truth | What is the current state of every customer relationship? |
| **Headquarters** | Executive Source of Truth | What matters right now? |

---

## BK-013 — Reality Always Wins

The purpose of Airtable is not to record activity. The purpose of Airtable is to represent reality.

Every record should answer one question:

*"If James opened this record without opening Gmail, would he understand the complete state of the customer relationship?"*

If the answer is no, the CRM is incomplete.

A CRM that is almost accurate is unreliable. A CRM that is continuously reconciled becomes trusted. Trust is one of Not Forgotten's competitive advantages. Protect it.

---

## Continuous Reconciliation

Every morning:
1. Read all inboxes
2. Classify every communication
3. Respond where appropriate
4. Verify every send
5. Update Airtable
6. Update Headquarters
7. Run a reconciliation audit

Reconciliation is part of the operating day — not a response to a request.

---

## Reality Audit

Before certifying any reconciliation complete:

- Does Gmail agree?
- Does Airtable agree?
- Does Send Evidence agree?
- Does Headquarters agree?

If any answer is "No", the work is not complete.

---

## Permanent Reconciliation Workflow

```
Receive → Analyse → Respond (if required) → Verify → Evidence → Airtable → Headquarters → Reconciliation Check
```

---

## Messages Standard

Every real customer interaction must have a Messages record containing:

- Date & Time
- Direction (Inbound / Outbound)
- Channel (Email / SMS / WhatsApp)
- Sender
- Recipient
- Subject
- Summary
- Gmail Message ID (where applicable)
- Gmail Thread ID
- Verification Status
- Customer
- Deal
- Outcome

Messages are the complete relationship history.

---

## Morning Reconciliation Audit

Every morning, after scanning inboxes, Atlas must perform an Airtable Reconciliation Audit comparing:

- Gmail ↔ Airtable
- Airtable ↔ Messages
- Messages ↔ Send Evidence
- Send Evidence ↔ Headquarters

Any mismatch must be reported immediately.

---

## Multi-Client Architecture

Not Forgotten should think: **One platform, multiple client workspaces.**

Each client owns:
- Pipeline
- Messages
- Business Intelligence
- Customer Timeline
- Intelligence Preferences

Headquarters provides the consolidated executive view across all clients.
