# QPaint Email 3 — Pipeline Truth (not breakup) — 2026-08-15

Founder correction to the proposed Jeremy Ho Email 3. Establishes the Email 3 philosophy
for QPaint quote recovery. Email 3 is NOT yet canonicalised (Jeremy is the calibration
case; Founder will approve before a template is created).

## Philosophy

- **A quiet quote is not a lost quote.** Do not voluntarily close an opportunity because a
  customer has not replied to two emails.
- Email 1 introduced the follow-up. Email 2 asked what the customer thought. **Email 3 asks
  for PIPELINE TRUTH** — it makes it easy for the customer to tell us where things stand.

## Email 3 must NOT

sound desperate · repeat Email 2 · say this is final · say we will stop following up ·
manufacture urgency · assume interest · assume disinterest · pressure a decision · close
the opportunity for the customer.

## The four response states (commercial information, not a funnel close)

| Customer response | Commercial classification |
|---|---|
| STILL CONSIDERING | Engaged opportunity |
| ON HOLD | Deferred pipeline + future timing |
| WENT ELSEWHERE | Lost + pipeline cleanup |
| WANTS TO PROCEED | Opportunity / potential recovery |
| NO RESPONSE | Remains unresolved — do not infer why |

## QPaint recovery ≠ cold prospecting

These customers already made an enquiry, engaged QPaint, and received a quote — an existing
commercial relationship. The follow-up cadence must be built from actual customer evidence,
not conventional cold-email "breakup" patterns. Email 3 is NOT automatically the end of the
journey.

## Refinement — Customer Context Overrides Generic Copy (2026-08-15)

Nuwan Fernando correction. Jeremy and Nuwan are both at Email 3, but their context differs.

**Principle:**
- EMAIL NUMBER determines POSITION in the journey.
- CUSTOMER EVIDENCE determines WHAT WE SAY.
- A canonical template provides structure, but known customer context takes precedence over generic copy.

Email 3 is NOT one universal message. If the customer already gave primary evidence (e.g. Nuwan:
"the committee is still weighing out their options, as they need more quotes to satisfy
legislation"), the next email must ACKNOWLEDGE that context — a context-aware progress check —
not ask a generic pipeline-status question that ignores what they already told us.

**Context-aware Email 3 (Nuwan, approved direction):** acknowledge the committee + additional
quotes, then ask how it's progressing and whether QPaint can help further. No deadline, no
pressure, no re-asking what we already know.
