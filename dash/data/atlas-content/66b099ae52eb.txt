# Tas Moulis — Conversion Research

**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Prospect:** Tas Moulis (tas@qpaint.com.au, 0418 980 566)
**Industry:** Painter (Queensland)
**Campaign:** [[Painter Distribution Validation]] (knowledge/campaigns/pdv-july-2026.md), Jul 2026

## What Happened

Tas received 3 outreach emails over 10 days (Jul 12-22). He opened 11 times total. After Email 3 (Jul 22, Travis Krieg case study), he called James directly and signed up.

## The Email That Triggered The Call

**Email 3 — Sent Jul 22, 2026 (Travis Krieg case study)**

> Hi Tas,
>
> A painting business in Adelaide, Travis Krieg Painting, five crew, mostly residential. He started following up quotes that had gone quiet, the ones where the client said they'd think about it and never came back. Over four months, 14 of those jobs came back. $177,555 in total. Same quotes, same prices, just systematic follow-up instead of hoping.
>
> The thing that struck me was the timeframe. Four months. Most of those jobs were written off as dead after a week or two. They weren't dead, they were just waiting for someone to ask again. Made me wonder what that number looks like for your crew.
>
> Atlas

## What Worked

- **Case study as closer** — The [[Travis Case Study]] (knowledge/case-studies/travis.md) story ($177k, 14 jobs, 4 months) was the final touchpoint that pushed Tas over the line
- **Painter-to-painter relevance** — "five crew, mostly residential" — same business profile, relatable
- **Curiosity hook** — "Made me wonder what that number looks like for your crew" — invites the reader to imagine their own ROI
- **Persistence paid off** — 3 emails over 10 days, 11 opens, called after Email 3

## Key Insight

The Travis case study is the #1 conversion tool in the sequence. It should come AFTER the problem has been established (Emails 1-2), positioned as evidence rather than a cold open. Tas didn't reply to Emails 1-2 but the case study in Email 3 was compelling enough to pick up the phone.
