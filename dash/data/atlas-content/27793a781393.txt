# Decision: 48-Hour Quote Follow-Up Delay

## Background

The follow-up-autopilot was sending Email 1 immediately when a quote arrived in quotes@. James identified this is too fast — customers need time to review the quote before being contacted.

## Decision

Quote follow-ups wait 48 hours from quote receipt before Email 1 is sent. This applies to all QPaint and future client quote intake.

The follow-up-autopilot now checks: `Quote date >= 48 hours ago` before processing.

## Rationale

- Gives customers time to read and process the quote
- Prevents appearing overeager or pushy
- Better customer experience — follow-up feels helpful, not impatient
- Still timely enough to stay top-of-mind

## Implementation

`scripts/follow-up-autopilot.py` updated to filter Quotes where `Quote date` is at least 48 hours old. Quotes less than 48 hours old are skipped (logged, not processed).

## Existing Quotes

Carol Beardsall's follow-up was sent today (within minutes of receipt). This decision applies to all future quotes. Existing in-progress follow-ups continue on their current schedule.
