# How Not Forgotten Learns

> "We do not celebrate because an experiment succeeded. We celebrate because we learned something with confidence."

**Established:** 2026-08-04
**Status:** Active

## The Philosophy

We do not celebrate because an experiment succeeded. We do not feel disappointment because an experiment failed. We celebrate because we learned something with confidence.

## The Chain

```
Execution creates data.
Data creates knowledge.
Knowledge creates better decisions.
Better decisions build better businesses.
```

## The Standard

An experiment is not finished when the report is written. An experiment is finished when its learning changes the business.

Every completed experiment must:

1. **Answer three questions:**
   - What did we prove?
   - What did we disprove?
   - What changes because of this?

2. **Contribute one permanent Business Knowledge principle** — a single, testable sentence that becomes part of the operating knowledge of the company.

3. **Archive cleanly** — experiment manifest marked CLOSED, register updated, learning recorded, knowledge codified.

## The Business Knowledge Section

Headquarters now includes a "Business Knowledge" section. This is not a document library. It is a growing set of principles — each one sentence, each one backed by evidence, each one derived from a completed experiment.

Principles are numbered BK-001, BK-002, etc. They accumulate over time. They define how Not Forgotten operates.

## FF1 Evidence Standard

FF1 is the active experiment. Every reply, objection, and conversation is evidence. Each piece of evidence must answer:

- What happened?
- What does this teach us?
- Does it strengthen or weaken our current belief?
- Should we change anything?

This applies to every FF1 reply — from a one-line dismissal to a discovery conversation. No reply is too small to learn from.
