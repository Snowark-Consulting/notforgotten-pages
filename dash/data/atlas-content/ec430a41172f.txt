# Decision: Production Foundations — Incidents Converted to Production Law

**Slug:** `2026-08-05-production-foundations`
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Status:** Made
**Review Date:** 2026-11-05

---

## The Decision

INC-2026-08-05-002 and INC-2026-08-05-003 are no longer incident notes. They are production law. Three new permanent principles (BK-010, BK-011), the Seven-Question Silent Self-Check, and the Production Confidence Score are now part of Atlas's operating system. Every future production action inherits these lessons without requiring James to remind Atlas.

---

## The Problem

5 August 2026 exposed multiple failures in judgment, verification, and execution:
- 172 emails believed sent, zero delivered (INC-002)
- Customer replies attempted with a token that couldn't send (INC-003)
- Airtable updated before delivery verified
- Protocol violated twice: Evidence → Action, skipping Verification

These were not technical failures. They were philosophical. The system trusted itself.

---

## Options Considered

| # | Option | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| A | Document incidents, update individual skills | Fixes symptoms, not the root cause. Same class of error could recur in a different system. |
| B | Convert incidents into permanent production law | Hard gates. Silent self-check. Confidence scoring. The system refuses to operate when uncertain. (Chosen) |

---

## Why This Path

James directed: "I do not want these remembered as incident notes. I want them converted into permanent operating behaviour." The system must prevent the same class of mistake — trusting its own output without independent verification — from ever recurring. This is not about email delivery. It's about how the system thinks.

---

## Core Principles Applied

- **Evidence before opinion** — Gmail Sent folder, not script output, is the source of truth
- **Security before convenience** — Production halts are inconvenient; 172 undelivered emails are catastrophic
- **Continuous learning** — Incidents became permanent knowledge, not temporary fixes
- **Human accountability** — James owns. Atlas stops and escalates when uncertain.

---

## Risks Identified

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Excessive caution blocks legitimate work | Low | Medium | Confidence Score 90 allows minor uncertainty with caution. James can override at 75. |
| Silent self-check missed under time pressure | Low | High | Check is in SOUL.md Safety Rules — loaded every boot. Production Readiness Check is a hard gate. |

---

## Expected Outcome

Every production action is silently scored for confidence. Uncertainty below 75 stops production automatically. The seven-question self-check runs before every action. No system update occurs before independent delivery verification. The same class of error cannot recur because the system refuses to operate when uncertain.

---

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Zero instances of Airtable being updated before delivery verification
- [ ] Production Readiness Check passes before every communication session
- [ ] Silent self-check embedded in every production action
- [ ] No token scope mismatch escapes pre-send verification
