## Entry format (for new dated records)

## YYYY-MM-DD — short title
**Confidence:** high | medium | low
**Contested:** true | false
**Symptom:** what broke
**Cause:** why
**Fix:** what works (exact commands/parameters)

When a new entry contradicts a prior one: set `Contested: true` on both, add reciprocal `Contradictions:` links. Never silently overwrite. When resolved, mark the superseded entry with `**Superseded by:**`.
