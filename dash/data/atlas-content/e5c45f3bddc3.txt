## The Decision

### Three-Tier Architecture

**Tier 1: Critical Alerts (Immediate)**
Only for genuinely significant events: emerging pattern (3+ same issue), systemic problem, major complaint, high-value relationship at risk, immediate action required.

**Tier 2: Weekly Loss Intelligence Report (COB Friday)**
Totals, loss by category, emerging trends, high-value losses, strategic relationships, recommendations.

**Tier 3: Monthly Client Insight Report (End of Month)**
Per-client: volume metrics, conversion rate, value metrics, loss analysis, customer feedback, month-over-month trends, strategic recommendations.

### Core Principle

Do not notify James or the client separately for every lost opportunity. The purpose of Atlas is to reduce noise, not create it. All lost opportunities are captured, analysed, and presented through structured reporting.

## Expected Outcomes

1. James and clients receive intelligence (patterns), not noise (individual events)
2. Critical Alerts are genuinely critical — high signal, rare
3. Weekly reports surface trends before they become systemic
4. Monthly reports give clients actionable insights about their quoting and pricing
5. Atlas's role shifts from notifier to analyst

## Success Criteria

- Zero individual loss notifications to James unless Critical Alert criteria met
- Weekly Loss Intelligence Report delivered every Friday COB
- Monthly Client Insight Report delivered by end of first week of new month
- At least one actionable recommendation per monthly report
