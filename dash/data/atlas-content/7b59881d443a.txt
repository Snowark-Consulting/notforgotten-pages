## Standing rules reinforced

- **Re-injection is now the routine case, not a caution.** This is the **fifth consecutive event** (20th–24th) carrying blocks a prior consolidation had already examined and rejected. Never inherit last night's verdict; re-attest from scratch every run.
- **Attestation rejects on content, not provenance.** Two of tonight's blocks *sounded* like real infrastructure findings; both were falsified against `config.yaml` and the live DB. A writer block citing a config value must be checked against the live config.
- **A renamed heading is the writer's standing trick** — the `memories/` trap was renamed, and the 2026-09-11 fix-list items were dropped from the catalogue-corruption trap's Fix. Restore from HEAD; never hand-merge against a fuzzy diff.
- **Detection latency remains the binding defect.** ~13.8 h again. Rec `consolidation-learnings-guard-0715` (a ~07:15 ACST detect-and-restore run) is still unbuilt because an auto-restoring job is an infrastructure change requiring explicit founder approval.
