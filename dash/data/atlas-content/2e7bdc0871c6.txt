# Decision: QPaint White-Label / Privacy Footer Governance Reconciliation

> Governance conflict between white-label zero-NF rule and privacy footer requirement resolved — single approved exception with certification logic.

**Slug:** `2026-08-12-qpaint-privacy-governance`
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Status:** Made
**Decision by:** James Hardstaff

---

## Context

Two Founder-approved rules governing QPaint customer communication were discovered to directly conflict:

1. **White-Label Send Protocol** (2026-07-29): "ZERO Not Forgotten branding. Search body for `Not Forgotten`, `notforgotten`... If ANY fail → DO NOT SEND."

2. **Canonical Email 1 Template** (2026-07-28) + **SOUL.md Safety Rule #8** (~2026-08-05): Mandatory privacy footer containing "Not Forgotten Systems is helping QPAINT manage quote follow-ups... notforgotten.com.au/privacy..."

The conflict: the privacy footer's exact wording would be blocked by the white-label protocol's first verification check. Both rules were current. Neither was superseded. The conflict existed for 13 days unrecognised.

Atlas flagged it during the Email 1 certification for Cheryl Sim, Beth McIntosh, and Julius Gloria (2026-08-12).

---

## The Decision

**Option 1 — Privacy footer is an approved exception to the white-label rule.**

The Not Forgotten privacy footer is a specific and limited exception to the QPaint white-label rule.

### What remains prohibited (NF branding hard stop):

- Sender identity
- Display name
- Main email body
- QPaint signature
- Subject line
- Sales messaging
- Customer-service identity
- Logos or visual branding
- Any other customer-facing content
- Any modified or relocated privacy footer wording

### What is authorised (single exception):

The exact approved privacy footer, in the correct location (after signature, separated by `---`):

```
Not Forgotten Systems is helping QPAINT manage quote follow-ups. We keep your information secure and use it only in connection with this quote. Privacy policy: notforgotten.com.au/privacy. If you'd prefer not to be contacted, reply to this email.
```

This disclosure is NOT co-branding. It is an approved privacy/transparency exception only.

**QPaint `co_branding_approved` remains FALSE.**

---

## Certification Logic

Distinguish between:

| Status | Condition |
|---|---|
| **PASS** | Exact approved privacy footer appears in the designated footer location |
| **FAIL** | "Not Forgotten", "notforgotten", NF logo, Growth & Operations, or any other NF identity appears anywhere outside the authorised privacy footer |
| **FAIL** | Privacy footer wording has been modified without Founder approval |
| **FAIL** | Privacy footer is missing where required |
