## Why This Path

Silence alone is not a reason for Not Forgotten to keep contacting a customer or to start managing QPaint's relationship with them. Jesse differs from an engaged contact (e.g. Nishant): he has raised no objection, asked no question, requested no change, and signalled no intent to proceed. Persistence is part of the product; repetition is not. The correct expression of persistence here is to hold the relationship warm and be ready to act on real evidence — not to manufacture a fourth touch because a review date arrived.

## Learnings

A silent previous client who has received a direct status question (Email 3) and not replied is best held in MONITOR — not chased further and not marked Lost.
