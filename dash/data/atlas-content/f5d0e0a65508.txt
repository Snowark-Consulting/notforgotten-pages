## Subject Rule

Subject MUST be `Re: ` + the original Email 1 subject, to maintain the Gmail thread.
For the standard batch (Email 1 subject "Your quote from QPaint"), the Email 2 subject is:

```
Re: Your quote from QPaint
```

## Template

```
Subject: Re: Your quote from QPaint

Hi {customer_first_name},

Just checking back in on the painting quote for {property}.

I'm interested to know what you thought of the quote and whether there's anything you're unsure about or still considering.

If there is, feel free to be completely open with me. It helps us understand what matters to you and whether there's anything we can do to help.

And if you're simply not ready to make a decision yet, that's completely fine too.

Kind regards,

Atlas
Customer Care — QPaint
```

## Rules

1. **Never** modify the template body. Variable substitution is the only allowed change.
2. **Subject** must be `Re: ` + original Email 1 subject to preserve the Gmail thread. Never create a new thread for an existing conversation.
3. **Never** use the customer's full name. First name only.
4. **Always** use "Kind regards" as the sign-off.
5. **Always** use "Atlas / Customer Care — QPaint" as the signature.
6. **Always** append the privacy footer — this is the ONLY authorised Not Forgotten reference in white-label QPaint communications.
7. **Never** mention the quote value.
8. **Never** open with "following up", "touching base", or any marketing phrasing.
