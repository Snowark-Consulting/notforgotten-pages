# Recovery Operations Playbook

**Established:** 2026-07-29
**Purpose:** Standard operating framework for all client recovery operations. Every client onboarded inherits these protections automatically.

---

## 1. Client Identity Protection

**Principle:** White-label integrity is not cosmetic. It is part of the trust clients place in us.

### Identity Profile Requirement

Every client must have an identity profile before any communication is sent to their customers. Profile location: `knowledge/client-profiles/{client-slug}-identity.md`

### Pre-Send Verification Gate (Hard Stop)

Before ANY outbound communication leaves the system for a client's customer, verify:

| # | Check |
|---|---|
| 1 | Correct sending account |
| 2 | Correct display name |
| 3 | Correct email signature (client's template, not NF) |
| 4 | Correct logo (or no logo for white-label) |
| 5 | Correct company branding throughout |
| 6 | Correct reply-to address |
| 7 | No unauthorised NF links |
| 8 | Tone matches client's voice and relationship stage |
| 9 | Zero NF references unless co-branding approved |

**Any failure = hard stop. Email not sent.**

### White-Label Default

All new clients default to white-label mode unless co-branding is explicitly approved. White-label means:
- No Not Forgotten logo
- No Not Forgotten website links
- No Not Forgotten tagline
- Atlas appears as an embedded team member of the client's business
- Signature: "Atlas / Customer Success / {Client Business Name}"

### Co-Branding Exception

If a client explicitly approves co-branding (documented in their identity profile), NF branding may appear. Until then, the default is white-label.

## 3. Escalation Framework

Atlas handles customer relationships until the client genuinely adds value.

Decision tree: `knowledge/qpaint-escalation-framework.md` (QPaint-specific, pattern for all clients)

Key principle: escalate only when the client's expertise is required — technical questions, commercial negotiation, confirmed awards, complaints, new quote preparation.

---

## 4. Loss Consolidation

Individual losses are not escalated to the client. Losses are consolidated into a single weekly email at end of working week.

Full process: `knowledge/weekly-loss-consolidation.md`

---

## 5. Post-Campaign Audit

After every campaign send, randomly sample 20% of sent emails (minimum 2).

Audit each for:
- Branding integrity (logo, company name, signature)
- Sender identity (display name, reply-to)
- Zero unauthorised NF references
- Tone matches client profile

Log results. Report failures to James within 24 hours.

Audit process: `knowledge/post-campaign-audit.md`

## Client Onboarding Checklist

When a new client is onboarded:

- [ ] Client identity profile created
- [ ] White-label or co-branding mode confirmed
- [ ] Signature template approved
- [ ] Recovery sequence designed and approved
- [ ] Escalation framework customised
- [ ] Airtable pipeline configured
- [ ] First calibration batch defined (8-10 contacts)
- [ ] Send method verified (no NF signature leak)
- [ ] Post-campaign audit scheduled
