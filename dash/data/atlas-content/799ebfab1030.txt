**The instrument it set for itself** (its own Success Criteria):
- Every wave produces a completed review within 7–14 days of its final touchpoint.
- At least one system update triggered by the Wave 1 review.
- The next wave's plan reflects the previous wave's evidence, not original assumptions.
- New vs Reset cohort analysis informs whether re-engagement continues.

**Evidence available now:**
- `knowledge/campaign-reviews/` holds **exactly two** reviews: `wave-1-july-2026.md` (17 Jul) and `painter-campaigns-july-2026.md` (26 Jul). **Nothing has been added in the 74 days since 26 Jul.**
- Four campaign-like motions have run since:
  - **CvC** (40 painters, results 6–7 Aug) — closed out by day-checkpoints and the T0–T5 reply taxonomy.
  - **FF1 painter wave** (3 Aug) — closed out in the experiment register.
  - **V2** (Email 3 28 Aug → HOLD 4 Sep) — closed out by decision packs (`customer-2-decision-pack-2026-08-28.md`, `v2-email3-decision-pack-2026-08-28.md`).
  - **CUST2-PILOT** (Sep–Oct) — still running.
- So the *function* the standard exists to perform has been performed each time; the *named artefact* ("Campaign Review") has not been produced since July.

**The question the verdict has to answer:** does "every wave receives a formal Campaign Review" hold as written, does it need re-scoping to "a review **or** an equivalent end-of-wave decision pack", or has it been silently superseded in practice by the experiment/decision-pack layer? Note the standard was written before the decision-pack pattern existed.

**Evidence available now:**

The only replies arriving are on the warm path (QPaint quote-holders, 50–60% on first touch). So criterion 4 cannot be read as met or failed from existing data — it has no data.

**The question the verdict has to answer:** keep the standard as-is (it is doing its job at the process layer), or instrument criterion 4 — a reply-quality classification already exists for CvC as `taxonomy.md` T0–T5, and could be pointed at the warm path where replies actually arrive — and set a first measurement date?
