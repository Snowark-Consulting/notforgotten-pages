# Review-Date Hygiene Defect — Data Hygiene Cannot Make the Commercial Decision (2026-09-24)

**Confidence:** high
**Contested:** false

## Symptom

The 1-Sep control audit's "64 stale monitor dates" hygiene operation (category B: "MONITOR, no auto next touch") cleared stale `Next Touchpoint Date` values on still-live quotes and parked them as MONITOR. This overrode the Daily Attention rule "Email 1 silent, review date elapsed → send Email 2."

## Confirmed victims (verification evidence)

| Customer | Quote | Value | Email 1 sent | Parked | Validity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sharon Fleay | #43091 (6 Avala Crescent, Elimbah) | $2,871.00 | 24-Aug | 1-Sep | valid to 18-Sep |
| Steve Liversidge | #43102 | $6.5k class | 24-Aug | 1-Sep | valid to 19-Sep |

Both were parked after a single Email 1 while still within the quote's validity window. The stale review date was the trigger for the NEXT touch, not a "done" signal.

## Cause

`clear stale review date → MONITOR` was treated as data hygiene. Data hygiene (CRM field cleanup) cannot make a commercial decision. A stale review date on a silent, still-valid quote controls a pending customer decision; clearing it without first determining what it controlled silently converted "REVIEW → DECIDE" into "PARK".

## Fix (standing rule — adopted 2026-09-24)

**A review date may not be cleared as data hygiene until Atlas determines what that review date was controlling.**

1. Reconstruct the record state.
2. Determine why the review existed.
3. Determine whether it represents a pending customer/commercial decision.
4. Make that decision (REVIEW → DECIDE).
5. Execute the appropriate routine action if required (EMAIL / SMS / PHONE / WAIT / MONITOR / HOLD / CLOSE / other evidence-supported action).
6. Only then update/clear the review date.

No automatic Email 2 rule was created either. The correction is "data hygiene cannot make the commercial decision", not "every silent Email 1 gets Email 2".
