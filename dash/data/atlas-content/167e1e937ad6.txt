# Review Prep — 48-Hour Quote Follow-Up Delay

> Outcome data gathered ahead of the 2026-09-03 review of `2026-08-03-quote-follow-up-delay`.

## Decision being reviewed

**What was decided (2026-08-03):** Quote follow-ups wait 48 hours from quote receipt before Email 1 is sent. Applies to all QPaint and future client quote intake. Follow-up-autopilot filters Quotes where `Quote date >= 48 hours ago`. Rationale: gives customers time to read and process the quote; prevents appearing overeager; follow-up feels helpful, not impatient; still timely enough to stay top-of-mind.

## Key finding

**The 48-hour delay is now part of the intake architecture, not just a script filter.** The original implementation was a `follow-up-autopilot.py` date check; the current operating model is stronger: QPaint daily attention uses a 48h observation window before Email 1 REVIEW→DECIDE, and the Customer Context Gate (INC-2026-08-19 post-incident) requires per-customer context before any send. The delay itself has held without incident — no customer has reported feeling neglected because of the wait, and no quote has been observed to go cold in 48h. One open thread: Maria Reid NTD 30 Aug (Email 1 REVIEW→DECIDE) is the next live test of the cadence in the new model.

## Verdict options for review (James decides)

1. **Confirm the standard** — 48h delay validated; keep.
2. **Tune the window** — if evidence later shows quotes going cold or customers expecting faster contact, consider 24h or 72h; no evidence today supports a change.
3. **Formalise into canonical pipeline** — codify the 48h rule as an explicit gate in the canonical send pipeline (currently enforced by workflow/operating model, not code).
