# Client Transition — Standard Operating Procedure

> **Established:** 2026-07-29 | **Governed by:** `2026-07-29-dual-identity-communication-policy.md`
> **Trigger:** A prospect becomes a paying Not Forgotten client
> **Sender identity:** james@notforgotten.com.au (Gmail Signature Rule applies — no sign-off)

## Purpose

When a prospect converts to a paying client, the relationship transitions from Atlas-led to James-led. This SOP ensures every transition is intentional, seamless, and personal — the client should feel the upgrade, not a handoff.

## Trigger Conditions

This SOP activates when ALL of the following are true:
- A record exists in the Airtable `Clients` table
- `Subscription status` is `Active`, `Trial`, or `Paused`
- The first email from james@ has NOT yet been sent

## Step 1 — Welcome Email

Draft and send a professional welcome email from `james@notforgotten.com.au`.

### Template

```
Hi [Client First Name],

I wanted to personally welcome you to Not Forgotten. Really glad to have you on board.

From here on, I'll be your primary point of contact. If you ever need anything — questions about the platform, ideas for getting more out of it, or just a chat about how things are going — just reply to this email and I'll get back to you.

Atlas will still be working behind the scenes — monitoring your quote activity, managing follow-ups, identifying opportunities, and making sure nothing slips through the cracks. But going forward, you'll hear from me directly.

Looking forward to working with you.
```

### Rules

- Send from `james@notforgotten.com.au`
- **No sign-off** — Gmail auto-appends James's signature
- Personalise with the client's first name
- Reference the specific value Atlas provides behind the scenes
- Feel like one business owner writing to another
- Use James's voice — direct, warm, no bullshit
- The coffee test: "Would this sound natural if James said it to a business owner over a coffee?"

## Step 2 — CRM Update

Update the Airtable `Clients` record:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `Onboarding status` | `In progress` |
| `Onboarding notes` | Append: `[Date] Client Relationship Transition — welcome email sent from james@. Atlas continues behind the scenes.` |
| `Last check-in date` | Today's date |

## Step 3 — Timeline Event

Record a "Client Relationship Transition" event. If a Relationship Timeline exists for this client (per the Relationship Timeline Standard), append:

```
[YYYY-MM-DD] Status Change — Client Relationship Transition. Subscription active. Welcome email sent from james@. James now primary contact. Atlas continues behind the scenes (monitoring, follow-ups, opportunity identification).
```

If the client later replies or acknowledges:
```
[YYYY-MM-DD] Reply Received — Client acknowledged welcome email. [Summary of their response].
```

## Step 4 — Ongoing

From this point forward:
- All NF communications to this client come from `james@notforgotten.com.au`
- Atlas drafts, sends, and manages — James is the visible identity
- Gmail Signature Rule applies to every james@ send
- The client's `Clients` record is the source of truth for relationship state

## Verification Checklist

Before considering the transition complete:
- [ ] Welcome email sent from james@
- [ ] No sign-off added to the welcome email
- [ ] Clients record updated (Onboarding status, notes, check-in date)
- [ ] Timeline event recorded
- [ ] Future communications will use james@
