## What the design did not anticipate

**A monitoring state with no way to record itself.** The 21 Aug decision created an observation period. It did not define what a prospect becomes when the observation ends without a reply. The sequence was subsequently held (`2026-09-04-v2-persistence-hold`) and 41 records now sit in a posture that reads as overdue on every scan while nothing is owed. That is a design gap in the 21 Aug decision, not a data error.

Recommended mechanism, unchanged from 11 Sep: clear `Next Follow-up Date` on the held records and append a dated MONITOR note to `Outreach Notes`. Pipeline stage untouched. Reversible, no send, no customer contact, no schema change. Two preps already carry the record IDs: `knowledge/decision-reviews/2026-09-11-v2-crm-posture-closeout-prep.md` and `knowledge/decision-reviews/2026-09-11-deconfused-email2-closeout-prep.md`.
