# Decision: QPaint Intake Completion Gate — Permanent Control (2026-09-02)

**Status:** 🟢 Made
**Founder Approval:** James Hardstaff, 2026-09-02 (directive)
**Builds on:** `2026-08-11-intake-completion-gate.md` (INC-2026-08-11-QUOTE-FALSE-COMPLETION)

## The Invariant (permanent, fail-closed)

> **NO QPaint quote email enters "Quotes Completed" until Airtable
> reconciliation has passed.**

`Quotes Completed` (quotes@) means:

    SOURCE PROCESSED + AIRTABLE RECORD EXISTS + DATA VERIFIED
    + OPERATIONAL STATE ESTABLISHED + RECONCILIATION PASSED.

It does NOT mean "Atlas read this email."

## Why

The 2 September source reconciliation surfaced a critical semantic risk: the
completion label must be PROOF OF RECONCILIATION, not proof of reading.  The
related root cause — the 28-July email-deduplication failure that dropped 15
quotes (~$702,050) — proved that contact email must never be the unique
identity of a quote.

## What changed

1. **`scripts/intake-certification.py`** — hardened to a two-gate contract:
   - `certify_intake_complete(persisted)` — now requires 8 fields, including
     the quote number (in Deal Title), Quote Date, and Extent of Work.
   - `certify_reconciliation(source, persisted)` — NEW. Verifies the read-back
     Airtable record matches source quote number + email + value.
   - Both operate on the READ-BACK record, never the intended write.
   - 12/12 regression + conceptual tests (cases A, B, C, F, F2, F3, H).

2. **`quote-intake` skill v2.3.0** — completion gate requires quote identity,
   read-back of the persisted record, and source↔Airtable reconciliation;
   multi-quote rule and quote-identity-by-number rule codified.

3. **`references/intake-certification-gate.md`** — full gate contract,
   invariant, 8 conceptual test cases, reverse control.

4. **`quotes-pulse-phase2` cron prompt** — POST-PROCESSING replaced with a
   HARD COMPLETION GATE: process → verify → reconcile → complete.  Sequence
   is mandatory; never move-to-completed then process.

5. **`scripts/quotes-completed-reconcile.py`** — reverse-control script.
   Cross-references every "Quotes Completed" email → Airtable by quote number.

6. **Reverse control cron** (`535899707838`, daily 07:00 ACST, no_agent) —
   silent when 100% clean; reports CONTROL FAILURE on any mismatch.

## Quote Identity Rule (permanent)

CONTACT EMAIL IS NEVER THE UNIQUE IDENTITY OF A QPAINT QUOTE.  Quote/deal
identity = the 5-digit quote number.  One contact may have multiple quotes/
properties/projects.  Relationship context connects records; it does not
collapse them.

## Scope note (no overbuild)

No new agent, dashboard, or subsystem was created.  The control is enforced
through the existing intake script + cron prompt + one lightweight no_agent
watchdog.
