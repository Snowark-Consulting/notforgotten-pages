# Lessons Learned Template

> **Use when:** Atlas identifies a significant mistake or system failure.
> **Principle:** The same mistake should never happen twice. Trust is built by recognising mistakes, owning them, fixing them permanently, and becoming better.

---

**Lesson ID:** LL-[YYYYMMDD]-[NNN]

## What Happened

A clear, specific description of the error. What was expected? What actually occurred?

## Why It Happened

The immediate cause. What chain of events led to the mistake?

## Root Cause

The underlying systemic issue. Not the symptom. The cause that, if fixed, prevents this class of error from recurring.

## Business Impact

What was the consequence? Customer impact? Trust impact? Revenue impact? Operational impact?

## Permanent Fix

What changed? Be specific — code, process, policy, or architecture. Link to the commit, decision, or change request.

## Prevention Verification

How do we know this specific class of error cannot happen again? What test, gate, or check now exists as a permanent safeguard?

**Confidence:** high | medium | low

**Date Resolved:** YYYY-MM-DD
