Infrastructure phase closed. Business shifts to **distribution validation and customer acquisition**. ### ⚠️ Criterion 3 — "At least one statistically meaningful A/B test completed by Aug 15"

**Tests ran; none produced a positive result. One produced a clean null.**

- **CvC (Context vs Curiosity)** — 40 painters, 10A/10B, released 14 Jul, reviewed 4 Aug. **0/40 meaningful replies in both arms.** Treatment *underperformed* on opens: **50% vs 70%**. Conclusion on file: curiosity-led outreach retired for painter acquisition.
- **PDV (Painter Distribution Validation)** — 20 painters, 10A/10B, Jul 2026.
- **V2-NEW50** — 50 fresh painters, released 15 Aug, reviewed 4 Sep. **0 replies from 32 at Email 3 by Day 6.** Folded into the V2 HOLD.
- **FF1 (founder-voice)** — 16 painters. **Contaminated**, cannot quantify the delta. Do not cite as a clean test.

The A/B machinery worked. The hypothesis it was testing (that copy variation moves reply rate) was answered **no** — twice, independently.

## 3. What the decision did achieve

It is important the review does not read as a failure of execution. Verified gains:

1. **Velocity.** Every touch in this window ran without a per-send founder gate. QPaint alone: **214 of 309 records** carry at least one touch (Contacted 151 · Engaged 44 · Replied 1 · Won 18). Book **$9,811,709.41**.
2. **A paying client and a live product.** QPaint at $199 MRR with recurring revenue confirmed.
3. **Instrumentation that did not exist in July.** The Airtable ledger is now trustworthy enough to say "0/135 cold replies" with confidence — that is the whole point of the phase.
4. **A control layer built from real failures.** The duplication, phantom-recipient and context-gate incidents produced code-enforced gates, not lessons on paper.
5. **Prospect→reply is now a measurable number, not a hope.** Criterion 3's machinery works.

## 4. What the evidence now forces (framing the question, not the verdict)

The decision's own risk table carried this line:

> *"Increased volume without improved approach burns through the pipeline with no results — Likelihood: High, Impact: High. **Mitigation:** … If zero conversations persist after concentrated testing, Atlas must recommend strategic changes."*

**The mitigation trigger has fired.** Concentrated testing happened across five cohorts, three trades, four angles and 300+ cold touches. Cold email produced **2 conversations, 0 of them from a curiosity or process-led touch**, and the one customer came from the proof-led touch — which is now also flat (V2 Email 3: 32 sent, 0 replies).

The question the review has to answer is therefore **not** "did we send enough?" It is:

> **Was 12 July's premise — that a delegated outreach machine would validate distribution — the right thing to test, or did it validate something narrower: that owner-led small businesses do not have a felt problem until they have a live, unanswered quote in front of them?**

Supporting evidence for the narrower reading, all from inside this window:

- **Warm quote-holders reply at 50–60% on first touch** (3 of 5 on 5 Oct, 1 of 2 on 7 Oct) against **0 for 135** cold.
- **All four recent QPaint wins are `Observed` attribution** — the operation is proving visibility, not causation.
- Two warm replies on 7 Oct declined within 10 hours (*already decided before we wrote*); two are engaged and still comparing.
- The 30 Aug Full Business Review verdict is already on file: *"a working service that cannot yet be sold; 1 paying customer, 0.4% acquisition; the next customer comes from founder calls, not email."*
