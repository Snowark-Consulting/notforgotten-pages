# V2 Persistence ≠ Repetition (2026-08-28)

## Decision

The V2 outreach sequence does NOT stop at two touches. Silence is not a rejection. A third, fourth or
fifth touch is commercially legitimate where the prospect remains valid and there is no stop signal.

The Customer Context Gate (not the touch number) determines whether we continue.

## Principle

**PERSISTENCE ≠ REPETITION.**

- No artificial two-touch limit.
- A legitimate reason for another touch can simply be: *the prospect has not yet responded and remains a
  valid commercial prospect.* Persistence itself is part of sales.
- Persistence must not become repetition: each touch should be materially different, add a new angle, or
  shift channel (email → phone → SMS), not re-send the same message.

## Stop signals (only these halt continuation)

no opt-out · no negative response · no bounce · no evidence the business is unsuitable or closed ·
no conflicting conversation · invalid contact · no commercial reason to continue.

## Working model

Touch 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5, where commercially justified. This does NOT mean every prospect receives five
touches automatically — every touch remains REVIEW → DECIDE.

## Supersedes

The earlier stance of "do not automatically progress silent prospects to Email 3" for the V2 sequence.
Classification is now A (Email 3) / B (founder call) / C (wait) / D (stop), with no arbitrary cap on the
number passing the gate.
