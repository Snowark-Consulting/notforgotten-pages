## 2. What We Learned

### The Travis Case Study Is the Conversion Engine

This is the single most important finding of the entire campaign.

**Both conversions happened at the exact touchpoint where the Travis Krieg case study was introduced.** Not before. Not after. Email 3.

This doesn't mean Email 1-2 are worthless — they may be building latent awareness. But the evidence strongly suggests the case study itself is the trigger. Two conversions from ~60 Email 3 sends: 3.3% conversion rate to paying client.

**Implication:** The Travis case study is not a bridge. It's the destination.

### Email 1 and 2 Are Not Generating Engagement

Across ~125 combined Email 1 and 2 sends:
- 0 meaningful replies
- 1 unsubscribe
- 3 bounces
- Zero trust-building signals (no "not interested but thanks", no "tell me more")

This doesn't prove Email 1-2 are harmful. But it strongly suggests they aren't doing the work we assumed — warming prospects up for the Travis reveal. The data is more consistent with a different model: **prospects who are going to convert do so when they see the Travis numbers, regardless of what came before.**

### The A/B Tests Produced No Differentiation

Both the PDV (Control vs Test) and CvC (Context vs Curiosity) experiments showed zero measurable difference in reply rates. Every variant performed identically: zero replies from Email 1-2.

This is actually useful data. It tells us the variable we're testing (email structure, length, context presence) is not the binding constraint on reply generation. Something more fundamental is at play — likely one or more of:
- Cold email itself as a channel
- Sender trust/recognition
- Subject line not driving opens
- Email landing in spam/promotions

### Owner-Accessibility Correlates with Conversion

Both converters had accessible email patterns:
- Tas Moulis: `tas@qpaint.com.au` (first name @ domain — the strongest owner signal)
- Star Painters: Prior qualification score of 9, meaning they had shown previous interest

No prospect reached via `info@` with no prior contact history converted.
