Was this a conscious consolidation (briefing absorbed into 04:00 readiness) or an accidental pause? The Overnight Founder Readiness prompt explicitly says "Generate the final Founder Brief using the CEO Brief standard" — so consolidation appears intentional, but there is no decision record of it in the Decision Library. **Flag: is there a decision authorising the CEO Brief cron pause?** If not, the review should either formalise the consolidation or re-enable the standalone cron.
