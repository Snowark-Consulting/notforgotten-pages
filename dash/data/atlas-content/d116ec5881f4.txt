# Decision: V2 Email 2 — Post-Send Observation & james@ Reply Monitoring

**Slug:** `2026-08-21-v2-email2-observation-monitoring`
**Date:** 2026-08-21
**Status:** Made
**Review Date:** 2026-09-21

---

## The Decision

V2 Email 2 ("14 jobs, $180,115") was sent to 42 eligible prospects from james@ and independently verified (42 in Gmail Sent, correct sender/copy/signature; 42 Activity records; 42 Prospect records reconciled to "Await reply" / Next Follow-up 2026-08-28).

From this point the 42 prospects enter an **observation period**. Three standing rules govern it:

1. **28 August is REVIEW → DECIDE, never REVIEW → SEND.** Each prospect is assessed individually through the Customer Context Gate. No automatic Email 3.
2. **No batch progression based purely on cadence.** A reply or a review date never triggers a send by itself.
3. **Replies are surfaced as they occur, never held to the review date.** Every reply is interpreted individually on commercial intent.

Additionally: james@ (the mailbox V2 replies and bounces land in) is now monitored in real-time by a new cron job `james-pulse-phase2` (every 5 min, read-only, dedup via seen-ID state file), alongside the existing atlas@ and quotes@ pulses.

---

## The Problem

V2 outreach sends from james@ (Single-Identity, James-Led). Replies and bounces therefore land in james@ — a mailbox that had **no real-time monitoring** (only the daily Overnight Founder Readiness scan). The first manual scan of james@ found 9 unread items that had been sitting unprocessed, including a genuine Email-2 bounce (Doctor Brush Painting Services) and several stale-but-unreconciled bounce/closure notifications. Without a monitor, replies were invisible until the next review date — contradicting "Do not wait until 28 August to surface a reply."

---

## Options Considered

| # | Option | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| A | Wait until 28 Aug to review replies | Simple, but replies (and opt-outs) sit for days. Rejected. |
| B | Real-time james@ pulse + interpret-each-reply | New wake-gate cron mirroring atlas@/quotes@ pulses; reply-by-reply commercial-intent interpretation. **Chosen.** |
| C | Fold james@ into an existing pulse | Risk of mailbox-identity confusion (Pitfall 37); james@ is read-only, distinct handling. Rejected. |

---

## Why This Path

The learning objective of Email 2 is to test whether **persistence plus a different second message creates conversations**. That test is only measurable if replies, bounces, opt-outs, and time-to-reply are captured as they occur. Option B gives prompt surfacing with a hard read-only boundary on james@ (no mark-read, no send), plus dedup so an unread reply is surfaced once rather than every tick.

---

## Core Principles Applied

- Trust before growth (#1) — honour opt-outs immediately; never auto-reply a prospect
- Evidence before opinion (#2) — measure what actually happens next, don't conclude cold outreach works/doesn't work prematurely
- Security before convenience (#3) — james@ is read-only; no label/send mutation from automation
- Privacy by design (#4) — no new data collection; monitoring only reads existing mail
- Human accountability (#6) — every reply surfaced to James for a decision; Atlas never auto-progresses a sequence

---

## Name Data Quality Rule (permanent)

For any prospect whose first name may have been derived **only from their email local-part** (e.g. `harry@` → "Harry"), before any future outbound touch:

- Verify the name from primary evidence (business website, or existing correspondence).
- If independently verified, retain it.
- If not verified, **remove the inferred name** and use the natural no-name greeting ("Hi there,").
- Do **not** infer identity from an email address alone, and do **not** contact the prospect as part of this verification.

---

## Learning Objective — Capture (Touch 1 = insight-led, Touch 2 = proof-led)

For the 42 Touch-2 prospects, capture: replies, meaningful replies, positive interest, objections, calls requested, demos/walkthroughs, opt-outs, bounces, and time from Touch 2 to reply. Do not prematurely conclude cold outreach works or does not work. Wednesday warm calls (Ahmad / Star Painters Adelaide, The Painting Company, Master Choice Painting) remain excluded from automated V2 outreach.

## Learnings

Replies to james@-sent outreach were invisible because monitoring existed for atlas@ and quotes@ but not james@. The fix is a read-only pulse that surfaces, dedupes, and never mutates. This is the same lesson as Pitfall 37 (mailbox identity gate): the mailbox you *send from* is the mailbox you must *watch*.
