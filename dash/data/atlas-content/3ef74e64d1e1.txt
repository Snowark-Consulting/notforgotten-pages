# 30-Day Execution Plan — July 3 to August 2, 2026

> Derived from [[Q2 2026 Quarterly Business Audit]] (notes/audit-2026-06-29.md). Principle: customer proof and revenue signal. No internal system improvements unless they directly serve the three priorities.

See [[Outreach Philosophy]] (knowledge/outreach-philosophy/INDEX.md) for calibration campaign design, [[Decision Library]] (knowledge/decisions/INDEX.md) for decision-point tracking.

## The Three Priorities

1. **Make the product easier to understand** — screenshots, walkthrough, "how it works"
2. **Generate real customer conversations** — calibration campaign with decision points
3. **Secure 2-3 design partners** — diverse industries, reduced cost, proof + feedback

## What We Will Not Do

- New CRM fields, automations, or pipeline stages
- New cron jobs, monitoring, or reporting
- Content marketing, blog posts, or SEO
- Social media strategy
- New prospect research or list building
- Pricing changes or A/B testing
- Brand or design system refinements
- Product features or platform improvements
- Any task described as "making the system more sophisticated"

Exception: bug fixes blocking the three priorities.

## Success Criteria (August 2)

1. Can a prospect see and understand the product without talking to a human?
2. Do we have evidence our outreach channel works, or evidence it doesn't and we're testing another?
3. Do we have at least one new business using the product who isn't Travis?
