# Decision: Travis Proof Figure — $180,115 Canonical ($177,555 was a formula error)

## The Decision

**$180,115** is the canonical Travis Krieg Painting proof figure (14 jobs, 4 months, Jan–Apr 2026).

**$177,555** is retired from all new outbound, website copy, sales material, and conversation. It was a tracking-sheet formula error, not the true total.

## The Evidence (primary)

The reconciliation is documented in `skills/snowark/case-study/references/testimonial-sourcing-and-data-integrity.md` (2026-08-19):

> "the tracking sheet's totals row said $177,555, but the 14 job rows actually summed to $180,115. The difference ($2,560) was exactly the first row, which the total formula had silently excluded."

- Travis's tracking sheet has 14 job rows. Manually re-summing them gives **$180,115**.
- The sheet's own "totals row" (a SUM formula) reported **$177,555** because its range started at the second row, silently excluding the first job ($2,560).

- The website case study's itemised table was built from the buggy formula result (13 jobs = $154,705 + "14th" $22,850 = $177,555), while its headline correctly used $180,115. The table is internally inconsistent and needs correction against the tracking sheet.
