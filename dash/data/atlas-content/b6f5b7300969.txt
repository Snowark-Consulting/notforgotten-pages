## Example Structure

Every example follows the same deconstruction format:

1. **Final email** — the complete email as sent
2. **Industry context** — what matters about this industry for outreach
3. **Business researched** — what we discovered during research (services, scale, context)
4. **Why this opening** — why we chose to start with this specific observation
5. **Why this question** — why this particular question, what gap it surfaces
6. **What problem we believe they may have** — our hypothesis (may be wrong; the reply will tell us)
7. **Core principles applied** — which principles influenced the email
8. **What we deliberately left out** — information we had but chose not to include, and why
9. **Emotional response we hope to create** — what we want them to feel, not just think
10. **Action we hope they take** — the desired outcome
11. **Success definition** — what counts as working vs. not working
12. **Outcome** (when available) — what actually happened, compared to expectation
13. **Learnings** — what this example taught us
