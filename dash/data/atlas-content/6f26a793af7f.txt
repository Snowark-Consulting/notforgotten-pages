# Customer Context Gate — Mandatory Protocol

**Status:** Canonical. Effective immediately.
**Established:** 2026-08-19 (incident: QPaint generic-mail-merge failure).
**Owner:** Atlas / James Hardstaff.
**Applies to:** ALL QPaint (and, by extension, any client) customer communication — email, SMS, call, any outbound touch.

---

## 0. The Decision Model (core operating principle)

The correct decision model is **NOT**:

> DUE + ELIGIBLE + TEMPLATE = SEND

The correct model is:

> CUSTOMER TRUTH
> → CURRENT COMMERCIAL STATE
> → CORRECT NEXT ACTION
> → COMMUNICATION OBJECTIVE
> → APPROPRIATE MESSAGE
> → COMPLIANCE GATES
> → SEND

Automation executes the decision. Customer context determines the decision.

---

## 1. Core Product Principle

**Not Forgotten is not an email sequence tool.**

The value is not "send Email 1 after X days, Email 2 after Y days."

The value is: **understand what is happening with every quote and determine the right next action.**

Sometimes that action is follow-up. Sometimes waiting. Sometimes escalating to the client
(QPaint). Sometimes answering a question. Sometimes founder judgment. Sometimes doing
nothing at all.

---

## 2. The Gate (A–H)

Before ANY customer communication, Atlas must produce an evidence-based customer brief
answering every section. A section that cannot be answered from evidence → **HOLD**.

### A. Customer
Who is the customer? (name, property, any known context)

### B. Quote
What exactly were they quoted for?
- Quote number
- Value
- Date
- Property / project
- Important scope details
- Options / alternatives
- Any unusual technical details

### C. History
What communication has already occurred?
- What has the client (QPaint) said?
- What has Not Forgotten said?
- What has the customer said?
- Has the customer spoken directly with the client?

### D. Commercial state
Is this genuinely one of:
- NEW
- SILENT
- ENGAGED
- WAITING
- QPaint action
- ATLAS ACTION
- FOUNDER JUDGMENT
- LOST
- WON

**Never determine this from CRM stage alone. Determine it from evidence.**

### E. Timing
Why is TODAY the correct time to contact this customer?

"Next Touchpoint Date = today" is **not** sufficient evidence by itself.

### F. Objective
What is the purpose of contacting them? e.g.:
- confirm receipt
- answer a question
- understand decision
- acknowledge response
- respect requested timing
- reopen a genuinely stalled quote
- obtain missing information

### G. Next action
Is customer contact actually the correct next action?

Possible correct next actions include:
- EMAIL
- WAIT
- MONITOR
- QPaint action
- FOUNDER ACTION
- HOLD
- CLOSE WON
- CLOSE LOST

**The system must be capable of deciding NOT to communicate.**

### H. Message
What should we say to THIS customer based on their evidence?

Only after A–G are resolved should a template be selected.

---

## 3. Template Principle

Templates provide: communication objective, tone, structure, brand voice, privacy/footer
requirements, and boundaries.

They are **NOT** necessarily finished customer copy.

A template may be used **verbatim** ONLY when the Customer Context Gate establishes that the
generic wording genuinely fits that customer's circumstances.

Otherwise:
- adapt using verified customer context, OR
- HOLD if adaptation requires commercial judgment.

**Never invent facts to create personalisation.**

---

## 4. Fresh Quotes vs Recovery Quotes

This distinction is mandatory.

A customer who received a quote 2–3 days ago is **NOT** a "recovery" opportunity. They are in
the normal quote journey.

An older quote that has genuinely gone quiet may be a recovery opportunity.

Age alone does not determine this either — customer history and commercial state do.
Understand where the customer actually is in the buying journey.

---

## 5. Customer Experience Test

Final reasoning test BEFORE mechanical send certification:

> "If I were this customer and knew everything I had already discussed with the client, would
> this email feel like it was written by someone who understood my situation?"

- **NO** → HOLD
- **UNCERTAIN** → HOLD
- **YES** → may proceed to the existing technical/compliance gateways.

---

## 6. Decision Appropriateness, Not Personalisation

The 2026-08-19 incident failure was **NOT** that emails were insufficiently personalised.

The failure was that **customer context was not considered before deciding to send.**

We are not trying to make every email highly personalised. We are trying to make every
decision appropriate.

- A simple, slightly generic email can be excellent customer communication when it
  accurately fits the customer's journey.
- A highly personalised email can still be wrong if the timing or next action is wrong.

Do not force technical quote details into emails merely to prove the quote was read.
The objective is **relevance**, not showing off data. A short, simple, relevant email is
often the correct email.

**The standard:** "Would this message make sense to this customer at this point in their
actual journey?"

---

## 7. Commercial Truth & Evidence

- **Primary evidence overrides stale CRM state.** CRM stage is bookkeeping, not truth.
- **No response alone ≠ Lost.** Silence is not commercial truth.
- **Won requires primary award evidence; Lost requires primary loss evidence.**
- **Unknown detail ≠ invented detail.** Never fabricate missing scope/context to look personalised.
- **Unknown detail ≠ permanent hold.** Missing non-essential detail constrains what we SAY, not whether we communicate. A generic but accurate message (referring only to what is known: the quote, its timing, whether still under consideration) remains appropriate when the context gate supports it. Do not make "scope obtained" a prerequisite for future contact.

---

## 8. Cadence vs Judgment

- **Review date ≠ send trigger.** A scheduled review surfaces the customer for review only.
- **Cadence surfaces a review, not a send.** The Customer Context Gate decides what happens next.
- **Follow-up requires a legitimate customer/commercial purpose.** "We are contacting this customer today because ______" must be answerable with a commercial reason — never "Email 2 is due" or "the CRM says so".
