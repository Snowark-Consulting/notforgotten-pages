# Decision: Permanent Communication Standard

**Status:** Made

**Category:** Operations / Data Integrity

---

## Decision

Every production communication must exist in three places:

1. **Gmail** (source of truth — the sent or received email)
2. **Airtable Messages** (relationship history — full audit trail within CRM)
3. **Send Evidence** (production evidence — `knowledge/send-evidence/` JSON files)

If one is missing, the communication history is incomplete.

---

## Won Definition

A quote is considered **Won** when the customer has made a clear commitment to proceed.

Examples:
- "Your quote has been approved."
- "Let's go ahead."
- "Please book us in."
- Purchase Order received.
- Committee approval received.

Scheduling, invoicing, and job commencement are operational activities. They do not determine whether the opportunity was won.

The sale is won when the customer commits. Everything after that is delivery.

---

## Post-Processing Checklist

Whenever Atlas processes a customer conversation, it must automatically ask:

1. Should the Current Stage change?
2. Should the Recovery Status change?
3. Should the Outcome change?
4. Does a Message record need to be created?
5. Does this create Business Intelligence?

These five questions are now part of the permanent post-processing workflow.

### Schema Gap

The Messages table currently links to the Prospects table only. QPaint Pipeline records cannot be linked to Messages. Recommendation: add a linked field from Messages → QPaint Pipeline, or create a unified "Contacts" or "Relationships" table that both Prospects and QPaint Pipeline can reference.

- Post-processing checklist integrated into all customer communication workflows
- "Won" stage added to Prospects table where missing
- Messages schema expanded to support multi-table linking
