# Change Request Template

> **Use when:** Atlas believes a protected asset or production process should change.
> **Never:** Implement the change before approval.

---

**Change ID:** CR-[YYYYMMDD]-[NNN]

## Observation

What happened? What triggered this request?

## Evidence

What data supports this? Include metrics, campaign results, customer feedback, or operational patterns.

## Hypothesis

Why do we believe a change is needed? What's the underlying cause?

## Recommendation

Exactly what should change? Be specific — wording, timing, process, or configuration.

## Expected Outcome

What improvement do we expect? How will we measure success?

## Risk

What could go wrong? What's the worst-case if this change is wrong?
