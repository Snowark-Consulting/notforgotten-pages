- Opener: "G'day [name], James here. I emailed you recently but thought I'd give you a quick call. I've been speaking with painting businesses about what happens after they send a quote. What do you normally do once you've sent one?"
- No name/receptionist: "G'day, James here. Is the owner around? I wanted a quick word about how you follow up your quotes."
- Stay with the problem. Pitch bridge only on a real pain signal: "That's actually exactly why I started Not Forgotten."
- Simple pitch: start with quotes already sent, then right next action on new quotes. $199/mo + $99 setup.
- Proof only when sceptical: "With one painting business, we recovered 14 jobs worth $180,115 from quotes that had gone quiet."
- Primary CTA: "Why don't we start by looking at some of the quotes you've already got sitting there and see what we find?"
