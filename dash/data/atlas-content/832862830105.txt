# Not Forgotten Systems

## Overview
Not Forgotten Systems is a SaaS platform that automates quote follow-up for service businesses. But the product isn't follow-up — it's **certainty**. The confidence that no opportunity is forgotten, no customer is accidentally ignored, and no revenue quietly slips through the cracks because life got busy.

Customers CC or BCC Not Forgotten on their quote emails, and the system handles follow-up by email and SMS automatically until the prospect responds.

## Core Philosophy (from Founder Interview 2026-06-27)

- **We sell certainty, not follow-up.** Follow-up is the mechanism. The outcome is confidence.
- **Help prospects discover their own problem.** Don't convince — ask questions that reveal the gap.
- **The product isn't AI or automation.** It's peace of mind. Headspace is what customers actually buy.
- **System > employee.** Knowledge that lives in the system compounds forever. Knowledge that lives in a person walks out the door.
- **"If James disappeared for a month, would NF still serve customers?"** That's the test of a real business.
- **We ask businesses to trust us with their reputation.** Every email represents someone else's business. That's a privilege.

## Sales Philosophy
1. **Relationship → questions → problem recognition → proof (Travis) → solution.** In that order.
2. **Core diagnostic:** "Once you send a quote, how do you know which ones are still alive?"
3. **Objection handling:** "I'll think about it" = "What's the biggest thing you'll be thinking about?"
4. **Travis case study is a bridge, not an opener.** Deploy after they recognise their gap.
5. **People don't need more information. They need more confidence.**
