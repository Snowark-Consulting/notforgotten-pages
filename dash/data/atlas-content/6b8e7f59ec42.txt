# Decision: Communication Ownership & Identity Standards

**Slug:** `2026-08-05-communication-ownership`
**Date:** 2026-08-05
**Status:** Made
**Review Date:** 2026-11-05

---

## The Decision

Permanent communication ownership and identity standards for all Not Forgotten channels. James owns founder outreach, cold outreach, sales, discovery, relationship building, strategic conversations, and all voice calls. Atlas owns white-label customer care, operational SMS, customer follow-up administration, monitoring, drafting, evidence recording, Airtable, Headquarters, and voice call recommendations. Atlas business number: +61 468 047 212. Email → SMS follow-up standard: Day 0 email, Day 2 SMS (only if no meaningful communication). One customer, one relationship, one communication history across all channels. Every communication experiment tracked independently in Headquarters.

---

## The Problem

Three communication channels (Email, SMS, Voice) were established but ownership boundaries, the Atlas business number, and multi-channel coordination standards were not formally recorded. Without clear ownership, there is risk of channel misuse, inconsistent identity, and fragmented customer experiences.

---

## Options Considered

| # | Option | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| A | Formal ownership matrix + single business number + multi-channel standard | All standards recorded. Clear boundaries. Independent experiment tracking. |
| B | Keep existing implicit boundaries | Risk of drift. No formal record. |
| C | Atlas owns all channels autonomously | Violates human accountability principle. |

---

## Why This Path

Option A. Formal boundaries protect trust. The ownership matrix ensures every communication has a clear owner. The single business number (+61 468 047 212) creates consistent identity across SMS and future WhatsApp. The Email → SMS follow-up standard enables coordinated multi-channel communication without spamming. Independent experiment dashboards keep learning clean.

---

## Core Principles Applied

- **Trust before growth** — Clear boundaries prevent channel misuse
- **Human accountability** — James owns voice. Atlas recommends, never initiates calls.
- **Privacy by design** — SMS consent framework applies to all outbound SMS
- **Evidence before opinion** — Independent experiment dashboards enable clean measurement
- **Continuous learning** — Every experiment tracked separately, learnings isolated

---

## Risks Identified

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| SMS sent without 48h observation check | Low | Medium | Standard encoded in quote-intake and follow-up-autopilot logic |
| Voice boundary eroding over time | Low | High | James-only rule. Any change requires new decision. |
| Experiment data contaminated across cohorts | Low | Medium | Independent dashboards. Separate Airtable tracking. |

---

## Expected Outcome

Clear communication ownership. Consistent customer identity across channels. Coordinated multi-channel journeys. Clean experiment measurement. No channel misuse.
