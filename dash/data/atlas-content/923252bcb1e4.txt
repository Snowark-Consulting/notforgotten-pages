**Who:** Rahamed, owner of Inspire Painting (89 Box St, Doveton VIC). Mobile 0450 724 858.
**Why call:** The only interested reply in the whole V2 campaign. He replied 21 Aug: *"I'm interested and how long waiting for job and inquired leads and you have to give me promise."* He thinks we generate leads/jobs and wants a guarantee. Email clarified + offered a call; he went quiet 9 days. This call clears the confusion email cannot.

**Objective: LEARN + QUALIFY + CLOSE IF WARRANTED.** Not "close Rahamed." Understand him first.

## The 7 things to establish (conversational order)

1. **What he thought we did** — "When you replied, what did you think I was offering?"
2. **How he handles quotes now** — "When you send a quote, what happens after it goes out?"
3. **Do quotes genuinely go quiet** — "Do customers you've quoted go quiet, or do most come back?"
4. **Is it a real problem** — "When they go quiet, what does that actually cost you?"
5. **What he does about it** — "What do you do now to chase those up? Or does it just sit there?"
6. **What stopped him after showing interest** — "You seemed keen then went quiet. What changed for you?"
7. **Does $199/mo make sense, correctly understood** — only AFTER he understands the offer: "So I follow up the quotes you've already sent. If I could get even one or two of those back each month, would $199 be worth it to you?"

## What to say when he asks "so what do you actually do?"

"I don't find you new customers. I follow up the quotes you've already sent out, so the ones that went quiet get another chance. One painting business gave me their list and we recovered $180,115 across 14 jobs. That wasn't new business — it was work they'd already quoted."

## Qualification signals (listen for)

- **Genuine fit:** he sends quotes regularly, they go quiet, and he doesn't systematically chase them.
- **Not a fit:** he only wanted leads/new customers, sends few quotes, or has no follow-up gap.

## Don'ts

- Do NOT promise jobs, a number of jobs, or a timeline. If he pushes for a promise, say: "I can't promise a number, because I don't know your quotes. What I can do is show you the ones you've already got sitting there."
- Do NOT reframe as lead-gen to please him. Keep it truthful: quote follow-up, not lead supply.
- Do NOT make commitments on QPaint or anyone else. This is a Not Forgotten conversation.

## Outcome handling

- **Fit + interested** → ask what a sensible next step looks like (walkthrough of his quote list). No hard sell.
- **Only wanted leads** → thank him, be straight ("that's not what I do"), leave the door open if he ever has quotes going quiet.
- **No answer / voicemail** → one clear, short message: "Rahamed, James here. Quick one on the quote follow-up we emailed about — not leads, just chasing the quotes you've already sent. Give me a call back when you have a minute." Do not repeat daily.
