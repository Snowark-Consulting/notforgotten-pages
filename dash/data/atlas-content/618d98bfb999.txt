## Note on the hourly commit hazard (positive this run)

The 17th event (2026-09-23) taught that the **hourly dashboard job's `git add -A`** had already committed the corruption, destroying the HEAD baseline. **Tonight it did not fire:** HEAD's newest commit (`692202da`, 2026-10-05 10:30:33 UTC) touched only `pages/dashboard.html` (2 insertions). The hourly job is not sweeping the whole tree, so the HEAD baseline survived. Do not assume this holds — keep testing HEAD by content marker.

## Standing rules (unchanged, restated)

- **Never fuzzy-patch `catalogue.md` or `cards/*.md`** — full-file rewrite or `git restore` only.
- **Diff heading LISTS vs HEAD, never counts.**
- **HEAD is not always clean** — verify by content marker, never by commit date.
- **Mtimes are evidence, not proof** — one instant across the whole dirty set = the snapshot autostash; a 1–6 s burst across 1–6 files = a real write.
- **"Re-apply the writer's new blocks" is FALSIFIED as a blanket rule** — attest every block against the filesystem, **from scratch, every run**; never inherit last night's verdict.
- **A dirty card is not damage** — attest before restoring, or the repair destroys a learning.
- **Detection latency is the binding defect**, not repair quality.
