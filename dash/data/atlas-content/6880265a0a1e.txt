# A prose gate in a record is not a gate — the send keyword outranks it

## What happened

Prepare-Ahead scan, 3 Oct 2026 22:00 ACST. Monday 5 Oct carried 22 QPaint Pipeline records
($572,614.99), 13 of them Email 1 candidates. Three of the thirteen carried a **founder condition
written in prose inside `First Action`**:

- **Stuart Wills #43281** — "founder to confirm base-only vs base+option **before Email 1**"
- **Damien Ryan #43249** — "Founder review point before any follow-up … confirm headline vs combined figure"
- **Scott Cain #43280** — intake note: "QPaint commercial matter — surface to founder, no customer contact"

All three also **opened** their `First Action` with the phrase *"Begin follow-up sequence"* — the exact
keyword the 07:30 Daily Attention DECIDE rule fires on ("New stage + 48h observation over + 'Begin
follow-up sequence' → SEND Email 1"). The condition sat at the END of a long free-text paragraph,
behind the trigger keyword.

## Why it matters

A condition that lives in prose is not a control. The rule engine reads state and a keyword; it does
not parse the second sentence of a 600-character field. Failure mode: the record says "do not contact
until the founder rules" and the automation sends anyway — a per-prospect instruction overridden by a
keyword, which is the exact class of error `learnings/cards/agent-architecture.md` warns about
(per-prospect instructions outrank general autonomy delegations, but only if the automation can see
them).

This is the same shape as the documented weekend-guard hole (`qpaint-prepare-ahead.md`: "the NTD alone
is not enough — also rewrite `First Action`") applied to a **content** condition rather than a date.

## Fix applied

Guard text written to the front of `First Action`, plus a dated append-only `Outreach Notes` line:

```
HOLD — FOUNDER CONFIRMATION OUTSTANDING before any Email 1 ... Do NOT begin the follow-up
sequence until the founder rules. This supersedes the 'Begin follow-up sequence' clause that
preceded it. Owner: Atlas.
[Original instruction retained] <the original First Action text verbatim>
```

The original instruction is **retained, not deleted** — auditability beats tidiness, and the guard
explicitly names what it supersedes. Read-back verified on all three records (guard present, notes
grew, prior intake text intact).

## Generalised rules

1. **A condition in `First Action` or `Outreach Notes` prose is not a control until it is at the
   FRONT of `First Action` and negates the trigger keyword.** Prepare-Ahead now sweeps records in the
   window for founder/condition language and rewrites the gate, rather than reporting it.
2. **Check the order, not just the presence.** "Begin follow-up sequence … founder to confirm before
   Email 1" and "HOLD — founder to confirm before Email 1 … [original] Begin follow-up sequence" carry
   the same words and opposite meanings to a rule engine.
3. **Fail closed when a record's own instruction is an unanswered condition.** Cost of a guard = a
   possible one-day delay. Cost of the alternative = a send that contradicts the record's own ruling
   requirement.
4. **Never resolve an option-GST or headline-vs-combined question unilaterally.** The house rule
   (tick-box add-ons never summed into Deal Value, Pitfall 55) is a recommendation to the founder, not
   a ruling that overrides a record's own gate. State the precedent, ask for the line.
