# Experiment: Founder-to-Founder v1.0 — Painter Wave 1

**Experiment ID:** EXP-2026-08-02-FF1
**Status:** Approved — awaiting Monday send

## Experiment Design

### Hypothesis

A founder-to-founder outreach sequence — where James shares patterns observed from industry conversations rather than pitching software — will generate meaningful replies (T3+) from painting business owners, outperforming the July 2026 curiosity-led Email 1 approach (0/65 replies).

### What We're Testing

Not individual email variants. We're testing the entire sequence as a single unit. The voice, the rhythm, the paragraph grouping, the absence of a pitch — the complete founder-to-founder philosophy.

### Baseline

| Metric | July 2026 |
|---|---|
| Email 1 (curiosity-led) replies | 0/65 (0%) |
| Email 2 (process questions) replies | 0/60 (0%) |
| Email 3 (Travis case study) replies | 2/60 (3.3%) |
| Conversions | 2 (Tas Moulis, Star Painters Adelaide) |
| Meaningful reply rate | 3.3% (Email 3 only) |

## Operating Rules

1. **Sequence is locked.** No changes while experiment runs.
2. **Observe, don't interfere.** Let the data speak.
3. **No optimisation mid-campaign.** No "small improvements."
4. **Weekend send prohibition.** Push to Monday if cadence lands on weekend.
5. **All sends via `scripts/send-canonical.py`.** Faithful reproduction only.
6. **Technical Verification on every send.** No exceptions.
7. **Airtable reconciled after every send.** Sequence Status, Current Touchpoint, Next Touchpoint Date.

---

## What Would Prove This Wrong

If 0/16 prospects generate a T3+ reply across the entire 4-touch sequence, the founder-to-founder approach for cold outreach is challenged for the painter vertical. We would not conclude "the voice doesn't work" — we would conclude "this sequence at this length with this cohort did not outperform the July baseline." A second experiment (different vertical, different timing, or adapted sequence) would be warranted before drawing firm conclusions.

## Confidence Level Framework

| Level | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Low** | Sample too small (<10 replies). No clear pattern. Directional only. |
| **Medium** | Clear trend emerging (1-2 meaningful replies). Requires another cohort to confirm. |
| **High** | Sufficient evidence (3+ meaningful replies, consistent pattern). Can recommend changes to canonical sequence. |

Never confuse confidence with enthusiasm. If the evidence is weak, say so. If it's strong, explain why.
