## Key finding

**Phase 2 ("Apply to all outbound links") appears incomplete or reverted.** The UTM naming convention document exists (Phase 1 complete), but no outbound template or send script carries UTM parameters. Since the convention was established Jul 25, outbound sends (RE-ENG Aug 10, V2 Email 1 Aug 14-17, V2 Email 2 Aug 21) have not carried UTM tags. The attribution experiment cannot produce channel data until links are tagged.

This mirrors the provider-failover pattern: decision documented, implementation not verified end-to-end. The convention exists as policy; enforcement (Canon Verification Gate per `2026-08-01-single-source-of-truth`) does not check UTM presence.

## Verdict options for review (James decides)

1. **Extend the Canon Verification Gate** to require UTM parameters on all customer-facing links (code-enforced, like checksum verification). Closes the gap permanently.
2. **Accept UTM as phase-2-incomplete** — mark EXP-001 as "policy only", defer attribution measurement until the next outbound campaign carries tags.
3. **Retire EXP-001** as failed (attribution not measurable without tags) and redesign when the next campaign launches.
