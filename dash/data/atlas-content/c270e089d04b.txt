# Quote Insights V1 — Product Architecture
## Not Forgotten Systems
### Certified: 2026-08-08 | Retention Layer: Active

---

## STRATEGIC PURPOSE

Monthly Quote Insights is a **retention tool** within the Not Forgotten product strategy. Its purpose is not simply reporting. It creates ongoing value for clients beyond the core quote follow-up service by showing them what Not Forgotten is learning from their quote pipeline and customer conversations.

### Retention Chain

```
FOLLOW-UP creates customer conversations
→ CUSTOMER CONVERSATIONS create structured data
→ STRUCTURED DATA creates business intelligence
→ BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE creates ongoing value
→ ONGOING VALUE supports CLIENT RETENTION
```

### Retention Goal

A client should eventually feel: "Even beyond the jobs Not Forgotten recovers, I don't want to lose the visibility and intelligence it gives me about my business."

### The monthly report answers six questions

1. What happened to my quotes?
2. What money was recovered?
3. Why am I losing work?
4. What opportunities should I pay attention to?
5. What is the data teaching me about my business?
6. What should I consider doing next?

---

## CORE PRODUCT

Every quote is tracked and professionally followed up until there is a meaningful next step or verified outcome.

---

## IMMEDIATE VALUE / ROI

Revenue recovery from dormant opportunities where attribution evidence supports it.

---

## RETENTION LAYER

Monthly Quote Insights — a one-page report containing:

1. What happened to your quotes (Won, Lost, Open, Recovered)
2. What was recovered and how
3. Old quotes where follow-up revealed what is happening
4. Why work was lost (verified reasons only)
5. Opportunities worth watching (max 5, curated)
6. What to pay attention to this month (max 3 evidence-based actions)
7. One thing we're learning from the data

## EVIDENCE RULES

### Report generation rules

1. Every number comes from the canonical BI resolver — never from ad-hoc classification.
2. Every outcome is verified by customer reply evidence or Gmail delivery confirmation.
3. Loss reasons come from Primary Objection field — never inferred from silence.
4. Recovered revenue uses Tier 1 attribution only. Assisted and Observed wins do not appear as Recovered.
5. Worth Watching selections use value + engagement signals + timing + context — not dollar value alone.
6. Recommended actions must be: specific, evidence-based, commercially relevant, and something the business owner can actually do.
7. "One thing we're learning" must be grounded in this client's data. No generalisations. No NF sales language.
8. Never manufacture an insight because a section exists. If evidence is insufficient, say so or omit the claim.
9. Never claim "this would have been lost without Not Forgotten."
10. Never claim absolute prevention ("this won't happen again").

### Language rules

- Plain English. Readable by a busy business owner in five minutes.
- No: CVS, attribution tiers, evidence confidence ratings, resolver diagnostics, system architecture, sequence terminology.
- Yes: Money, customers, decisions, outcomes, reasons, actions.

## 90-DAY VALIDATION

### Measures

| # | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verified commercial outcomes surfaced | ≥15 cumulative dormant outcomes |
| 2 | Recovered wins | ≥1 additional recovery |
| 3 | Recovered value | Growth from baseline |
| 4 | Percentage of losses with specific verified reason | ≥60% of new losses |
| 5 | Active-consideration opportunities surfaced | Tracked — no target yet |
| 6 | Communication/process failures identified | 0 new instances |
| 7 | Time from quote to verified decision | Emerging metric — baseline only |
| 8 | Business actions taken because of Quote Insights | ≥1 observed action |

### Measure 8 — Action definition

Record only where evidence exists the client changed or reviewed something because of an insight:

- Reviewed pricing approach
- Changed quoting for specific customer types
- Reviewed a lead source investment
- Changed an internal communication process
- Prioritised a specific opportunity identified in the report
- Changed follow-up handling based on evidence
- Corrected missing business data (e.g. lead sources)

Do not count reading the report as an action. Observe indirectly — do not survey or track.

## PRODUCT BOUNDARIES

### CORE PRODUCT — always

- Professional follow-up on every quote
- Customer reply detection and response
- Outcome surfacing and classification
- Revenue recovery from dormant opportunities

### RETENTION LAYER — must earn its place

- Monthly Quote Insights report

### INTERNAL INTELLIGENCE — supports the product

- CVS
- Attribution model
- Evidence confidence
- Operational leakage classification
- Resolver diagnostics
- Portfolio reconciliation

### DO NOT BUILD

- Full CRM
- Quoting software
- Generic BI dashboard with 20+ charts
- Large analytics suite
- Sales consultancy
- Custom reports requiring manual analysis per client
- Automated business recommendations
- Industry benchmarks before 3+ clients in same vertical
- Client-facing portal or login

### SCALABILITY PRINCIPLE

Quote Insights must ultimately be generated automatically from information Not Forgotten already collects while performing its core follow-up function. James should not need to manually analyse every client's business each month. Human approval may remain during validation, but manual analysis must not become the product.
