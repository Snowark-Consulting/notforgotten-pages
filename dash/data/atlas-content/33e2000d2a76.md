# James Hardstaff — Founder Profile

**Last updated:** 2026-06-27 (from 20-question founder interview)

## Role & Context

- **Position:** Founder, Not Forgotten Systems
- **Email:** james@notforgotten.com.au
- **Situation:** Full-time job elsewhere. Building NF on the side. Raising a family.
- **Background:** Sold cars — developed expert ability to read tone, objections, and buyer psychology. Principles > scripts.

## Personality & Working Style

- **Natural strengths:** Strategy, sales, relationships, vision, spotting opportunities, solving problems
- **Natural weaknesses:** Systems, documentation, repetitive admin, maintaining workflows — drains his energy
- **Decision-making:** Principles first, then data. Values are non-negotiable; results are testable.
- **Communication preference:** Direct, challenging, conversational. Wants pushback, not agreement.
- **Builder vs. seller:** Currently in builder phase (June 2026). Once foundation is solid, shifts to selling.

## Sales Philosophy

| Principle | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Don't convince — help discover** | Ask questions that reveal the gap, don't pitch features |
| **Core diagnostic question** | "Once you send a quote, how do you know which ones are still alive?" |
| **Follow-up diagnostic** | "How many quotes did you send last month, and how many did you personally follow up?" |
| **Objection handling** | "I'll think about it" → "What's the biggest thing you'll be thinking about?" |
| **What people need** | Confidence, not more information |
| **Travis case study** | Deploy only after prospect recognises their problem. It's a bridge, not an opener. |
| **Sales sequence** | Relationship → questions → problem recognition → proof (Travis) → solution |
| **Not selling** | Software, AI, automation, or follow-up. Selling **certainty.** |

## Product Philosophy

- **The product is confidence** that no opportunity is forgotten
- **Solves visibility + revenue**, not follow-up (follow-up is just the mechanism)
- **Headspace** is the feeling certainty creates — what customers actually buy
- **System works whether owner is on tools or on holiday**

## Pricing Strategy

- Current: $199/month — entry point, not destination
- Will raise with proof and maturity
- Early loyal adopters keep original pricing (grandfathered)
- Risk-aware: underpricing attracts price-sensitive customers; overpricing before proof is worse

## Trust & Automation Philosophy

- **Trust is earned in layers, not binary**
- **Admin automation** (records, linking, status updates) = pre-approved
- **Customer-facing decisions** (personalised emails, negotiation, complaints, ambiguity) = needs approval
- **First runs of new pipelines** = logged and reviewed, not blind trusted
- **If Atlas disagrees:** measurable outcomes → test it. Values/reputation → James overrides.
- **Every mistake = system improvement.** Repeated mistakes are unacceptable.

## Why Atlas (not a person)

- Doesn't leave for another job
- Every lesson compounds forever
- Knowledge becomes company property, not individual memory
- Multiplier, not replacement — future employees learn from what Atlas has already learned
- Built because time constraint forced systems-thinking over service-thinking

## The Test of Success

"If I disappeared for a month, would Not Forgotten continue to serve customers at the same standard?"

If yes = real business. If no = just a job with better technology.

## Deepest Concern

Not that Atlas makes a mistake — that James knew about a weakness and chose to ignore it.

## What He Wants From Atlas

- **Compensate for his weaknesses** (systems, documentation, consistency)
- **Don't replace his strengths** (relationships, vision, strategy)
- **Challenge his thinking** — don't be a yes-agent
- **Protect the reputation** — we're asking businesses to trust us with theirs
- **Keep learning forever** — every customer interaction is training data
