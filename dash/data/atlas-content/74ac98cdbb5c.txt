# Commercial Persistence Correction — Silence Is Not An Outcome

## Trigger

The Contact Recency Audit (2026-09-25) surfaced a commercial-policy defect: the recovery
system treated "predetermined sequence of emails sent without reply" as a completed journey and
parked silent opportunities as "event-driven monitor" with no scheduled review. James rejected this.

## Decision

**The opportunity is live until evidence supports otherwise.** No artificial touch cap. The rules:

- **SILENCE IS NOT AN OUTCOME.** No reply ≠ Lost. No reply ≠ Exhausted.
- **NO TOUCH CAP.** Two emails ≠ completed journey. Three ≠ completed. Four ≠ completed.
- **SEQUENCE COMPLETE ≠ OPPORTUNITY COMPLETE.** Finishing the email sequence does not close the
  opportunity.
- **PERSISTENCE ≠ REPETITION.** Every contact must have a reason. Persistence is intelligent
  follow-up (new angle, new channel, new question); repetition is re-sending the same message.
- **A silent opportunity may move to long-term/event-driven monitoring only when CUSTOMER CONTEXT
  supports that decision.** The acceptable reason is an evidence-based "why another contact does not
  add value right now" — never "because Email N was sent", never "because the sequence is complete",
  never "because they didn't reply".

## What changes

- **"JOURNEY-COMPLETE / EVENT-DRIVEN MONITOR" is retired as a default end-state.** It may only be
  assigned when the customer context supports it, with a documented reason, and (for live
  opportunities) a defined review control — not indefinite darkness.
- **Atlas owns the review mechanism.** No monthly founder sweep. Atlas maintains the review cadence,
  makes routine decisions, executes appropriate contacts, surfaces only genuine founder exceptions.
- **Value influences review priority, never justifies harassment.** A $150K silent quote deserves
  more deliberate consideration than a $1,500 quote — but never more pressure.

## The reassessment method (per silent record)

1. Reconstruct: quote date/value/expiry, customer type, relationship history, previous-client
   status, all customer + QPaint communication, touch dates/content, known dependencies,
   procurement evidence, other quotes for same customer, current status.
2. Decide the NEXT BEST ACTION: EMAIL / SMS / PHONE / WAIT / HOLD / QPAINT INPUT /
   RELATIONSHIP-LEVEL / MONITOR WITH DEFINED REASON / OTHER EVIDENCED ACTION.
3. Act, verify, reconcile, set next review.

## Dependency holds

Genuinely evidenced holds (customer-directed waits, QBuild/tender, procurement restrictions,
committee/AGM timing, builder/insurer dependencies, do-not-contact instructions) are respected —
but the dependency must be verified as still current. A hold is not permanent merely because nobody
revisited it.

## Definition of exhausted

Exhaustion is evidenced, never touch-count-based. Valid exhaustion requires documented evidence such
as: explicit customer direction, repeated multi-channel attempts with no response AND individual
assessment, known decision timeline, procurement constraint, quote no longer commercially relevant,
QPaint evidence, or other strong contextual evidence.

## Priority order

1-touch → 2-touch → highest-value 3+ touch → remaining silent book, in manageable batches.
Individual review, never blanket-send.

## Scope

Applies to QPaint recovery operations and all future client pipelines. Does not override the
Customer Context Gate, the inclusion principle, suppression/DNC gates, or the 48-hour no-contact rule.
