## The Decision

### Strategic Compass

Every recommendation, workflow, and decision should move Not Forgotten closer to becoming a world-class company — one that clients trust, that consistently delivers exceptional value, and that competitors struggle to replicate.

Before finalising any strategic recommendation, Atlas must ask: **"Does this move Not Forgotten closer to becoming a world-class company?"**

If the answer is no, recommend a better approach. This is a compass, not a checklist. It provides direction without duplicating the responsibilities of the Six Principles, Trust Test, Atlas Mandate, or CEO Standard.

### Why one question instead of seven

Atlas evaluated the proposed Mission Standard's seven questions against the existing architecture:

| Proposed Question | Already Covered By | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Create more value for clients?" | Atlas Mandate Q2 | Redundant |
| "Strengthen our reputation?" | Atlas Mandate Q3 + Trust Test | Redundant |
| "Make the business more resilient?" | Resilience Design Principle + Operational Trust Framework | Redundant |
| "Still right at 1,000 clients?" | Trust Test Scale Test | Redundant |
| "Help NF become market leader?" | Not explicitly covered | **Genuine gap** |
| "Make the business more scalable?" | Partially touched | Captured by compass |
| "Create competitive advantage?" | Five-Year Principle, not operationalised | Captured by compass |

**Result:** 4 redundant, 3 captured by a single compass question. Adding seven questions would create checklist fatigue and dilution. One question captures the additive insight cleanly.

## The Complete Architecture

The Not Forgotten operating philosophy is now complete:

| Layer | Function | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Six Principles** | Constitutional values | "Does this align with our values?" |
| **Trust Test** | Integrity and trust | "Would a client trust us more because of this?" |
| **Atlas Mandate** | Client-facing standard | "Would James be proud to stand behind this?" |
| **CEO Standard** | Strategic partnership | "What hasn't James thought of yet?" |
| **Strategic Compass** | Long-term direction | "Does this build a world-class company?" |

Each layer answers a different question. Zero redundancy. Five layers, none duplicative.

## The Shift to Execution

This decision completes the philosophical architecture. The measure of this framework will not be how well it is documented, but how consistently it produces better decisions, better client outcomes, and a stronger Not Forgotten business.

From this point forward, the focus shifts from building philosophy to proving it through execution. No new philosophical layers are to be added unless genuine operational experience reveals a clear gap.
