# Star Painters Adelaide — Conversion Research

**Date:** 2026-07-23
**Prospect:** Ahmad Haidari, Star Painters Adelaide (info@starpaintersadelaide.com.au, 0422 196 814)
**Industry:** Painter (South Australia)
**Campaign:** [[Context vs Curiosity Experiment]] (knowledge/experiments/context-vs-curiosity/README.md), Jul 2026

## What Happened

Star Painters received 3 outreach emails over 9 days (Jul 14-22). Ahmad replied to Email 3 on Jul 23 with three specific buying questions — pricing, follow-up method, and volume. Atlas answered same day. He converted to client.

## The Email That Triggered The Reply

**Email 3 — Sent Jul 22, 2026 (Context variant — Travis Krieg case study)**

> Hi Star Painters Adelaide,
>
> A painting business in Adelaide, Travis Krieg Painting, five crew, mostly residential. He started following up quotes that had gone quiet, the ones where the client said they'd think about it and never came back. Over four months, 14 of those jobs came back. $177,555 in total. Same quotes, same prices, just systematic follow-up instead of hoping.

## Ahmad's Reply (T5 — Qualified Opportunity)

1. What's the cost for 100 follow-ups/month?
2. How are follow-ups carried out — phone, text, email, or combination?
3. Does each event include one follow-up or multiple?

## Atlas's Answer

- Pricing: $199/mo + $99 setup
- Multi-channel: email, SMS, and phone
- Outcome-driven sequence: Won/Lost/Deferred (not fixed touch count)
- Discovery: asked how he sends quotes

## What Worked

- **Travis case study was the trigger** — same as Tas Moulis. Both converted after Email 3 ([[Travis Case Study]] (knowledge/case-studies/travis.md))
- **Local relevance** — "A painting business in Adelaide" — same city, same market
- **Specific buying questions** — not "tell me more" but pricing, method, volume. Strong intent signal
- **Fast reply mattered** — Ahmad replied Jul 23, Atlas answered same day
- **Discovery question** — asked how he sends quotes, kept conversation going

## Key Insight

The Travis case study is now 2-for-2 as Email 3 in the sequence. Both painters responded to it — one called directly, one replied with buying questions. The pattern: curiosity opener → problem framing → case study closer.
