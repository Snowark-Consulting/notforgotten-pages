# Decision: CUST2-PILOT — HOLD expansion, continue the 15-business pilot, no automatic Touch 3

**Slug:** `2026-10-09-cust2-pilot-hold-expansion-no-auto-touch3`
**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Made
**Review Date:** 2026-10-16

---

## The Decision

1. **HOLD** expansion of the CUST2 pilot to the remaining 35 businesses.
2. **Do NOT terminate** the existing 15-business pilot solely because Touches 1 and 2 produced zero replies.
3. **Continue** through the approved journey, subject to individual evidence-based send decisions.
4. **Do NOT automatically send Touch 3** (cron `281abe707fb8` paused 2026-10-09 before its 09:00 fire).
5. Before any further progression, review targeting quality, message relevance, deliverability, and available engagement evidence.
6. Preserve the experimental evidence and return a recommendation once sufficient pilot evidence exists.

---

## The Problem

The CUST2 cold-email pilot (15 businesses across HVAC, landscaping, flooring) had sent Touches 1 and 2 (30 touches) with **0 replies**. A stopping rule — pre-commit that 0 replies at Touch 3 means cold acquisition stops — had been asked of James on 7 Oct and was still unanswered as Touch 3's 09:00 Friday deadline approached. The pilot was also flagged for a delivery-target drift (retired `Atlas - Updates` group). James needed to resolve: expand, terminate, or continue — and whether Touch 3 should fire automatically.

---

## Options Considered

| # | Option | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| A | Expand now | Scale to the remaining 35 businesses immediately. High volume, zero evidence of cold-channel efficacy. |
| B | Terminate now | Stop the pilot after 2 touches / 0 replies. Preserves resources but discards the evidence touch (Touch 3 is where both historic cold conversions occurred). |
| C | HOLD expansion + continue pilot, evidence-gated Touch 3 | Keep the 15-business pilot, do not auto-fire Touch 3, review targeting/deliverability first, return a recommendation on evidence. |

---

## Why This Path

Chosen **C**. Touch 3 is the *evidence* touch — both historic cold-email conversions (Tas Moulis, Star Painters Adelaide) arrived at the evidence touch, not Touches 1-2. Terminating now would discard the single most informative data point. Expanding now would scale a channel with zero demonstrated efficacy. HOLD expansion + evidence-gated continuation respects *Evidence before opinion* (Principle 2) and *Trust before growth* (Principle 1): do not scale what is unproven, do not discard what is still mid-experiment.

---

## Core Principles Applied

- **Evidence before opinion** — do not scale or kill on intuition; let the evidence touch resolve it.
- **Trust before growth** — cold outreach is paused/held rather than expanded on zero signal.
- **Continuous learning** — preserve the experimental evidence and return a recommendation.

---

## Expected Outcome

The 15-business pilot remains intact and observable. Touch 3 does not fire automatically. Targeting, message relevance, and deliverability are reviewed before any further progression. A recommendation is returned once sufficient pilot evidence exists (or the experiment is deliberately closed).
