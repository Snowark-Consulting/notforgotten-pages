# Review Prep — Log Retention Standard

> Outcome data gathered ahead of the 2026-09-03 review of `2026-08-03-log-retention-standard`.

## Decision being reviewed

**What was decided (2026-08-03):** Tiered log retention for high-frequency cron outputs — routine logs >48h auto-deleted daily (20:30 ACST), failure logs preserved 90 days, protected records never purged, daily summaries, storage health monitoring every 6h with >85% warning / >92% critical, dry-run mode first. Immediate result at the time: 17,414 files deleted, disk 93% → 89%.

## Key finding

**Standard is working and quiet.** The storage crisis that motivated the decision is fully resolved: 93% → 38.5% and stable, with a ~141-day runway to the 95% threshold. The failure-preservation path was exercised (28 files) and protected paths untouched. The remaining question is whether the 90-day failure retention window is the right length now that disk pressure is low — but there is no evidence of pressure to extend it. Also note: state.db remediation (2026-08-18, 8.9GB → 1.4GB) was a separate storage win outside the log-retention standard; it belongs in the review as context, not credit to this decision.

## Verdict options for review (James decides)

1. **Confirm the standard** — all six requirements holding; no change.
2. **Extend failure retention** — if 90 days is too short for incident archaeology (e.g. Aug incidents still being analysed), extend to 180 days; disk has headroom.
3. **Raise the warning threshold** — with 38.5% baseline and stable trend, consider whether >85% warning / >92% critical remains appropriate or should tighten to catch growth earlier.
