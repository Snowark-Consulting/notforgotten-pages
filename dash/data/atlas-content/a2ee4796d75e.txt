## What the living-system rule demonstrably did

1. **Redistribution over rigid continuation** — calibration prospects were moved into a live experiment
   rather than marched to the end of a sequence that had stopped producing signal.
2. **Persistence ≠ repetition** (`2026-08-28`) — silence was not treated as loss; the Customer Context
   Gate, not the touch number, decided whether a further touch was owed.
3. **Hold under evidence** — on 4 Sep, at 0 replies on Day 6, the correct move was to stop, not to add
   Email 4. That is the living system refusing to run on rails.
4. **CRM truth maintained as the system moved** — 23 Sep: 42 dormant records closed to Campaign
   Complete, 10 B-tier kept as founder-call candidates, nothing marked Lost.

## Open question the review should settle

The living system has been **adaptive** but the channel has been **flat** (1 inbound from 138 outbound
across V2; 0 from the calibration cohort). Adaptation inside a dormant channel cannot produce revenue.
The honest framing for 28 Sep is not "was the rule right" — it was, and it prevented waste — but
"does the living system now need a channel it has not yet been given". That decision belongs to the
founder-call motion (10 B-tier candidates on the call sheet), not to further email optimisation.
