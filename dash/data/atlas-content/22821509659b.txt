# Sequence Review Framework

> Every completed recovery sequence feeds a growing knowledge base. No outcome is wasted. Applied after each QPaint sequence. See [[Atlas Learning Framework]] (knowledge/atlas-learning-framework.md), [[Pipeline Recovery]] (skills/snowark/pipeline-recovery/SKILL.md).

**Established:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** Active — applied after every completed QPaint recovery sequence
**Purpose:** Every completed sequence contributes to a growing knowledge base. No outcome is wasted.

---

## When to Perform

After every sequence that reaches a terminal state:
- Won
- Lost
- Deferred
- No Response (all 5 emails sent, no reply)
- Customer unsubscribed or opted out

---

## The Six Questions

For every completed sequence, answer all six:

### 1. What stage of the sequence generated the first meaningful engagement?

Was it Email 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5? Or was there no engagement at all? Record in `Engagement Stage` field.

### 2. What was the customer's buying stage when they first replied?

Were they still deciding? Ready to proceed? Gathering quotes? Budget-dependent? Record in `Buying Stage at First Reply` field.

### 3. What was the primary reason for the outcome?

Price, timing, scope, competitor, committee, procurement, lost contact, or no reason given? Record in `Primary Objection` field.

### 4. Could we have recognised that earlier?

Were there signals in the quote data (age, value, category, referral source) that predicted this outcome? Does the Recovery Score need adjustment for this pattern?

### 5. Did our sequence help move the customer forward?

Did the emails create forward progress, or did the customer's journey happen independently of them? Would a different approach have been more effective?

### 6. If we ran this sequence again, what would we do differently?

One concrete, specific change. Not "be better." Something like: "For government quotes, add procurement timeline question to Email 1 instead of Email 2."

| Field | Purpose |

| `Primary Objection` | Root cause of outcome |

---

## Monthly Aggregation

All Sequence Reviews feed into the monthly Recovery Intelligence Report. Individual reviews are the evidence. The monthly report is the synthesis.

---

## Principles

- **Evidence before opinion.** Record what happened, not what you think happened.
- **One concrete change per review.** If you can't name a specific improvement, you haven't thought hard enough.
- **Patterns over anecdotes.** One customer not replying to Email 1 is noise. Five customers replying to Email 2 with the same objection is a signal.
- **Honesty over narrative.** If the sequence didn't help, say so. False confidence produces worse sequences.
