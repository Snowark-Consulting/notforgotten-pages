## 2026-08-30 — One-day founder autonomy: 4 operational learnings

**Confidence:** high
**Contested:** false
**Learnings:** (1) Lead-gen confusion is structural — prospects interpret automated outreach as a lead-gen pitch; de-confused first-touch ("I don't generate leads") is a hypothesis to test, not proven doctrine. (2) Prospect research files deplete — of 49 pre-researched candidates only 4 were uncontacted; treat research as a consumable resource, do fresh sourcing. (3) Owner names extractable from on-site JSON-LD schema (founder/jobTitle/owner Person objects) — better than email local-part inference, which is forbidden. (4) Standing per-prospect instructions ALWAYS outrank general autonomy delegations — Rahamed case: "Monitor for reply, do NOT chase" won over persistence under autonomy.
