# Decision: Headquarters Complete — Shift from Building to Living

**Slug:** `2026-08-02-headquarters-complete`
**Status:** Made
**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Review:** 2026-10-02

## Context

Headquarters v2 launched today as an Executive Operating System with five sections: Atlas's Brief, Business Health, Waiting For James, Atlas's Recommendation, and What Changed. James has declared it complete.

## Decision

1. **Headquarters is complete.** No further sections, widgets, or features unless they pass a single gate: "Will this materially improve James's ability to make better decisions?"

2. **Focus shifts to execution.** Better recommendations, insights, and prioritisation — not more features.

3. **Headquarters becomes the primary communication channel.** Routine updates, summaries, recommendations, campaign notifications, learnings, CEO Briefs and operational messages surface inside Headquarters. Telegram becomes exception-only:
   - New client signs
   - Payment fails
   - Critical system failure
   - Major opportunity created that cannot wait
   - Urgent approval required before James next opens HQ

4. **Small refinements are encouraged without approval.** Major redesigns require James's approval with a recommendation that explains why it matters.

5. **Success metric:** Does Headquarters help James build a better company? Not how impressive it looks.

## Atlas's Challenge

None. This is the natural evolution from building to operating. The architecture is sound. The four-question framework holds. The Trust Test gates remain. Now we execute.

## Expected Outcomes

- James opens Headquarters daily as his single source of truth
- Telegram traffic drops to exception-only (target: < 5 messages/week)
- Atlas's daily data refresh becomes the heartbeat of the business
- Recommendations improve in accuracy as Atlas learns patterns

## Atlas's Commitments

1. Daily Headquarters data refresh alongside CEO Brief
2. Atlas's Brief and Recommendation regenerate with fresh analysis
3. Small refinements proposed proactively, major changes only when genuinely warranted
4. Telegram discipline: only exception-level notifications
5. One source of truth: if it matters, it's in Headquarters
