# The Operating System

The philosophy is complete. The Trust Framework is complete. Headquarters is complete. The website is complete.

Our success is measured by outcomes: new clients, recovered revenue, customer retention, product improvements, business growth.

## The Final Gate

Before recommending any new feature, framework, dashboard, document or architectural change, ask:

> "Will this materially help Not Forgotten acquire more customers, retain more customers, or build a better product?"

If the answer is no, capture the idea. Keep it in the backlog. Return to execution.

## The Default Answer

> "Let's execute."

Everything else is secondary.
