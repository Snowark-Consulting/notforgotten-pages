# Learning: Missing Data ≠ Automatic Hold

**Date:** 2026-08-25
**Source:** Founder directive — Tom Clark (TKC Projects) and Russ Dee classification decisions.
**Status:** Active learning. Standing rule, no schema/infra change authorised.

## The core insight

**A customer should only be HOLDed when the missing information is material to making a safe or commercially appropriate contact decision.**

Missing data constrains what we SAY. It does not automatically determine whether we COMMUNICATE.

The test, applied to every gap:

> Would knowing this missing fact change what we say, or change whether it is safe / commercially appropriate to say anything at all?

- If **yes** → HOLD, resolve the gap first.
- If **no** → proceed with what is source-verified, and keep the unknown OUT of the message.

## The two worked examples (this session)

| Customer | Missing data | Was it material? | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tom Clark** (#43072, Isedale Street) | Relationship context — was he a homeowner or a builder/B2B? | **Yes** — changes the entire framing (project vs property, builder vs homeowner language). | Evidence resolved it (Director/Founder/Principal of TKC Projects, architectural building business, business email, building/renovation scope). → Reclassified Residential → Commercial. → Builder-specific Email 1. |
| **Russ Dee** (#43047, Ellendale Circuit) | Surname ambiguous (source "Russ Dee" vs email local-part "dunster"). | **No** — first name is source-verified (PandaDoc recipient field) and the surname is unnecessary for the greeting/communication. | Follow-up proceeds safely with "Hi Russ". Surname mismatch noted internally, source identity preserved, no surname used. |

The contrast is the lesson: **Tom** needed the gap resolved because the answer changed the message. **Russ** did not, because the answer would not change the message.

## The rule

1. **UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN.** Do not invent the missing fact to create false personalisation.
2. **UNKNOWN must not unnecessarily paralyse the customer journey.** A gap that does not change the message or the safety/appropriateness of contact is not a reason to HOLD.
3. **Materiality is the gate.** Hold when the missing fact is material to a safe or commercially appropriate decision; otherwise proceed on source-verified truth.
4. **Primary evidence overrides inference.** When the gap IS material, resolve it from primary source (quote document, verified identity), never from email domain alone.
