# Next-Action Principle — CRM Stage Is Not Commercial Truth

**Established:** 2026-08-17, from the QPaint Founder Attention Audit.

## The principle

Primary customer evidence overrides stale CRM state. The CRM stage (Email 1/2/3, Contacted,
Engaged) is a bookkeeping signal, not commercial truth. The question is never "what stage is
this record in?" — it is "what is the right next action for this customer?"

**Not Forgotten optimises for the right next action, not the next follow-up.**

## The eight commercial states

Every QPaint record resolves to exactly one of these, determined by evidence, not by the CRM field:

| State | Meaning | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| SILENT | No reply, sequence running | Continue the appropriate evidence-led sequence |
| ENGAGED | Replied, actively considering | Respond to the reply; assess the actual blocker |
| WAITING | Customer (or third party) needs time | Deliberately do nothing until the review date |
| QPAINT ACTION REQUIRED | QPaint must provide something | Escalate to QPaint; do not re-contact customer |
| ATLAS ACTION REQUIRED | Next step is a follow-up/acknowledgement | Atlas handles within the sequence |
| FOUNDER/COMMERCIAL JUDGMENT | Commercial judgment call (pricing, scope, negotiation, committee) | Escalate to James/Tas; do not auto-email |
| LOST | Genuine loss evidence (customer said so) | STOP — graceful close already sent |
| WON | Genuine award evidence | STOP — operational handover complete |

## Rules

- **No response alone never equals Lost.** Lost requires an explicit customer reply ("went
  elsewhere", "not proceeding", "no longer needed").
- **Won requires award evidence**, never inference (e.g. "works confirmed", signed contract).
- **High quote value is not evidence of buying intent.** Value, recovery score, and silence
  are prioritisation signals, not reasons to escalate to a phone call. Escalate only when
  customer evidence (a reply, an objection, a committee, a third-party wait) justifies it.
- **A customer who has explained a delay is WAITING, not SILENT.** Re-contacting them adds no
  value and reads as noise. Set a review date and wait.

## Examples from the 2026-08-17 audit

- Nuwan Fernando ($167k) → committee procurement → **WAIT**
- Jason Manley ($46k) → "heritage job, office review" → **WAIT**
- Nicholas Zappacosta / QAS Beenleigh → QPaint managing, paperwork pending → **HOLD (QPaint)**
- Elisha Paige ($113k) → committee + "cost is a lot" → **FOUNDER/COMMERCIAL JUDGMENT**
- Silent normal quote → continue sequence
- Lost with reply evidence → STOP
- Won with award evidence → STOP

## Constraint

This is a principle, not a schema change. Do NOT create new Airtable statuses, redesign the
QPaint journey, or build new automation from this learning. First collect evidence from the
existing journey. The principle governs how Atlas interprets existing CRM state at each review
point.
