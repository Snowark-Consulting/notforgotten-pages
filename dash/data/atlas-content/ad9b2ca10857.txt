# Decision: Campaign Review Operating Standard

> Every outreach wave receives a formal Campaign Review. Permanent operating standard. Every recommendation backed by evidence.

## Problem

Outreach campaigns produce data — replies, objections, patterns, dead ends. Without a formal review process, that data is lost. Each wave would start from scratch, repeating the same mistakes. The Calibration Outreach Living System decision (2026-06-28) established that outreach is a learning system, but didn't define the formal mechanism for extracting and persisting learnings after each wave.

## Decision

After every outreach wave, Atlas conducts a formal Campaign Review using the template at `knowledge/campaign-review-template.md`. The review must answer:

1. **What happened?** — delivery, reply rates, cohort comparison, industry/subject/angle performance, pipeline movement
2. **What did we learn?** — patterns, objections, unexpected results, confidence shifts
3. **What should change?** — messaging, subject lines, personalisation, ICP, follow-up, timing, operations
4. **What should remain exactly the same?** — evidence-supported elements we keep
5. **Confidence assessment** — which conclusions are strong vs early signals
6. **What to test next** — isolated experiments for the next wave

After James approves the review, Atlas updates: the Learning Framework, Decision Library (where applicable), Outreach Philosophy, cold email skill, growth operations skill, Sales Brain, and ICP documentation.

## Why

- **Learning compounds.** Without a formal review, Wave 2 makes the same mistakes as Wave 1. With a review, every wave permanently improves the next.
- **Evidence over intuition.** The review forces every recommendation to cite specific campaign data. "I think" becomes "the data shows."
- **Cohort analysis.** The New vs Reset split (established for Wave 1) must persist through reporting. Without it, we can't answer: is re-engagement worthwhile?
- **System updates are the review's purpose.** A review that doesn't permanently change the system (skills, philosophy, ICP) is a wasted review. Section 7 is the most important section.
- **The Outreach Philosophy is a living document.** It should evolve with campaign evidence, not remain static.

## Expected Outcomes

- Every wave produces a completed review within 7-14 days of final touchpoint
- Wave 2 materially outperforms Wave 1 on at least one metric (reply rate, positive rate, conversion) because of evidence-driven changes
- The Outreach Philosophy reflects evidence from multiple waves, not just first-principles thinking
- The Sales Brain contains real objection patterns, not hypothetical ones
- The Decision Library captures strategic shifts driven by campaign evidence

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Campaign Review template filled completely for Wave 1 (by mid-August 2026)
- [ ] At least one system update (skill, philosophy, or decision) triggered by Wave 1 review
- [ ] Wave 2 plan reflects Wave 1 evidence (not just original assumptions)
- [ ] New vs Reset cohort analysis informs whether re-engagement continues

## Atlas's Challenge (Pre-Decision)

No challenge. This is a natural operational evolution of the Calibration Outreach Living System decision. The only risk is over-engineering — building an elaborate review framework before we have data. Mitigated by: the template is comprehensive but the review itself starts small. Section 1 (What Happened) is the core; everything else scales with data volume.

## Notes

- The Campaign Pulse cron job (every 48h) handles mid-wave monitoring. Campaign Review is the definitive end-of-wave assessment.
- For Wave 1: the first full review should occur after the 4-email sequence completes (~mid-August) or when reply data reaches statistical significance, whichever comes first.
- The review template itself should be reviewed after 2-3 waves — does it capture the right things? Are there sections we never fill?
