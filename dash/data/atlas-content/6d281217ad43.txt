# Decision: Star Painters / Ahmad — Never Became a Paying Client

**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Type:** Classification correction (Founder clarification)
**Status:** Applied

## Context

Ahmad Haidari (Star Painters Adelaide) was classified `Won` / `Became client` in the
Prospects table (V2 Status = `CLIENT`), and the campaign-intelligence metrics counted
`conversions = 2` (Tas Moulis + Star Painters).

## Founder clarification

Ahmad **never became a paying Not Forgotten client**. He showed interest, agreed to a
trial, but never converted to a paid subscription. No paid subscription was established.

## Correction applied

- **Prospects** (`recT4Tr0RV84rN6wu`): Pipeline stage `Won` → `Outreach Active`; V2 Status
  `CLIENT` → `ACTIVE`; cleared `Won date` and `Became client` link; `Days to convert`
  auto-cleared (computed field). Trial history preserved in Outreach Notes.
- **Clients** (`recvCrAQuaMnDmCex`): kept `Subscription status = Trial` (already correct —
  no MRR, no Plan). Record preserved, not deleted.
- **campaign-intel-monitor.py** + **campaign-intel-state.json**: `conversions` 2 → 1.
- **campaign-intelligence-report-july-2026.md**: Conversions 2 → 1; Star Painters note
  changed from "Now paying client" to "Entered trial — never became a paying client".
- **founder-reengagement-approval-pack.md**: Star Painters "Now a paying client" → "Trial only".

## Resulting commercial truth

- Paying NF clients: **1** (QPAINT, $199 MRR)
- NF MRR: **$199**
- Active trials: **1** (Star Painters Adelaide)
- Ahmad is now an **active commercial opportunity / trial** (trial → paid conversion).

## Lesson

"Won" / "Became client" classification requires evidence of an **established paid
subscription**, not merely agreement to trial. A trial agreement is an active opportunity,
not a conversion. Do not count trials in conversions, paying clients, or MRR.
