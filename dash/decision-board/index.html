What v2 Teaches Us

1. "We don't know" is never a reason to hand work back to the client. In v1, four customers were classified as "QPAINT INPUT REQUIRED" — we wanted relationship context, builder connections, or corporate/residential clarification. None of those things are required to send a check-in email or a closing email. Not Forgotten can execute the next action without the client. If we need information, we ask the customer — not the client.

2. Previous client status doesn't change who owns the journey. Jesse Zhao and Aaron Whitlock hired QPaint before. That's context we use in our communication — not a reason to hand the communication back to Tas. "Atlas here working alongside Tas" acknowledges the relationship while keeping ownership with Not Forgotten.

3. The distinction between ACT NOW and WAIT/MONITOR is about the purpose of the next communication, not the channel. Aaron Whitlock moved from ACT NOW to WAIT because at 81 days, the purpose of the next contact is closing the loop — not actively pursuing the opportunity. Helen Walsh and Jayantha Manel are in the same position. A closing email is still a Not Forgotten action — we just need to be clear about what we're trying to achieve.

4. CLIENT HANDOVER REQUIRED being empty is the correct state. If we had customers in this category, it would mean customers were asking for things we couldn't provide. The fact that it's empty means Not Forgotten is absorbing the workload as designed. When a customer DOES ask to speak with QPaint or needs a revised quote, that customer will move here — and that's exactly when the client should be involved.

5. The v1 recommendation to have Tas call Jesse and Aaron was a reflex, not a strategy. "Previous client → call from Tas" felt intuitively right because the relationship existed. But the question isn't "what feels right?" — it's "what does the customer actually need, and can Not Forgotten provide it?" The answer: the customer needs a genuine question about where they stand. Not Forgotten can ask that question. Tas doesn't need to be involved.
