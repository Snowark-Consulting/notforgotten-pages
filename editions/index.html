Follow-ups

Messages that sound human

Every follow-up is written to sound like a tradie, not a marketing department. Short, direct, and effective.

1

The gentle nudge

Sent 3 days after the quote. A simple check-in that keeps the conversation alive without being pushy.

2

The value reminder

Sent 7 days in. Highlights what's included, answers common questions, and reinforces why you're the right choice.

3

The final check

Sent at 14 days. A respectful last touch that gives them an easy way to say yes, no, or not yet.

✨

You stay in control

If they reply, the sequence stops. If they say no, we stop. You can pause, skip, or customise any message before it sends.
